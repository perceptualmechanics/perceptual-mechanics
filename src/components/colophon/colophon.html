Colophon

perceptual mechanics

A personal site by Scott Jason Cohen — ten small experiences, each its own self-contained piece of code, none of it built from image assets: everything on screen is drawn live, in the browser, from geometry and canvas-generated texture. Built with Three.js and vanilla JavaScript.

If you are so inclined, please support this and other work of mine on Patreon.

The mark

The leaping hare used as this site's colophon — carrying the Moon, Venus, Mercury, the Sun, and the stars cut straight through its body — is drawn by Abby Williams. It's also a found line of Scott's own, the reason a hare is the mark at all: "A Golden Hare ran across the sky, carrying the sun in its belly, where it would be safe. The hare ran up the ladder to the sky."
