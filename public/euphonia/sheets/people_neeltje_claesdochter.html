Neeltje Claesdochter

Human · Guide · Druid 4 (Land) · level 4
PeopleEuropeLeidenCircle of the LandLevel 4

Dutch towns licensed ice-masters to judge when the canals would bear, and in a hard winter the whole town was on the ice: skaters, sledges, fairs and the koek-en-zopie stalls that sold hot drink and cake. In Euphonia the license is for a hedge charm as well as an eye, and the guild keeps the list.

Dawn on the Rhine at Leiden, the ice still grey. A small, broad woman in three skirts walks ahead of the first skaters, tapping with a pole, drilling a hole with an auger, muttering over a knot of red wool. Behind her the town waits on the bank. She stops, listens, and points: that way bears, that way doesn't. By noon she is frying pancakes at her stall and refusing hot zopie to a student who skated the wrong way anyway.

Gear

An ice pole with an iron spike, an auger, a knot of red wool (her Druidic Focus), skates with wooden blades, a brazier and a pancake pan, a kettle of zopie (beer, eggs and spice), three skirts and a fur hat. Languages: Dutch, enough English and Frisian for the boatmen, Druidic. Tools: Cartographer's Tools (Guide), for the canal map in her head; she cooks without needing to be told how.

The license. A guild paper with a seal, which she keeps in her bodice and can't read. It names her mother, crossed out, and her, and a blank line under it.

Trijntje's copybook. Her granddaughter's notes on the ice in a careful hand: every crack, every sound, every charm word. Neeltje carries it for luck.

Background

Leiden's ice-reader. Her mother read the ice before her and taught her the charm in the red wool, the tap of the pole and the sound good ice makes, which she can describe to no one. The city licenses her through the fishmongers' guild for a few stuivers a year, and in forty winters she has never been wrong. She can't read, and lately the guild talks about giving the license to a man with a book of tables. She wants it for her granddaughter Trijntje, who has the ear, and who can read.

How to play

  • She is an ally and a contact: she knows every canal, every bridge and every skater in Leiden, and who went out on the ice at night.
  • Her stall is the warmest place on the river. A party that eats there hears the town's news before the town does.
  • Shape Water and Frostbite make her the best friend a party can have on ice, and the worst enemy.
  • The hook is the license. Help her keep it for Trijntje, and Leiden's winter is the party's.

Why this build

  • Why 4th: a licensed hedge-wise woman, not a sage: a few strong charms and forty winters of knowing.
  • Circle of the Land with the Polar land: ice and cold water are her whole art.
  • Guide for the woman who walks ahead of the town on the ice; Healer for the skaters she has pulled out.
