The Gotthard pass carries the mule trains between Lucerne and Milan over the Alps, through the Swiss canton of Uri and down the Leventina, which Uri rules. Lanternhall's Field Service, a dozen medics who travel with the city watch and the caravans (The Setting, Lanternhall), takes paid contracts on it, and the brigands on the Italian side have learned what that means.

On the Gotthard road, the Field Service medic riding with the caravan draws his bow at the brigands' captain and puts the arrow into the back of a muleteer who has just been knifed. The muleteer stands up. The brigands, who were told the caravan carried only doctors, reconsider.

Background

A muleteer's son from Uri, twenty-nine, who drove his father's mules over the Gotthard from the age of twelve, walked to Basel at twenty to study at Lanternhall, and found the Hall would sooner put him back on the road. The Field Service taught him to shoot before it trusted him with a lancet, and now he rides escort on the pass he grew up on, with a bow across his saddle and a surgeon's chest on the mule behind. He knows every inn between Lucerne and Bellinzona, every muleteer by name and most of the brigands by reputation. The muleteers call him the Herr Doktor and don't mean it as a joke. He wants to finish his doctorate, which needs two terms in Basel he can't afford, and he wants the Hall to stop sending him students who faint.

How to play

  • The bow is the medicine chest: one arrow heals a muleteer 150 ft away without a roll, the other goes into the brigand.
  • Covering Shot: when a friend drops, his next arrow is already in the air, and they get up with Temporary Hit Points.
  • On the Gotthard he is a guide as much as a medic: he knows the inns, the weather, the muleteers and the brigands, and Camp Physic keeps a caravan well.
  • He is a Lanternhall man without a degree. Students who faint, masters who forget his name and a bursar who remembers his fees are all part of his life.
  • The hook is the pass. A party hiring a caravan escort over the Alps gets him, his mule and his opinions, and the brigands on the Italian side know his bow.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: six seasons on the pass, Extra Attack and Covering Shot. Field Pharmacology (10th) needs the two terms in Basel.
  • Combat Medic: the Combat Medic's opening vignette, the Field Service medic on the Gotthard road (The Classes, Physician, Combat Medic). Abel Fenwick rode two seasons with the same Field Service before he went to sea.
  • Sage for a Lanternhall man; Skilled for a muleteer's son who can still drive a train. Longbow, not crossbow, for medicated shots at range.
