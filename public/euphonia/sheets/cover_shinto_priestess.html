Japan has shut its doors, and closing the country may have closed it to foreign magic too; it may be its own pocket universe, and its full treatment is still to come. Her sheet keeps her world light: a shrine, a village, and the hidden world, kakuriyo, close at hand.

Dusk on a mountain road, and something wearing a yamabushi's clothes has a village child by the wrist. The priestess doesn't run. She opens her hands, and the light comes off her like morning off a lake, and the thing in the borrowed clothes lets go to cover its eyes.

Iron and Salt (Adventuring Gear, cheap and common). A pouch of salt and a few iron nails. Ten minutes to iron and salt a room or small building (a great house: 10 minutes a floor or wing) wards every door, window and chimney of it until dawn: a Hidden creature can't pass inward unless someone inside who isn't Hidden invites it (a Charmed householder counts; a lodger's invitation stands while they lodge), or it succeeds on a DC 13 Charisma save, at Disadvantage while the Hidden are at the ebb (not on an open night); one that fails can't enter that place by any warded opening that night. A gap in the iron or a break in the salt ends the ward on that opening. The salt at a shrine's threshold is older than the rule.

Background

An aasimar, born to a shrine family in a mountain province, with the light in her from birth; the shrine's account is that its kami looked at her once, closely. She keeps the shrine's rites, writes its charms, and is sent for when a child is lost at dusk, which in her valley happens often enough that she keeps a lantern by the door. She has never seen a foreigner and doesn't expect to. What happens beyond the four gates is the shogunate's business; what happens at the edge of the village is hers.

How to play

  • You are the light in the dark. Radiance of the Dawn is your opener and your answer to magical darkness; Spirit Guardians is your Concentration spell, and War Caster keeps it.
  • Warding Flare is your Reaction every round: Disadvantage on the attack, and Temporary HP for whoever was targeted. It comes back on a Short Rest.
  • Heavenly Wings once a day: fly above the fight and rain Sacred Flame. Inner Radiance instead when the Spirit Guardians are up and you're in the middle.
  • Out of a fight: a lost child at dusk, a thing in borrowed clothes, a threshold that needs salting. Remove Curse, Dispel Evil and Good and the Iron and Salt are the shrine's tools.
  • Divine Intervention once a day, for the spell you didn't prepare.

Why this build

  • Light Domain: the aasimar's light, the shrine's lantern, and a domain for driving off what comes at dusk. The setting's Hidden Domain (a go-between who bargains for the taken) is the other fit, for a priestess whose work is the children hidden by the kami; a table can swap it.
  • Level 10: Divine Intervention, so her kami answers when she asks. A priestess of standing in her valley, not a legend of the realm.
  • Acolyte (Wis +2, Cha +1; Magic Initiate (Cleric)). Thaumaturge and Potent Spellcasting: a priestess, not a temple guard; no armor.
  • Wisdom 20 by 8th through War Caster and an Ability Score Improvement: DC 17, and Concentration that holds.
