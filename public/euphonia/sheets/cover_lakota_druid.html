In 1668 her people, the Eastern Dakota of Mille Lacs, are a woodland and wild-rice people who hunt bison on foot at the prairie edge. She knows of the colonists, and her attention is on her own world. Dakota ceremony isn't depicted.

The hunters on foot have the herd moving toward the river breaks when one cow turns out of the dust and comes back through them, and they part for her. The thing that has been following the herd for three days, which isn't a wolf, finds out what she is when she reaches it.

Background

Eastern Dakota, of the woodland bands around Mille Lacs, where the lakes give wild rice in the fall and the prairie edge gives bison. Her people hunt on foot, with the herd driven toward the breaks and the bows waiting. She has taken the bison's shape since she was young, and her people know it the way they know which of the hunters runs fastest: a fact about her, not a secret. The French are on the far side of the Anishinaabe, trading kettles and guns, and she has heard about them more than she has seen them. Her world is the lakes, the rice, the herd, and the things that live past the camp's fires, and she has enough to do there.

How to play

  • Your Bonus Action is the bison. Take the form before the fight closes, and the Temporary HP and AC 18 do the rest; when they're gone you're still on your own 83.
  • In the form you can still cast your Circle spells: open the fight with Fount of Moonlight or Moonbeam up, then charge.
  • Out of a fight, the forms are tools: a raven to see the herd from above, an elk to cover ground, a wolf to follow a trail. Speak with Animals is always prepared; the herd talks.
  • Pass without Trace and Locate Animals or Plants run the hunt; Plant Growth on the rice beds (the 8-hour version) gives a lake twice the harvest for a year.
  • Wild Companion: a spare Wild Shape use or slot calls a familiar (a Fey creature) to scout the camp's edge at night.

Why this build

  • Level 8: a rising figure, not a legend. It gives her CR 2 forms (a real bison), Fly Speeds for scouting, and a fourth spell level; a table can raise her as her story grows.
  • Guide (Wis +2, Con +1; Magic Initiate (Druid)) is the 2024 background for someone who reads country and moves through it; Tough (human Versatile) stacks under the Moon druid's Temporary HP.
  • Warden: hide armor and a bow, because she hunts on foot. Primal Strike at 7th, because the bison is her main way of fighting.
  • Wisdom goes to 20 by 8th: AC 18 in form, DC 16, and Increased Toughness adds +5 to Constitution saves.

Wild Shape

The bison. Her signature form. The core rules have no bison, so she uses a Large beast of CR 2 or lower that the DM approves as one: a heavy grazer with a horned charge (the Rhinoceros's numbers are the usual stand-in). In the form she keeps her own 83 Hit Points and her Wisdom, Intelligence, Charisma and proficiencies; the beast's body gives her its Strength, Dexterity, Constitution, Speed and attacks.
