Garabed Vanetsi

Human · Wayfarer · Sorcerer 9 (Kinetic) · level 9
ShowcaseLevel 9Kinetic OriginIstanbul, 1668The siege of Candia

The siege of Candia on Crete is in its twenty-fourth year, and the grand vizier has gone to finish it himself. Everything it needs, powder and shot and cannon and timber, goes out from the arsenal and the Galata quays on a porter's back. Istanbul's porters, the hamals, are a guild of men from the east (Armenians and Kurds, mostly) with saddle-pads on their backs and loads on them that would stop a mule. The Kinetic sorcerer is much in demand wherever something heavy has to be moved and nobody wants to pay for oxen.

The bronze culverin is coming off the cart wrong, and six hamals are under it when the rope parts. It stops. It hangs a yard above them with nothing holding it, and on the quay their kâhya, who hasn't moved, says in Armenian, "Out, then," and when they are out he lets it down onto the cradle as gently as a basket of eggs. The Venetian spy on the next pier writes very fast.

Gear

A porter's semer, the padded saddle on his back, worn shiny; a coil of rope and a carrying pole as his Arcane Focus; a sheepskin jacket and a felt cap; a knife (as a dagger); the gang's tally-sticks and a purse of akçe for their wages; Thieves' Tools, for the customs warehouse's worst lock; his mother's prayer scroll from the winter the house knocked, rolled small in a tin case. The knocking didn't stop. She felt better. Languages: Turkish (in place of Common), Armenian and the dock Greek of Galata. Tools: Thieves' Tools (Wayfarer).

The kâhya's seal. A small brass seal on a cord: the guild's mark for a gang-boss. On the quays of Galata it gets his gang paid, his loads waved through the customs house, and his word taken over a clerk's. The guild's elders gave it to him the week the culverin stopped.

Background

Born in Van, by the lake, in a house that knocked. Every night for a winter it rapped in the wall behind his bed; his mother sent for the priest, then for a better priest, who wrote her a prayer scroll and took a fee. The knocking stopped when he was fourteen and walked west with his uncle to carry loads in Istanbul, as the men of his village have for a hundred years. He never stopped carrying. The other porters noticed that his loads didn't weigh what they should, that a bale he steadied never slipped, and that the quay's bullies learned not to shove him. Now he is the kâhya, the gang-boss, of thirty hamals at the Galata customs house, and the arsenal sends for his gang when it has a gun to move for Crete. He sends money to Van, and dreads the day the vizier's men decide he is worth more on the siege lines than on the quay.

How to play

  • Equal and Opposite (4 a day, Bonus Action, a minute): every hit on him lands 3 softer (half his Charisma modifier, round up), and whoever struck from 5 ft goes 5 ft back. Braced, he climbs at his full Speed, across the warehouse ceiling with his hands free, and a fall does him no harm (Deflection).
  • Your Release moves people: 10 ft per Pressure, and Prone at 3. Push them off the quay, out of the doorway, away from the friend on the ground. Or move yourself: the same push, on you, is a 30-ft leap across the gap with nobody getting a swing.
  • Deflection turns your Pressure into a shield for the whole party, 4 times a day. A held 3 that you didn't get to Release isn't wasted if someone you like is about to be hit.
  • Haste, Telekinesis and Arcane Hand come free with the origin; build your list around moving things, and let the origin do the hitting.
  • Porter doubles what you can carry, and nothing you carry spills. On the quays that is your trade and your standing; on the road it is the party's baggage.
  • You have Strength 8 and win every arm-wrestle on the waterfront. Don't explain.

Why this build

  • Kinetic Origin: the text's knocking houses and its "much in demand wherever something heavy has to be moved" are the same man at twelve and at forty; Porter at 6th is his trade, written into the origin.
  • Level 9: Telekinesis and Arcane Hand come at 9th, and a gang-boss the arsenal sends for has earned them.
  • Wayfarer (Cha +2, Dex +1; Lucky): a boy who walked west from Van to carry loads. Tough (human Versatile) for the back.
  • New character: no roster row has a Kinetic sorcerer, so he is invented: Garabed Vanetsi, "Garabed of Van", as the quays would call a man who walked west from Van.
