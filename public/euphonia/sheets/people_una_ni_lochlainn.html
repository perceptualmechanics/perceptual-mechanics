In Ireland the fairy doctor, the bean feasa, is the one a family sends for when a child or a cow has been "taken" and something else left in its place (Life on the Border, Hidden Domain Cleric). After the Cromwellian conquest, soldiers and adventurers were paid in confiscated land across Munster and Leinster, and many of the dispossessed were moved west of the Shannon.

A cottage door in the Burren on a November night, the limestone white under the moon. A woman of fifty with a bowl of new milk stands on the threshold and talks into the dark in a reasonable voice, about a child who was taken at Samhain and what would be fair to give for her. Behind her, the thing in the cradle has stopped crying to listen. Out in the dark, something is listening too, and has started to haggle.

Background

A bean feasa of the Burren who went under the hill at eleven and came back at eighteen knowing the way. The parish owes her trust, a share of every harvest and silence about what she sees at night, and she owes it every bargain struck in its favor. The priest doesn't approve of her and sends for her anyway when a child cries in a voice that isn't its own. She deals with the Gentry the way a good attorney deals with a difficult landlord: politely, precisely and never taking the first offer. The Cromwellian settlers on the new estates think her a quaint survival, and cut thorns and level the old forts for plow-land and wonder why the cattle die; she has started a list. She wants the Mac Mathúna child home before the year turns, without paying more than the Gentry think she's worth.

How to play

  • She talks first. Parley before Initiative, and a fight that never starts is one she won.
  • Bargains: Advantage on reading the Hidden and on striking a bargain with them, with Wisdom. A bargain she strikes binds both sides.
  • Bring Back is for the taken: a Charmed or carried-off friend comes back to themselves, and the fallen get up.
  • Play her as patient, dry and not to be hurried. She has never lost a child she went after, and she has paid for some of them.
  • The hook is the Mac Mathúna child, and the price. Úna will want someone to stand behind her at the door with iron, quietly.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: thirty-four years of bargains, Rowan and Red Thread, and Banishment for the ones who won't deal.
  • Hidden Domain: the Hidden Domain chapter's opening vignette, the woman at a cottage door in Clare with a bowl of milk (Life on the Border, Hidden Domain Cleric).
  • Cunning Folk, the book's background for the healer nobody licensed; Magic Initiate (Druid) for the Burren's plants.
