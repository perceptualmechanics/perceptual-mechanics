Background

An Evenki shaman of the upper Lena, chosen by a dead shaman's spirit through an illness that lasted three winters, who walks the Tree's three worlds for her clan. The fort at Yakutsk wants fur, not licenses, and she gives it exactly as many sables as the forest can spare, which is never enough. Her nephew is held in the hostage house there. She wants him out before this year's furs come in short.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • She walks the three worlds: Reach Beyond once a day, and Soul's Journey for the road she has walked.
  • The Mead-Bench: the fort's Cossacks take her for one of their own after an evening. That is how she will get into the hostage house.
  • The hook is her nephew. A party at Yakutsk on tribute day has found her.

Why this build

  • Why 10th: a shaman of her clan for fifteen years, with Wider Vistas.
  • The Tree (the roster's Yggdrasil): the world's axis, climbed up and down.
  • Guide and Magic Initiate (Druid) for a life on the Lena; Tough for winter.
