Ammakka

Dwarf · Artisan · Natural Philosopher 9 (Experimentalist) · level 9
PeopleIndiaGolconda and the Kollur minesExperimentalistLevel 9

Mutapa's artifice runs on platinum, which no one else can work. Golconda's Kollur mines give the world its great diamonds. Ammakka wants to know whether a diamond can do for magic what platinum does for Mutapa, and three crowns would like to know her answer before she does.

The grading sheds at Kollur at dawn, the merchants not yet arrived. A dwarf woman of about a hundred and forty holds a rough stone in tongs in a lamp flame, two fingers on her own wrist, timing something by her pulse, and mutters a number in Telugu. The stone does not change. She writes the number down, frowns, and moves the lamp three fingers to the left. Behind her, a scorched patch on the wall marks the last time the stone did change.

The untested stone. A rough diamond the size of a hen's egg, from the deepest pit at Kollur. Every test she has made on smaller stones says this one will answer. She has not dared yet.

The rice pot. Her notebook lives in it, under a hand of rice. Thieves have searched her house four times. None of them has thought to cook dinner.

Background

A diamond appraiser of Golconda, of a family that says it came from the treasure-keeping yakshas. She can tell a Kollur stone from a Borneo one by its smell, she says, which is a lie told to protect a method. She has started testing whether a diamond can do for magic what platinum does for Mutapa; her trials so far are inconclusive and twice explosive, and she keeps the notebook in a pot of rice. The Invisible College has written twice asking for her method; she has written back asking for theirs. She wants a clean answer, with nobody from three crowns watching when she gets it.

How to play

  • She is a contact who knows stones: Touchstone tells a true diamond from a false one, and platina from silver, which almost no one outside Mutapa can.
  • Correspondence: one question a week to the Republic of Letters. Her correspondents are suspicious of her, and answer anyway.
  • In a fight she is a dwarf with a stonebow and two jars that should not be shaken. Volatile is real: the wall at Kollur is scorched for a reason.
  • The hook is the untested stone. Whoever stands guard while she tests it will be the first to know what the answer is, and the first three crowns come looking for.

Why this build

  • Why 9th: a hundred and forty years of appraising, and Correspondence: she writes to people who would very much like to read her notebook.
  • Experimentalist, as the roster has her: the trials are the work.
  • A dwarf, as the roster has her: the yakshas' line. Artisan and Crafter for the scales, the loupe and the glass.

Features

Natural Philosopher (Experimentalist)
1Natural Philosophy. Study (Bonus Action, or 1 minute outside a fight): a creature, object, or feature you can see within 60 ft; no check. One creature plus one object or feature at a time; studying another replaces the earlier one of its kind. A creature stays studied 1 minute; an object or feature until you finish a Short or Long Rest. Learn one fact. A creature: its AC, which save has the lowest bonus, one Resistance, Immunity, or Vulnerability (the DM chooses; or tells you it has none), or its Speeds and special senses. An object or feature: what it's made of, rough age, how it was made, or where it's weakest. Studying what you're already studying renews it and teaches another fact. You can study the place itself (from level 3, that finds Mechanism's features). Only what can be seen, heard, or measured; never thoughts or feelings. Measure: by eye and instrument, a thing's distance, height, weight, and temperature to within a tenth, and the time of day to within a few minutes.
1Ballistics. Crossbows and firearms (Pistol, Musket, the period firearms; the stonebow is a crossbow) can use Int for attack and damage. Calculated Shot: once per turn, a crossbow or firearm hit on a creature you're studying deals +3d8 of the weapon's damage type. Weapon Mastery: the mastery property of one kind of crossbow or firearm you're proficient with, changed on a Long Rest: Hand Crossbows (her stonebow counts as one): Vex, so a hit carries Advantage into her next attack.
1Aim. When you study a creature on your turn, your next attack roll against it this turn has Advantage if it's a crossbow or firearm attack using your Intelligence. Move as you like before or after; restudying the same creature counts, so you can Aim every turn. Breathe out, and let the number settle.
2First Causes. A minute examining something physical, then ask the DM why it happened (or is happening): a sentence or two on the likeliest cause that can be seen, measured, or tested. Too little to go on, the DM says so and the use is kept. Never about minds or feelings. 5 uses per Long Rest.
2Instruments. Expertise with two of your tools, or with one tool and Investigation.
3Mechanism. Finding the feature: study the place (Bonus Action, or 1 minute outside a fight) and the DM names one feature you could set off (part of the place: nothing you or your allies placed, lit, or made), the laws it allows, and its reach; you're now studying it. A rich place has two or three (study again for the next); a place with none costs no action. Setting it off: Utilize action, one precise act from where you stand (or at a distance: a shot, a thrown stone, a tug on a line already rigged); choose one of the named laws. Chosen creatures in a 20-ft Cube within its reach, origin within 60 ft, save, DC 17. Fail: 5d8 and the law's effect. Success: half damage only. The feature is spent; what it leaves isn't a new one until the DM says so. 5 uses per Long Rest. Mechanics (a load, slope, hanging weight): Dex, Bludgeoning; Restrained (pinned) until the end of its next turn; rubble (Difficult Terrain). Heat (fire, furnace, powder, sealed vessel): Dex, Fire; pushed 10 ft from the feature and Disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn; flammables catch fire. Pneumatics (gas, smoke, foul air): Con, Poison; Poisoned until the end of its next turn; Heavily Obscured until the end of yours. Hydrostatics (water held back): Str, Bludgeoning; pushed 10 ft from the feature and Prone; Difficult Terrain for 1 minute. Optics (strong light and a lens, mirror, or glass of the place): Con, Radiant; Blinded until the end of its next turn.
3Apparatus. Alchemist's Supplies and Glassblower's Tools (for either you already have, another tool from the class list).
3Experiments. An hour of a Long Rest with both tools prepares two; they keep until your next Long Rest and work only for you. Utilize action, no studied feature: release one at a point within 60 ft, choosing its energy as you mix it. Chosen creatures in a 20-ft Cube there (Lightning: a 60-by-5-ft Line from you) save, DC 17. Fail: 4d8 (6d8 overloaded) (one die fewer than Mechanism, minimum 2d8) and the energy's effect. Success: half damage only. Fire: Dex; unattended flammables catch fire. Lightning: Dex (Disadvantage in metal armor); no Reactions until the start of its next turn. Thunder: Con; pushed 10 ft from the point of origin and Deafened until the end of its next turn; heard 300 ft away. Cold: Con; Speed halved until the end of its next turn. Acid: Dex; AC −1 until it finishes a Long Rest (doesn't stack).
3Volatile. Roll a d6 on each release: on a 1 it still works, and you take half the damage rolled, no save.
3Demonstration. An hour to set up and an audience: a harmless, plainly unmagical demonstration (sparks from a rubbed globe, a bell falling silent in an emptied jar, water frozen on a summer afternoon). For 24 hours, Advantage on Charisma checks to impress, persuade, or raise money from anyone who watched it to the end.
5Out of the Line. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you and you aren't in Medium or Heavy armor: halve that damage (round down).
6Overload. When you release one, add two dice (6d8); Volatile then goes wrong on a 1 or 2.
6Touchstone. Alchemist's Supplies and 10 minutes: what a metal, alloy, salt, or acid is and how pure (a clipped or debased coin, adulterated gold and with what, a powder that isn't what its seller says). You tell platina from silver, which almost no one outside Mutapa can yet do reliably, and you know what that's worth to the people who'd pay.
7Leverage. Your Shove's save DC can use Int in place of Str. Once per round, when you or a visible ally pushes a creature, it goes 10 ft farther. With a lever, rope, or pulley, you lift, drag, or brace as a creature two sizes larger: a portcullis, a loaded cart, a boom, a door four guards are leaning on.
9Correspondence. One question by letter to the Republic of Letters (history, nature, a place, a creature, an object). Someone knows: a reply in 1d6 days with as much as they know, which the DM decides. No one knows: a letter says so. The learned can be wrong. Then not again for 7 days.
