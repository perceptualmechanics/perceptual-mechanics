The Amis are matrilineal: the house, its fields and its name pass through the women, a husband moves into his wife's house, and a wife who has had enough puts his things outside the door. The harvest festival, Ilisin, is danced by the age-sets of the men, and the women of the house feed everyone who comes.

Dusk on a house platform above the east coast, the mountains black behind it. A tall woman with betel-red teeth pours millet wine for a Chengtian trader, two Atayal visitors and a sunburned Japanese castaway, all on one mat. She is teaching the castaway his first Amis word, which is the word for more. Below in the field her youngest daughter is pretending not to listen to a young man singing.

Background

The eldest sister of her house in an Amis village below the mountains, and so its head: she runs its fields, its rice, its millet wine and its marriages. She has put two husbands' things outside her door and stays on good terms with both; one still brings her fish. Everyone who comes along the coast eats on her platform first, which is how she knows what the Chengtian soldiers on the west side of the mountains are doing before anyone else does. She wants her youngest daughter married to a man who can sing, and the soldiers kept on their own side.

How to play

  • She is a host first: the first open door on the east coast, and the person who knows who has come over the mountains.
  • Her house's marriages are her politics. Ask her about anyone on the coast, and she will tell you who they married and why it went wrong.
  • In a fight she stands at the top of her own ladder with a spear, which is a hard place to get past.
  • The hook is the soldiers. She wants them on their own side of the mountains, and she pays in rice, wine and a welcome.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: the head of a house, with a lifetime in its fields and a healer's reach.
  • Circle of the Land, Tropical land, and the Warden order for a woman who has climbed a house ladder with a spear.
  • Farmer and Tough for the fields; Musician because a house that marries daughters to singers knows its songs.
