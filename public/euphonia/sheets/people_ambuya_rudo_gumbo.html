The royal medium in Euphonia: a Shona king's legitimacy runs through the royal dead, the mhondoro, who speak through mediums on rain, war and famine, and here they answer. Her spells are the Life Domain's; the voice is the royal spirit's, and she is not the one with the opinion.

The court on the plateau, the day the royal spirit is to be asked a question. An old woman in black cloth sits on a mat with her staff across her knees, and the king's counsellors sit very still. She answers the first question in her own voice, tartly. Then, without any warning at all, she doesn't: the voice that answers the second question is a man's, two hundred years old, and it has views about the shewstones.

The returned gifts. A list in a granddaughter's hand of every gift the Shava house has sent, and the date each went back. Munyaradzi Shava has a copy. He keeps sending them.

The spirit's axe. An old iron axe-head on a carved haft, older than the kingdom's new fire. Whoever carries it speaks for the royal dead, which is why no one has ever tried to take it from her.

Background

The senior royal medium. She was chosen at nineteen and has carried the same royal spirit for over forty years, which she describes as a marriage to a man with very strong views. She remembers nothing of what he says and is told afterward, and lately what he says is about the stones, which the living king did not want to hear. The shewstone house sends her gifts; she returns them unopened. She would like it known that she is not the one with the opinion, and has noticed that this makes no difference to anyone. At home the day after a sitting, shelling groundnuts and cross about it, she would like, for an afternoon, to be spoken to as herself by someone who isn't waiting for the other voice.

How to play

  • She is two people at the table: an old woman with opinions about groundnuts and grandchildren, and a dead king with opinions about everything else. Play the switch without warning.
  • As a patron she can heal anything short of the grave and some things past it (Raise Dead, Regenerate). She asks in return that the party listen to what the spirit said about the stones, and act on it.
  • Commune, Divination and Augury are the royal dead answering. Whether the answer serves the crown or the mediums is the politics of Mutapa in one question.
  • Talk to her as herself, for an afternoon, and she will remember it longer than any gift.

Why this build

  • Why 14th: forty years carrying the same spirit, and the senior medium of the realm. Divine Intervention is a dead king answering on the day he is asked.
  • Life Domain, as the roster has her: the royal mediums are asked for rain and health before they are asked for war.
  • Acolyte and Magic Initiate for a life at the shrine since nineteen; Healer, because the people who come to her house the day after a sitting are mostly sick.
