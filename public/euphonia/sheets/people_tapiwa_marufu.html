Tapiwa Marufu

Human · Entertainer · Bard 5 (Lore) · level 5
PeopleMutapa and the Indian OceanSofalaCollege of LoreLevel 5

A bira is a Shona all-night ceremony of music, dance and beer, held to call the ancestors to speak to their families. The mbira dzavadzimu, the "mbira of the ancestors", is its instrument, played in interlocking parts until dawn. In Sofala's new quarter, where the guild's journeymen come from every corner of the empire, a bira is also a housewarming.

A courtyard in Sofala's new quarter after dark, beer out, a crowd of journeymen and their grandmothers. A lanky young man with an mbira in a gourd resonator plays a pattern that loops and loops until you can't find where it starts. Somewhere above the courtyard, one of the guild's singing birds starts to sing the other part. The grandmothers look up. A woman in the corner sits down hard, and speaks in her dead father's voice.

A song for Simbarashe. Not written down. A new pattern, in the old style, built on the first bird-song the old man ever wrote. He has never played it for anyone.

Background

An mbira player who plays every bira in Sofala's new quarter from dusk to dawn. By day he sleeps under a shade tree, or tunes the guild's singing birds to mbira songs, which the guild says it never taught him and can't make him stop: the birds hear him and learn, and he hears them back. He is welcome in every household in the quarter and would be welcome in better ones if he went. He wants to play once for Simbarashe Moyo, who wrote the birds' first songs, and hear what the old man thinks.

How to play

  • He is an ally and the player any bira would hire: a welcome in every household in the quarter comes with him.
  • Speak with Animals and Animal Messenger are the birds. Ask him where a bird has been, and he will ask it.
  • Speak with Dead is the bira: the ancestors come when he plays. Bring him to a family that needs to hear from one.
  • The hook is Simbarashe Moyo. Get him in front of the old man, and the party will hear the song.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: young, and already the best player in the quarter. The birds are learning from him.
  • College of Lore: the mbira's whole tradition is memory, and calling the ancestors.
  • Entertainer and Musician for the bira; Skilled for the shade-tree life of a man who sleeps in other people's courtyards.
