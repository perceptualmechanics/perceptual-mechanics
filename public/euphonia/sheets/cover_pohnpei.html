Sehpin

Human · Farmer · Cleric 11 (Life) · level 11
On the coverPohnpei, 1668Nan Madol

Pohnpei is the civilization druids and clerics built: two sorcerer brothers, Olisihpa and Olosohpa, raised Nan Madol's basalt by magic, and in Euphonia they did. Forty years after the Saudeleur fell, the chiefs hold the titles and the priests the rites, and the stones answer neither by right: whoever can still work the stones holds Pohnpei's magic.

A Spanish pilot off the reef sees it from the deck: a column of black basalt, longer than his ship's mast, lifting out of the shallows and laying itself crosswise on a wall that was already old. The figure standing on the wall doesn't look up at the ship. When the pilot asks his crew what they saw, none of them will say.

Background

Of a family that grew yams for the feasts and was never meant for the rites. The stones answered anyway: at fifteen, mending a sea wall, they set a basalt prism with their hands on it and no one else's, and the priests took them to Salapwuk to be taught. Now, forty years past the Saudeleur, they are one of the few left who can still raise a course at Nan Madol, and they keep the people who live behind the walls as well as the walls. A Nahnmwarki asks them for a sea wall and a priest asks them to keep the old rites, and both expect an answer. The Monsoon Road's traders have started asking how the stones were raised, and whether the method can be bought.

How to play

  • You're the one who can still work the stones, and the one who keeps the people inside them. Your best stone spells take minutes, not seconds: plan the wall before the fight.
  • In a fight, Spirit Guardians and healing. Disciple of Life and Blessed Healer make every slot you spend on a friend count twice.
  • Preserve Life is the answer when three people are down at once.
  • Out of a fight: Heroes' Feast and Create Food and Water are the feast that ranks a chief; Hallow keeps a place; Commune asks the island's gods.
  • The chiefs hold the titles and the priests the rites. Every request you take, a sea wall, a feast house, says which of them you answer to.

Why this build

  • Cleric, Life Domain: a civilization druids and clerics built is kept as much as it's raised, and Life is the domain of keeping a people. No 2024 domain is about stone or sea, and the Hidden Domain doesn't fit: Pohnpei's eni are gods, ancestors and owners of places, not a fairy people. The stone work comes from the Cleric list (Stone Shape, Control Water, Hallow).
  • Level 11: Heroes' Feast, the feast a chief ranks by, and the spell slots to raise a course with Stone Shape after a day's healing. One of the few left who can, not a legend like the brothers.
  • Farmer (Wis +2, Con +1; Tough): Pohnpei's chiefs rank by the feast, and a family that grows the feast's yams is a family of standing. Crafter (human Versatile) gives the mason's, woodcarver's and weaver's tools.
  • Thaumaturge and Potent Spellcasting: a priest, not a guard. Wisdom 20 by 8th.
