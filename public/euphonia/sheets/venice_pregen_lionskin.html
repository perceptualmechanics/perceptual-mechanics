Who You Are

Kudzai is patient, precise and faintly offended by anything badly made, which in Venice means most of the locks. He hums while he works, and the suit hums back.

In Venice. Kudzai was born at Sofala to a Shona trading family and raised in their house at Surat. He came in on a Portuguese ship as the quiet technician who retunes Mutapan lamps in Venetian drawing rooms, which gets him into every great house in the city and thanked for it. What he carries home is what he saw.

Your way in. A ship from Sofala lies at the Lazzaretto Nuovo on a forged health pass, and in its hold is a Mutapan device the Republic has impounded. Mutapa's guild of makers wants it back, or destroyed, before Venice's artificers open it. He takes his orders from the factor in Venice of the House of the Bird, Mutapa's bank, which pays Mutapa's spymaster.

Secret. His name isn't on the guild's rolls. He learned his craft in Surat from a Gujarati master who had learned in Sofala, so in Mutapa's eyes he's a curiosity to be watched. Bringing that device home is how he means to earn his name.

The crew. Bettina found you through a lamp that needed retuning in a house you shouldn't have been in. She was a Dutch factor at Surat when you were a boy there; neither of you has said so. Lend the Spring Boots to whoever can't keep up on the roofs.

How to play me. You wear the suit, and you chose its model in Scene 1. As a Guardian you stand beside whoever's in trouble and take the hit for them with the Guardian Reaction; every blow you take, yours or a friend's, charges the suit. As an Infiltrator you are quick and quiet, and the suit starts every fight already charged. In either suit your next Arc Gun hit lets the charge go with a crack you feel in your teeth. Point-blank is fine.

Features

Spellcasting (Mhizha 1). INT half caster; prepare 4. You cast through Tinker's Tools, Artisan's Tools you know, or the Arc Gun, and the hand holding it can perform Somatic components; in the suit, the suit.

Arc Gun (Mhizha 1). Magic action, once per turn: a ranged spell attack (INT + PB) against a target within 120 ft, 1d10 + 3 Lightning on a hit. It's a magic item's attack (Counterspell can't stop it), and items that add to spell attacks add to it. Only you can fire it; rebuild it over a Long Rest if lost.

Tinker (Mhizha 1). With Tinker's Tools and 1 minute, mend a break as Mending does, or build a Tiny clockwork trinket that does one simple thing for 8 hours (up to 2 at a time; one can be a small lamp). Retuning: 10 minutes fixes a drifted Mutapan curiosity (for a gold piece or two, or a favor), and gets you through doors a letter wouldn't.

Preparations (Mhizha 1). 2 per Long Rest, nonmagical. Bonus Action: throw one at a point within 30 ft. Smoke: 10-ft-radius Sphere Heavily Obscured until the end of your next turn. Slick: 10-ft square of Difficult Terrain for 1 minute; DEX save or Prone. Flash: 5-ft-radius Sphere; CON save or Blinded until the end of its next turn. DC 13.

Devices (Mhizha 2). You build two different devices after a Long Rest (1 hour with Tinker's Tools) and can hand them to anyone. Tonight's two are built. Lockbreaker: its user adds +3 (your INT) to picking locks and disarming traps with Thieves' Tools. Spring Boots: the wearer's jumps are tripled and a fall of 30 ft or less does no damage. Each device: AC 15, 5 HP.

Mhizha Subclass: Lionskin (Mhizha 3). Your subclass.

Lionskin Spells (Lionskin 3). Always prepared: Magic Missile, Thunderwave.

Powered Suit (Lionskin 3). Heavy armor training; any armor he wears becomes his suit: Heavy armor, base AC 18 (no Dex), no Strength requirement, Disadvantage on Stealth; it gives its AC and the benefits below only while he isn't holding a Shield (with one, it's only the armor it was made from). Built In: the Arc Gun fires without being held, and the suit is your focus. Gauntlets: firing the Arc Gun at a creature within 5 ft has no Disadvantage, and that shot can deal Force instead of Lightning; Unarmed Strikes deal 4 Force (1 + INT) and use INT to hit. Tailored: out of a fight it passes as a merchant's coat (WIS (Perception) vs DC 13 to spot it). Model, chosen each Long Rest: Guardian: a Reaction when a creature you can see hits an ally within 5 ft: reduce the damage by 1d10 + 3 (2 per Long Rest). Infiltrator: no Stealth Disadvantage from the suit, Speed +10 ft, and the suit starts every fight charged.

Kinetic Charge (Lionskin 3). When you take any damage while you wear the suit (damage to Temporary HP counts), or you reduce damage with the Guardian Reaction, the suit becomes charged (one charge); an Infiltrator suit is also charged when you roll Initiative. Release it when you hit with the Arc Gun: +1d6 Force (2d6 as an Infiltrator). Fades if you take off the suit or finish a rest.

Magic Initiate (Wizard) (Feat (Human Versatile)). Light and Mage Hand; Shield always prepared, once per Long Rest without a slot. INT.

Skilled (Feat (Mutapan Agent)). Proficiency in Athletics, History and Thieves' Tools.

Resourceful (Human). You gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest.

Equipment

Chain mail, rebuilt into his suit (calibrated Mutapan work, tailored to pass as a merchant's coat; copper-wire mane on the helm); Arc Gun (a light gun, built into the right gauntlet); Tinker's Tools; Smith's Tools; Thieves' Tools; Lockbreaker and Spring Boots (devices); Dagger; Traveler's Clothes; Lamp; Backpack, Crowbar, Rope, Grappling Hook; Potion of Healing (Common magic item; 2d4 + 2); 28 GP.
