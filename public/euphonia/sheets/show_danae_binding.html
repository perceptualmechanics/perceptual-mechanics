Background

Golnar is a pari of the bride's family at the wedding Farrokh of Isfahan ran from, holding a shape in Isfahan to get the painted glass cup back. The pari call themselves better than us and mean it as accuracy. She is polite, patient and bound by every promise she makes, which she makes very carefully. She wants the cup back unbroken, and Farrokh returned to the feast of his own free will, which she thinks would be kinder than the other way. She knows where Shushan Ghukasian's younger son is, and won't say. She has tried the coffeehouses and found them fascinating, and hasn't yet worked out why everyone runs from a wedding. Her family in Paristan, and the Hidden working through her, are who she answers to; Nane Gulbahar and Banu Shirin know her.

How to play

  • Knot and talk in one breath: Kind Words ride the knot, once for each knot she ties. A knotted creature saves against her warps at Disadvantage, and her Charisma checks against it have Advantage.
  • A geis isn't a cage: a creature can break it, and pays 2d8 + 5 Psychic to. Most won't, which is the point.
  • Handfast is what Danae of the Binding are sent for: tie it at a wedding, a contract, or a truce, and each side knows when the other breaks the promise. Five at a time. The one she means to tie is Farrokh's.
  • Autumn is your season: an autumn warp charms whatever walks in, and a knotted creature saves against it at Disadvantage. Winter holds a runner in place.
  • You carry a piece of the Hidden: you are a Hidden creature. A place warded with iron and salt keeps you out by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in. An invitation lasts until dawn or until it's taken back, and one given to a lodger can last the stay. A Danae's Sight or a Hidden Killer's craft knows you for what you are.
  • Fey Ancestry on top of it: Golnar has Advantage against being Charmed. She does the charming.

Why this build

  • Why 9th: the roster's level. Heartstrings and Handfast (6th) are a pari's wedding magic; Thresholds (9th) is the way home to Paristan. Oath, the promise with teeth, is 10th, a level on.
  • Elf (high elf), because the setting writes the pari as elves; Prestidigitation for the rosewater. Noble, for a guest of the bride's family: Charisma, History, Persuasion. The dark half, because autumn charms, and her knotted marks save against it at Disadvantage.
  • Her Token of Method is Binding's, the spool of gray thread that can't be cut: when the warps run out, it gives one back, once a day.

Features

Danae (Binding)
1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't come in through an opening warded with them unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in (they bar only the openings they line, so teleportation and a Door aren't barred); a companion who is already inside can (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. Within 30 ft: see thresholds between worlds, and know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or a shape-changing effect, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 17). 6 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save. Success: it moves to the nearest unoccupied space outside, unaffected. Failure: it stays, and the warp (and its season) affect it at the start of its next turn if it is still inside. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +4 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the dark half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): autumn (Wis save or Charmed until the start of its next turn, or Disadvantage on its next save before the end of your next turn) and winter (Con save, only on entering, or Speed 0 and no Reactions until the start of its next turn). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Short or Long Rest: unravel one or more of your warps, in place or unused (a subclass's warps count) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 39 with every warp, 14 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Courtly Grace. Proficiency in one of Deception, Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion; Advantage on Charisma checks against a creature you've knotted.
3Knots. Warps can be knots. Bonus Action: tie a creature within 60 ft to another creature or a fixed point within 30 ft of it and speak a geis. Each unwilling creature makes a Wis save; if all succeed, no knot (the warp is still spent); in a two-creature knot, if one succeeds the other is tied to the ground instead. A knotted unwilling creature is bound, repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, and has Disadvantage on saves against your warps, its save to end the knot included. Stay (no more than 30 ft from what it's tied to), Spare (can't target it with an attack or harmful effect), or Heed (Disadvantage on attacks against anything else, or break the geis instead). Breaking a geis, as it's about to do what the geis forbids: 2d8 + 5 Psychic first, the knot comes undone, then it acts.
3Kind Words. As part of tying a knot (once per knot), speak to one creature in it that can hear and understand you: Wis save or Frightened or Charmed (your choice) by you until the end of its next turn. At the start of any of its turns it can defy you at the geis's price, ending the condition.
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
6Heartstrings. Disadvantage on saves against Kind Words. When they succeed against a creature knotted to another creature, the other is affected too, without a save.
6Handfast. Outside combat, 10 minutes, no warp: tie a binding between willing creatures who make a promise in your presence (a marriage, a contract, an apprenticeship, a truce, a sworn friendship). Each knows when another breaks it, and roughly which way the others lie, at any distance on the plane. It holds until all agree to untie it, or you do. Cha-modifier bindings at once; a new one unties the oldest.
7Reading the Season. Bonus Action: up to 5 creatures you can see within 30 ft are read for 1 hour; the DM tells you each one's temper (one for each group of like creatures) and what lies under it. Advantage on Insight against them, and on Cha checks that play to their temper.
7Open Book. Detect Thoughts PB times per Long Rest without a slot; a creature you've read has Disadvantage on the save.
9The Door Ajar. While Away, each Flicker can leave a free warp (any shape, a season you know) in the space you left.
9Thresholds. Spend 1 minute at a threshold you can see with The Sight to open it: Plane Shift without a slot or Material component, for you and up to eight willing creatures, only to or from the Hidden, arriving at the threshold's other side. Once per Long Rest.
9Loop. New shape: a creature trying to leave by any means makes a Wis save or steps back in from the opposite side, losing the movement (or the slot) it spent, and can't try that Loop again until its next turn; one that succeeds isn't affected by that Loop again.
