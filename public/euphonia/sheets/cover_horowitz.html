Background

Simon Horowitz: a Kabbalist, given a book by the archangel Michael and charged to decipher it. Every page he reads is a rung climbed: what he has deciphered is what he can do, and which angels answer. He trained in a study house (the Acolyte), and he reads the names the way the wizards do; the difference is that one of them reads back. In 1668, two years after Sabbatai Zevi converted before the sultan, every Kabbalist is asking what they actually heard. Simon has a book that answers, which is worse.

How to play

  • Two slots before a Short Rest, and one angel a fight: the angel and a spell. The angel is the better use when the fight will run three rounds; Banishment when one thing in the room is the whole fight.
  • Every fight asks one question: which errand? Mend when two allies are low, Guard on whoever is about to be the target (the daring one, the one about to jump), Strike when something has to fall. You can be the one it Guards.
  • Out of a fight, the book: Comprehend Languages and Tongues read and speak anything, the rituals cost nothing, Witch Sight sees through every disguise, and Tongue of Fire knows which messenger is which. Courts can tell you carry messages (Heaven's Crown). An angel on Bear carries a word or a small thing a mile; out of a fight the DM voices it, and it remembers you.
  • Play the decipherment. Each level is a page: tell the table what the new page said, and let the angel that answers be one of its words.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: a rising figure, halfway up the ladder. His angels are Rank 4, a real angel with two blades; Heaven's Crown already has him reading court protocol; the greater angel (10th) and the archangel (14th) are pages he hasn't reached.
  • Acolyte for the +2 Charisma and the study house; its Magic Initiate (Cleric) gives Healing Word and the two cantrips a Kabbalist would expect.
  • Pact of the Tome because the book is the character. Lessons of the First Ones takes a second Magic Initiate (Wizard): Kabbalists are Wizards, Envoys, or both, and Simon reads the names the wizard's way too.
  • Witch Sight and Eldritch Mind: Truesight to see the angels (and anything pretending to be one), and a held Hold Person while the angel works.
