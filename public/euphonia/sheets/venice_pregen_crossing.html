Tilde Zorzut

Danae 3 (Crossing): features through level 3
AC 13 · HP 17 · Speed 35 ft (climb 35 ft) · Spell DC 13

Who You Are

Tilde laughs at heights and at nothing else. She keeps every promise to the letter and not one word past it, which she says is the Hidden in her and her friends say is just Tilde.

In Venice. Her family are hearthfolk from the Friulian hills, elves whose ancestors came out of the Hidden and forgot what they were; she was taken at seven and sent home at eight, remembering. In Venice she carried letters over the roofs for a patrician house until its court wizard lost his license.

Your way in. She'll take the job for one price: her own file from the Ten's archive. It names her for what she is, and it sits in the same press as the report.

Secret. For two years she ran the Ten's own messages over the rooftops for Signor Marco Padavin, the Spy who sleeps in the Secret Chancery during sales. She knows the clerks' windows. He knows her face. She handed his letters in at the cipher clerks' windows and was never let farther inside. She knows the Palace's roofs, the Piombi skylight and the canal windows, and she knows the walls over the canal are too thick for a Door, though a pair of Doors linked through a window works. She has never seen the Secret Chancery or the press.

The crew. Bettina hired you because nobody else can get five people across Venice without touching a street. You don't fully trust her.

How to play me. You're the roof-runner: Venice is a city of roofs, and they're yours. You climb at your full Speed and long-jump 10 ft farther, so a wall is a stair and a narrow canal is a step. Your magic is a place on the map, and every turn you can bend one more piece of the city. A pair of Doors puts the crew where it needs to be: go through and take two friends with you, or leave the pair open and let everyone follow; latch it behind you and you keep the warp. Walls are doors to you, and iron and salt at a house's doors don't stop a Door in its wall. Doors carry only the willing, so you can't drop an enemy through one; in a fight you move your friends and make the enemy's ground worse (a Stretch, a Spring or Summer warp where it has to stand), and you cast with your action. In a chase, you're the road: Doors across the canal, spring under the bravo at the gutter's edge, and Feather Fall when the whole crew steps off the roof together.

Features

Hidden Creature (Danae). You carry a piece of the Hidden in your soul. Where iron and salt ward a doorway, window or chimney, you can't pass inward through it, and no save gets you through. The one way through is to be asked in by someone inside who isn't a Hidden creature; a friend who has gone in ahead can do it. Iron and salt bar only the openings they line: your Doors aren't barred. You can't break a ward yourself, and a friend can. Few Venetian doors are warded in fair week.

Spellcasting (Danae 1). CHA full caster; you learn your spells in dreams and prepare 6 after a Long Rest. Cantrip Damage: a one-die cantrip adds your Cha modifier to one target's damage (until cantrips gain a second die at 5th).

The Sight (Danae 1). Within 30 ft, you see thresholds between worlds, and you know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or shapechange, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. You know Sylvan. The Sight also gave you two skills, Perception and Arcana, which are already in your skill list.

Warps (Danae 1). Bonus Action: bend a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; 4 per Short Rest). Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: your Gates chain together; anyone who moves into one of their own will steps out of the next (nobody pushed in, or caught in a Gate made on them, goes anywhere). Your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once per turn you can make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a DEX save to step clear, and on a failure the warp takes it at the start of its next turn if it's still inside. Warps affect everyone alike and can't overlap; anyone can travel through your warps only once per turn. Saves use your spell DC.

Denial (Danae 1). Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp (end one in place, or spend one you haven't used) for +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning the hit into a miss. The air folds, and the blow arrives somewhere you aren't.

The Half: the light half (spring and summer) (Danae 2). A warp can carry a season. Spring: STR save or you move the creature 15 ft horizontally (a willing creature can teleport 15 ft instead). Summer: 1d6 Fire; creatures you choose are immune. Only one of your warps' seasons takes a creature each turn. A season takes a creature that enters the warp or starts its turn there; a warp made on a creature takes it at the start of the creature's next turn, if it failed its DEX save to step clear.

Flee (Danae 2). Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0): unravel one or more warps, in place or unused. For 1 minute you're Away: Temporary HP = 3 + 5 per warp unraveled; attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport up to 15 ft. You keep casting. It ends early when those Temporary HP are gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it (no action). When it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport up to 15 ft. 1 per Long Rest.

Danae Subclass: Crossing (Danae 3). Your subclass. Proficiency with Thieves' Tools, which can also be your spellcasting focus.

Crossing Spells (Crossing 3). Always prepared: Arcane Lock, Feather Fall, Knock, Silence. Feather Fall is a Reaction: when anyone goes off a roof, you are the net.

Doors (Crossing 3). A warp can be a pair of linked 5-ft Doors on solid surfaces within 60 ft (a wall, floor, ceiling, the side of a boat). Anyone who moves into one of their own will steps out of the other, facing away from its surface; nobody pushed or dropped in goes anywhere. A Door can open in the floor under a willing creature, which drops through. A Door of Your Own: iron and salt bar only the openings they line, so they don't stop you, or anyone who comes through with you, from passing through a Door you open in a house's wall, floor or roof; a Hidden Killer's ward on it does. Inertia: fall into a Door and come out of the other still moving, as far again as you fell; then take one fall's damage for the whole distance (Prone if you take any). Keyhole: one Door can open unseen beyond a wall, door, floor or ceiling up to 5 ft thick within 30 ft, and you know if the space is open. Latch: once on each of your turns, when you pass through a pair of your Doors no more than 30 ft apart, you can close them behind you and get the warp back (Doors left open for friends still cost it). Fellow Travellers: when you go through one of your Doors, up to 2 willing creatures within 5 ft of you go through with you, arriving within 5 ft of the far Door (each its one trip through your warps that turn).

Back Ways (Crossing 3). You're proficient in Stealth (or another skill, if you already were). You have a Climb Speed equal to your Speed (35 ft), and your long jumps carry you 10 ft farther (18 ft with a run-up). After a day in a settlement, you know a route between any two places in it that avoids its main streets, watch posts and toll gates. After an hour in a building, you know every way in and out: doors, windows, chimneys, drains, the servants' stair, and where its walls are thin enough for a Door.

Alert (Feat (Court Creature)). Add your Proficiency Bonus to Initiative; after rolling, you may swap Initiative with a willing ally.

Wood Elf (Species). Darkvision 60 ft; Speed 35 ft; Druidcraft; Longstrider once per Long Rest free (CHA). Fey Ancestry: Advantage on saves against Charmed. Keen Senses: Survival. Trance: no sleep, magic can't put you to sleep, Long Rest in 4 hours.

Equipment
