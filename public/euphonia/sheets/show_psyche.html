The manuscript. The third attempt at her book, forty pages, in a careful hand: "On the Passions, and How They Come Up to the Surface." Each attempt begins with a different patient. None of them gets past the second chapter, because the second chapter is where she would have to explain what she sees.

A gift from a Chinese physician who passed through on the way to Venice, after he watched her for a day. She keeps it for the night she can't.

Background

Forty-one, a judge's widow, who came to the hospital of Bayezid to sit with her mad brother and was still there when he was well, because the physicians had noticed she could do what the music did without it. She doesn't play anything. She watches. Lanternhall has written twice to ask how it's done; she answered the first letter with a question about their patients' sleep and hasn't opened the second. She wants to write down what she does so that a girl in Bursa could learn it from a book, and has started three times. She thinks Basel will understand it in about a century, and she is in no hurry. Kemal of Kasımpaşa, who came back from Candia's countermines not speaking, mops the halls and follows her everywhere.

How to play

  • Let them come to her, then Stir the one who swung: it costs her nothing, and its next save is at Disadvantage, so her Suggestion lands. Keep the Ease for Not a Hair Out of Place and for Settle, the answer to fear and charm, which from 6th covers a whole knot of allies.
  • Out of a fight, she sees the strongest passion in any face across the room: fear, desire, grief, rage, or joy. Tell the table which, and let them work out why.
  • The physicians of any hospital let her work beside them, and The Read plus Insight with Expertise makes her the best interviewer in Rumelia.
  • She tells the loudest person in the room, kindly, what he's afraid of. Decide how often she does it on purpose.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: the roster's level. Boundless Passion (6th) is what the physicians noticed; Catharsis (10th), where what she settles heals, is the book she hasn't finished.
  • College of Psyche: the passions, worked without music; she forgoes Muses. The Euphonia.md vignette for the college is her.
  • Hermit for the hospital years and the Healer feat; Blind Fighting because the wards are dark and the patients don't always know where they are.
