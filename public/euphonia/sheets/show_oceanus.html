Background

Kaiawa is a tohunga of a Waikato hapū: an expert, not only a priest, in healing, in the ways across the sea to Hawaiki, and in what the patupaiarehe want. She has seen the far side of the ocean the dead sail for, and come back each time with water on her hands that heals. When the mist is down and the patupaiarehe's flutes sound on the ridge, she keeps the pā's cooking fires going, because the flutes call the women away and fire is what the fair folk of the mist won't come through. All she wants is one misty season without a woman of her pā walking toward the flutes. The pā-building has paused in the Waikato, and she tells anyone who'll listen that a pause is not a peace. Morfudd Prys, a world away, would know her fires and her listening at once.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • The Wellspring is your best healing, and it costs no slot and no Concentration. Open it where someone is about to be Bloodied; it follows them 30 ft a turn, free, and heals every Bloodied friend in it on each turn they start there. It runs while you keep striking or casting; between fights it heals each Bloodied friend nearby once and closes. It isn't a vista: open Merriment, call the Blessed Dead, or let in Peace while it runs. Once a rest, break it on a Planar Strike as The River Rises: 2d6 to every friend you choose within 10 ft.
  • Against the patupaiarehe the fight is the flutes. The Blessed Dead's aura keeps the women near it from being Charmed; Merriment frees one who already is; Magic Circle against Fey creatures across the pā gate keeps them out for an hour. They won't come through fire, and Produce Flame keeps one in your hand.
  • Out of a fight, Water-Taken tells you which spring is fouled and from where, and the springs' spirits tell you who came by; Clean Water makes a well safe for a day. Healer lets your kit stretch the wounded's Hit Point Dice (Battle Medic).
  • Spare Favors: Surge of Life (Positive Energy, two Favors, 1d8 + 5) on whoever is going down, Peace (Elysium, two Favors, 10 Temporary Hit Points) on whoever is about to be hit (neither closes your Wellspring), Renewal (the Maelstrom) on whoever is Poisoned or Prone.

Why this build

  • Why 9th: the roster's level: a tohunga a whole hapū leans on, with Elysium and The River Rises (7th) and third-level slots for Mass Healing Word (9th). Water Finds Its Level, which lifts every Bloodied friend near the spring back above half, is 11th.
  • Hermit is the nearest 2024 background to a tohunga's long training apart: Medicine, Religion, the herbs, and Healer, which stretches a healer's kit. Human Versatile takes Magic Initiate (Druid) for Produce Flame, the fire the fair folk won't cross.
  • Hide armor's numbers for a heavy flax war cloak (medium armor: 12 + Dex, up to 2). Positive Energy at 5th, for Surge of Life and a Radiant Strike. Wisdom 20 by 8th. The keepsake is the pathway's own: one more Favor.
