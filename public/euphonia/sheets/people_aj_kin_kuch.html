Aj K'in Kuch

Human · Acolyte · Revelator 14 (Bridge) · level 14
PeopleThe AmericasNojpetén, Lake Petén ItzáThe BridgeLevel 14

The Itza kingdom at Nojpetén is the last independent Maya kingdom; its priests read the k'atun count, the cycles of the long calendar, as prophecy. In our history Nojpetén falls in 1697, twenty-nine years after 1668. The Bridge is the Revelator's straight road through the Plenum.

Temple steps at Nojpetén at dawn, the lake still. An old priest sits with a folded screenfold book across his knees. A Franciscan friar has paddled across the lake to bring the Itza the faith. The priest receives him courteously, opens the book to the right k'atun, and tells him, politely and precisely, that he has come twenty-nine years too early.

Background

An Itza priest who reads the k'atun count for the Aj Kan Ek' at Nojpetén. Friars come, and the Itza receive them courteously, and the friars go home with nothing; the gods' answer is why. He has walked a straight road through the Plenum and seen it end where it ends, and he reads the prophecy as instructions, not hope. A Spanish surveyor is marking a road toward the lake, and he wants that man never to arrive. He wants to train someone who can read the count after him, before the Spanish road reaches the lake.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • He is a patron and a mentor: the k'atun count is a calendar and a prophecy, and he wants someone to learn it.
  • The Assessor knows when a friar lies, and so does he. Receive the Spanish courteously and send them home with nothing.
  • Ethereal Wayfarer: once a day he can take the party out of the world for a while.
  • He knows when Nojpetén falls. Whether the table does is their choice.

Why this build

  • Why 14th: a lifetime reading the count, and Ethereal Wayfarer: a priest who has walked the straight road to its end.
  • The Bridge (the roster's Path of Judgment): the straight road, the record, and the verdict.
  • Acolyte and Magic Initiate for the temple; Linguist for the old script and the friars' Spanish.
