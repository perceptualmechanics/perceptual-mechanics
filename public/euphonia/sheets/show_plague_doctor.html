Ibn al-Khatib's treatise. A copy of the fourteenth-century Granadan vizier-physician's treatise on the plague, which argued from what he saw that the disease passes by contact and kept the exiled apart. It is the first book she hands a new nurse. It is real; her marginal notes against it are hers.

The 1651 notes. Her notes from Bulaq: who caught a fever twice, who was cured how, and who came through the slow way and so could nurse the rest. She has made three fair copies for Basel and sent none of them, because she wants to hand one to someone.

Background

A physician of al-Mansuri, the Cairo hospital that has taught medicine at the bedside for nearly four hundred years, who runs its plague service. In 1651 at Bulaq she noticed what Lanternhall noticed later, that a patient cured by magic catches the same fever again, and wrote it down; nobody in Europe has read it. She treats the poor free under the hospital's endowment and the beys at the price of a horse, and sees no contradiction. She and the senior physician at Edirne have disagreed about music by letter for twenty years. She finds Lanternhall's fame puzzling, and would like her notes on who catches a fever twice read by someone in Basel who will take them seriously.

How to play

  • Miasma is a heal for a whole room: one creature gets the die, everyone else in the cloud who is still standing gets 5, and all of them get Advantage on the next save. Put it where the party is standing.
  • Twice a rest, turn it gray: the enemies in it take Poison, and the ones who fail carry an infection that spreads among them, never to your allies.
  • Purge is the plague-house move: Miasma and a Utilize action, and one Medicine check treats everyone in it for the same thing.
  • Out of a fight she is a health officer: Bill of Health opens any port, The Slow Way tells her who can nurse, and Tracing finds where the fever came in.

Why this build

  • Why 10th: her roster level, and the one where the redesigned Plague Doctor comes together: Outbreak, noxious Miasma twice per rest, and a Miasma that wards the creatures it heals. Doctor's Rounds (14th) is ahead.
  • A zone healer since 2026-11-08: the Bonus Action cloud heals everyone in it a little and one creature a lot, so the build leans on Intelligence 20 (by 8th) for the per-creature number.
  • Schooled (a madrasa) and Healer by Versatile: a hospital physician who runs a plague service. Magic Initiate (Cleric) gives Bless.
  • No beak: the beaked mask is a European costume. She has a censer and a veil, as Cairo's plague physicians would.
