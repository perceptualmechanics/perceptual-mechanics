Background

He went to a pari wedding at Nowruz, in trance or in body (he isn't sure which), and ate at the feast, so he made terms. The Dreamlord kept him a guest for what he says was a night; his family says it was three years. He came back in the wedding robe with a painted glass cup in his hand and the Realm's voice in his ear, and since then he chases the way back in smoke: the bang and hashish of Isfahan's bang-houses and coffeehouses, period habits both, which open nothing and which he can't leave alone. The pari say az ma behtaran isn't flattery, it's accuracy.

How to play

  • Spirited Away first, on whatever in the room matters most, then deal with the rest while it's gone. Three times a day. A friend about to take a blow they can't survive can choose to go.
  • Cast first, then Step into the Hidden: it doesn't give Advantage, and it ends when you attack, damage, or cast. Use it to get out, or to pull the friend about to be cut down out with you.
  • Out of a fight: Manners knows a binding bargain when a Hidden folk offers one, and Hidden Tracks knows who else has been to the wedding and come back. Families of the taken find you.
  • The Fairy Cup after a Short Rest: a use of Step back, and no one charms you until morning.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: a rising figure, three years back from the wedding: Spirited Away has just arrived, which is the Dreamlord's signature, and Iron and Oaths (10th) hasn't, so he can still be Charmed by anyone but the Realm.
  • Entertainer for the +2 Charisma and the instruments; Musician because he plays at the coffeehouses and the song still carries a little of the wedding.
  • An elf, as the roster has him: the setting's pari-touched. Wood elf for Druidcraft and Pass without Trace, the garden in him.
  • Pact of the Chain for the sprite, a pari the size of a sparrow; Misty Visions because his illusions come up out of the smoke.
