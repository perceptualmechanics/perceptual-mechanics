Stanisław Gołyński

Human · Noble · Sorcerer 8 (Frost) · level 8
ShowcaseLevel 8Frost OriginPoland-Lithuania, 1668The royal election

In September Jan Kazimierz abdicated, and the Commonwealth will elect a king: every noble may ride to the field outside Warsaw and vote in person. France and the Emperor have candidates, Sweden has interests, Mutapa's factors have goods to sell, and all of them are buying the szlachta one vote at a time. A petty Mazovian noble with a famous saber and a famously cold house is being offered a great deal this winter.

Three men are waiting for him at the inn: a Frenchman with a pension, a Swede with a license, and a Mutapan factor with a brass lamp that is warm to the touch. Stanisław sits down on the bench by the stove, which frosts over under him, and puts his hands out to the fire, which leans away. "Gentlemen," he says. "Which of you has come to buy me, and which of you has brought the lamp?"

Gear

Fur-lined delia over a żupan; a karabela saber with a frost-rimed scabbard (he has no training with it now; his hands remember, his class doesn't); a quarterstaff of hazel, silver-capped, as his Arcane Focus; a leopard-skin pelisse from his hussar days; a horse and a sledge; a purse of thalers from the man he hasn't yet decided to vote for; one eagle feather from the wing he wore at Warsaw in '56, rimed white, that never thaws. Languages: Polish (in place of Common), Latin as every szlachcic speaks it, and Ruthenian from the eastern campaigns. Tools: Dice (Noble).

Rime Stone (Origin Stone, Frost; Wondrous Item, Common). Always cold, which he resents; keeps a box of food fresh, and its holder comfortable in any heat a person could survive. A Swedish agent left it as a courtesy. He keeps the sausages in it.

Background

A Mazovian of the petty szlachta: a manor, a village, two hundred years of pedigree and very little income. He rode with the hussars through the Deluge, when the Swedes overran everything and the Cossacks and Muscovites took the rest, and the cold came on him in the war winters and never left; frost grows on his saber in its scabbard. He holds that an elected king proves kings are only men, and intends to vote for whichever candidate irritates the most foreigners. The French pension tempts him a little. The Mutapan lamp tempts him a great deal, because it's warm, and nobody else has thought to offer him a warm house.

How to play

  • Rime first (3 a day, Bonus Action, a minute): 4 Temporary Hit Points (half his Sorcerer level), and whoever hits him in melee loses 10 ft of Speed. Rimed, he walks across the millpond, and ice and snow don't slow him (Hoarfrost).
  • Your Release is a stop: Freeze takes the Speed and the Reactions, and at 3 Pressure it can Restrain. Release on whoever is about to run, or about to stop your friend running.
  • Hoarfrost makes your Release spread: the ground around the target turns slick for a turn. Release where the enemy has to cross.
  • Heightened Spell is for the save that matters, the 3-Pressure Freeze or a Banishment.
  • His Mark freezes a pond or a ford solid as a Bonus Action; a Vent at 3 Pressure freezes a 40-ft Cube of it. Icehouse keeps a cellar cold for a day, once a day. In August you are the most welcome man in Poland; in a siege you are the one who keeps the food.
  • Everyone wants your vote. Let them make offers, and take notes. Nobody has yet offered you the one thing you want, which is a warm house.

Why this build

  • Roster: Stanisław Gołyński (Europe, hooks), Frost Origin, level 8, as the roster has him: Ice Storm and Icehouse, a veteran's full kit, no level 5 spells.
  • Noble (Cha +2, Str +1; Skilled) for the szlachta and a hussar's riding. Tough (human Versatile): he survived the Deluge, and he is fifty.
  • Charisma 20 by 8th: DC 16, and Discipline spares five.
  • The saber: a 2024 Sorcerer has no Martial weapons; he wears it for his rank and his past. A table that wants him to swing it can give him a level of Fighter later.
