Background

Buatu Makaya is a Vili merchant of Loango who carries copper and cloth up to Malebo Pool and speaks the language of every valley on the road, including some no one taught him. He has picked up languages ever since the hill the way other traders pick up debts, and has learned not to ask where they come from. He knows every chief's toll and every guarded crossing between the coast and the Pool, and is the best guide on the copper road for a party that wants to arrive with its manners intact. The Maloango's customs men take their share. What he wants is the valley whose language he has dreamed in since boyhood and never heard spoken. Ndona Nsimba, Ngueve Kiamenha and Dom Garcia Nsaku know him.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • On the road you are the guide. Everything Is Next Door lends you a language and a tool each Long Rest; Linguist and the road have given you six more; from a hilltop, High Places shows you a day's travel around: the tolls, the smoke, an army's dust.
  • In a fight, keep the spirit beside whoever is weakest. Hundred Hands makes every blow aimed at them worse, and Aegis on them makes the first hitter flinch.
  • Twofold Vista is your best turn: two Influences in one Bonus Action, a Favor each. Arete is one more Favor in the pool.
  • The Middle World's spirit, the Pilgrim, understands and speaks every language: for the village whose tongue even the Mountain hasn't lent you yet.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: the roster's level: a merchant with his own caravan and the copper road's best guide, with Twofold Vista, High Places and the Middle World (7th). Guest-Friendship, for a man who eats at every chief's fire, is 11th.
  • Guide, because he is the best guide on the road: Wisdom and Constitution, and Magic Initiate for Goodberry to feed the porters. Linguist at 4th for a man who picks up languages the way other traders pick up debts; Skilled for the market.
  • The Deep Ethereal at 5th, for Through the Curtain and the Mist-Walker's Ferry: a guide's plane. A quilted coat and a hide shield; the spear is his focus, so his hands are full and his Planar Strike still works. Planar Adept (1st): Guidance and Message.
