Sossi

Human · Merchant · Counsellor 5 (Factor) · level 5
PeopleWest and Central AfricaAlladaFactorLevel 5

Allada and its coast at Offra were a center of the Atlantic slave trade in the 1660s, where the king's agents sold captives to Dutch, French and English company ships. Families did sometimes ransom kin back before a ship sailed, at whatever price the factor named. Cowries were the currency.

The beach at Offra beside a company lodge, a ship riding at anchor beyond the surf. A woman in her best indigo cloth kneels on a mat and counts cowries into strings of forty, and strings into heads, and never once looks at the ship. A factor's clerk watches her from the lodge door. When she finishes she stands, ties the last string, and asks you, very calmly, whether you can talk to a captain.

Background

A market woman of Allada who has sold everything she can sell and walked to the beach at Offra to buy her brother back before the company ship sails. The factor's price went up when he saw she could pay it. She knows to the cowrie what she has, what she can borrow and from whom, and what every person between her and her brother wants. She needs someone who can talk to the captain, the king's man who sold her brother, or anyone. She will owe a party for the rest of her life, and pay it.

How to play

  • She is the patron of the roads at Offra (see Hooks): a party that helps her has a friend for life, and she will pay.
  • Assay and Everyone Has a Price: she can tell the party what the factor, the captain and the king's man each want.
  • Play her calm. She has decided not to look at the ship, and she won't.
  • The hook is her brother, before the ship sails.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: a market woman who knows the price of everything, and has spent it all on one thing.
  • Factor: she is a merchant to the bone, and this is the deal of her life.
  • Merchant and Lucky; Skilled for a lifetime in the market.
