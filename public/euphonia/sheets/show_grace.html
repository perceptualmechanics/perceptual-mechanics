The roads. Sixty pages of his commonplace book: every road from Isfahan to the frontiers, with the inns, the fords, the governors and what they drink, all from dinner conversation. He thinks of it as a hobby.

A pomegranate. There is always one in his sleeve. Hosts have learned to plant a tree.

Background

Twenty-three, a provincial vizier's son from Sabzevar, a gentleman of the shah's household hoping for a secretary's post in the royal chancery: one that comes with a seal and lets him stop being decorative. Everything he does looks easy, which is the point and the problem: the court thinks a man who makes nothing look hard has never done anything hard. He knows the roads to Tabriz and Qazvin from every envoy he has Charmed at dinner, and writes them down afterward in a fair hand. He would make an excellent spy, and hasn't yet realized he already is one.

How to play

  • At a reception he's the whole kit: a minute's Conversation (1 Ease) moves a guest one step friendlier without its noticing; The Read tells him who has the authority and who has the power; With Ease treats a Charisma roll as 13, and The Hostess lends it to a friend's clumsy toast.
  • Speak the maxim at dawn for the day you expect, aloud, so the party hears it: Know how to refuse for a negotiation, Think with the few, speak with the many for a petition, Arrive before you're needed for the road (a free First Through the Door). Whoever acts on it first that day gets the Advantage.
  • In a fight, shoot. Keep Ease for the ally whose failed save will end the evening.
  • Musician: after a rest, a ghazal on the tar gives three allies Heroic Inspiration. The court thinks it's decorative.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: the roster's level. Young and rising: Extra Attack, and the college's whole kit at 3rd. Poise Under Fire (6th) and Coming and Going are the next season at court.
  • College of Grace: the balanced center, and the only college that can be perfectly rounded. At 5th he's three arts in; he'll want the Muses and the Mysteries next.
  • Court Creature (Euphonia's background): a vizier's son raised on access, letters, and the card tables where posts are won. Musician for the ghazals; Archery for the pomegranate.
