Background

Ser Marin Zorzi is one of the three State Inquisitors of Venice, the Council of Ten's secret court, which reads the denunciations dropped into the stone lions' mouths and sits without jury, record, or appeal. A tiefling of an old patrician house, he has worn the mask in court since the day his horns came in. A Revelator who walked the paved road in spirit, all the way down to the bridge, and then crossed it in the body: Acheron has marked him. He wears the mask so that no one he sentences ever has his face to curse or scry. He has a file on Zanetta Bon, the halfling the Ten's men keep failing to catch on the rooftops at Carnival, and it is thicker every year. Whether the court is just is the question the cover leaves open.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • In court the fight is words: Zone of Truth, the Assessor at your elbow catching three deliberate lies a calling (call it again for more), The Paperwork reading every seal and pass laid on the table. Compelled Duel is for the accused who runs.
  • In a fight, Weigh whatever is hitting your friends, then stand next to whoever it hits and take the blow: that is what heals you. One tip, and the verdict is open: Found Wanting.
  • The Hush is your answer to a sorcerer in the dock. The masked face and the Blank Portrait are your answer to his friends.
  • The tiefling's darkness, once a day, puts out the room's lamps when the court needs no witnesses; hellish rebuke is a second Reaction option when nobody beside you needs Bear the Verdict. You resist fire, which matters in a city that burns its evidence.

Why this build

  • Why 15th: he sits on a court of last resort. Acheron and Found Wanting (15th) are the sentence on the cover: the accused banished to the road and back with the last minute gone. He has crossed the bridge in the body, and it marked him.
  • Scribe for Wisdom and Intelligence and a court clerk's start; Skilled for the courtroom skills. Tiefling (infernal legacy) because the mask hides the horns: Fire Resistance and darkvision suit a man who sits in a windowless room; Planar Adept (1st) takes message instead of thaumaturgy, which the species already gives. Heavy Armor Master under the robe, because Bear the Verdict means he takes the blows meant for the court.
  • Revelations: the Deep Ethereal (step behind the curtain) at 5th, and Cocytus at 13th for The Hush. Walked: Acheron, whose mark (Advantage on Concentration saves; nothing can make him attack a creature he chooses not to) is the incorruptible judge.
