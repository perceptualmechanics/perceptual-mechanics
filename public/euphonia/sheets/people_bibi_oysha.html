PeopleThe Steppe and the NorthBukharaAbjurerLevel 8

An otin is a woman teacher of Central Asia who teaches girls to read the Qur'an and the poets. The science of letters is the Islamic world's learned magic, taught in Bukhara's madrasas, which do not take girls.

The courtyard of a Naqshbandi lodge in Bukhara. A woman of fifty-two draws a square of letters on a doorframe in saffron ink, reciting as she writes, and a whole courtyard of girls recites it back. When she finishes, the doorframe is a door that nothing unwelcome will pass, and every girl in the courtyard knows how she did it.

Background

A Bukharan otin, a woman teacher, who writes the protective talismans for her father's Naqshbandi lodge and teaches the science of letters to girls the madrasas won't take. She has outlived half the scholars who said a woman couldn't do it. She is the reason the lodge's shelter holds. She wants one of her girls admitted to read in a madrasa library, once, with the door open.

How to play

  • She is a mentor who teaches anyone who will sit still. A party with a Wizard has found a teacher.
  • The lodge's shelter holds because of her. A party hiding someone in Bukhara should hide them there.
  • Projected Ward: she stands between her girls and anything.
  • The hook is the madrasa library, once, with the door open.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: thirty years of teaching, and Projected Ward.
  • Abjurer, as the roster has her: the talismans on the doorframes.
  • Scribe and Skilled for a teacher; Magic Initiate (Cleric) for the lodge.
