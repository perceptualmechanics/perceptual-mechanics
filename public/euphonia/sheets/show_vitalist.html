Mutapan Water Pump. A curiosity she wrote to the capital for and got, after a year: it lifts the fever house's spring by a hand's turn and keeps lifting for a day. Clean water upstream of the ward did more for the fever season than any spell. It drifts out of calibration (a 1 on a d6 when an adventure begins, or after hard travel or a spell's area), and the harbor's one Mhizha owes her a favor.

The sparrowhawk. Her familiar (Find Familiar, Magic Initiate), a black sparrowhawk off the mountain, using the hawk's statistics. It brings her news of the herds, and she brings it the worst of the fever house's rats.

Background

One of Mutapa's two healers at Hoerikwaggo, its town at the Cape under the mountain, who treats the town as one patient and the mountain as part of it. She trained among Mutapa's instruments in the capital and came to the Cape because no one else would, and found the whole place breathing at her: the springs, the herds, the wind. She has read Ilyanor Sael's lectures in copy. Every ship from Sofala, Batavia and Lisbon puts in at Hoerikwaggo, and when the sickness comes there will be more patients than she can reach; whom she treats first will decide what the Cape becomes. She would like a second pair of hands she doesn't have to train, and a ship from Sofala with no fever in the hold.

How to play

  • Heal freely: overhealing isn't wasted, it goes on to the next person who needs it, and every heal you give mends you a little.
  • Keep a Prowess Die for The Ground Takes It: a Reaction that takes 1d8 + 4 off a hit on anyone near you.
  • Feel for the pulse before the door opens: nothing you sensed can surprise you, and you know who's hurt worst without looking. Your Healing Prowess reaches 30 ft whether you're feeling for it or not.
  • Out of a fight, diagnose a place and treat it: a fouled spring, a sick herd, a blighted field. At the Cape, that's most of the job.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: her roster level. Continuity, The World's Pulse and The Ground Takes It are the redesigned Vitalist's core; Nothing Is Lost (10th) is ahead.
  • The 2026-11-08 Vitalist is a holistic, public-health healer: Continuity at 3rd makes every heal top her up and spill over to the next patient, so she casts the party's cheap heals and lets nothing go to waste.
  • Sage, for the capital's training; Magic Initiate (Wizard) for the familiar and a spark of Mutapan current. Skilled by Versatile, for the herds and the camps.
  • Signatures gives the Herbalism Kit, so her Physician tool is Alchemist's Supplies.
