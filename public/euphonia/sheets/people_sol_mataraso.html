Sol Mataraso

Human · Acolyte · Warlock 6 (Envoy) · level 6
PeopleThe Ottomans, Persia and the HornSalonicaEnvoyLevel 6

Salonica is the great Jewish city of the Ottoman world, where Sephardic exiles from Spain and Portugal have lived since the 1490s, each congregation keeping the name of the town it came from. In 1665 and 1666 the city went wild for Sabbatai Zevi of Smyrna, proclaimed the messiah; in September 1666 he converted to Islam before the sultan, and Salonica's congregations are still counting who believed, who still believes and who has followed him. The Envoy's angel answers a Kabbalist in Salonica (The Setting, Characters, Classes in 1668).

An upper room off the Frankish street in Salonica, the shutters closed against the afternoon, a candle burning in daylight. A woman in dark wool sits over an open book with her finger on a line of Hebrew, and she says one of the names in it aloud, carefully, the way you would say the name of a stranger you want to be sure of. The candle flame stands straight up and goes white. Something tall leans down through the ceiling as if the ceiling were water, and reads the line over her shoulder.

Her brother's last letter. From the spring, before he went to Edirne: four lines, saying he had seen the truth and she would understand one day. She keeps it in the Zohar, at the page where he stopped reading.

Background

A rabbi's widow of forty-two of the Lisbon congregation, who learned the Zohar at her father's table because she was the child who asked the questions, and kept learning after he died because nobody told her to stop. When she was thirty she said a name from her father's notebook aloud, carefully, and something answered: tall, bright and in a hurry, an angel of the ladder, which has answered her ever since when she calls and asks nothing in return. It carries her letters, it stood over her son's sickbed, and once it read a line of the Zohar over her shoulder and corrected her father's commentary. She did not believe in Sabbatai Zevi in 1666, and she said so, and lost friends. Her brother believed, and followed him into the turban in the spring, and has not written. She wants her brother to come home, whatever he believes now, and she wants to know whether the angel that answers her also answered the prophets of 1666, and what it told them.

How to play

  • The angel comes in bursts: one errand, then it goes. She calls it carefully, and only when she means to.
  • Messenger: Sending once a day without a slot, an angel bearing it, and anyone who reads a letter she signed knows whether she believed it when she wrote it. In Salonica in 1668 that is a rare and uncomfortable gift.
  • Tongue of Fire and Witch Sight: she reads Celestial, knows the messengers by name and rank, and sees what is really standing in a room.
  • She is a scholar first, and a mother. Treat her learning as serious, and she will treat yours the same way.
  • The hook is her brother. He followed the convert into the turban, and a party going to Edirne could carry a letter, or bring him back to the door.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: twelve years since the angel first answered; Messenger, and level 3 slots for the angel and Revivify.
  • Envoy: Classes in 1668, Warlock: "An Envoy's angel answers a Kabbalist in Salonica" (The Setting, Characters). Simon Horowitz is Venice's Kabbalist Envoy; they have corresponded for years about the names, and disagree about two of them.
  • Acolyte and Magic Initiate (Cleric) for a rabbi's daughter; the Pact of the Tome, because her book is her father's notebook, and the angel reads it too.
