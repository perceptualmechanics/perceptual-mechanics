Brites Fagundes

Halfling · Artisan · Courtier 4 (Art) · level 4
PeopleEuropeLisbonCollege of ArtLevel 4

Portugal's convents made the country's sweets: egg yolks left over from starching nuns' habits and clarifying wine became pastéis, ovos moles, toucinho do céu and a hundred others, sold through the convent turnstile. The Alfama is Lisbon's old Moorish quarter, a tangle of stairs and alleys below the castle.

Before dawn in the Alfama, the alleys still dark, and one doorway full of warm light and the smell of bread. A small woman with flour to the elbows slides a peel into the oven and out again without looking. Two novices in grey wait on the step for the convent's order. Behind her, a battered Mutapan box in the back room hums to itself and frosts over. She hands you a heel of yesterday's loaf, which is as good as today's, and asks if you've eaten.

Her mother's starter. A crock of sourdough older than she is. She feeds it every night. Nobody else's bread made from it comes out right.

Background

A halfling baker of the Alfama whose bread is as good on the third day as the first, and whose egg-yolk sweets the convents send novices to buy. A sailor from Sofala paid a debt with the Mutapan Cold Box in her back room, and it keeps her butter hard in August; she has never asked what it's worth. She can't say why nobody can make her mother's bread come out right, not even her. Lately a familiar of the Holy Office buys a loaf every day and takes it away uneaten, and she would very much like someone to find out why.

How to play

  • She is a host and a safe kitchen: the Alfama's back door, and a quiet thread to Mutapa.
  • Bread opens doors. A party that needs to get into a convent, or out of one, should ask her for the order.
  • Every Table means she knows who in the quarter is feeding whom. Ask, and she will tell you over a coffee.
  • The hook is the familiar and the uneaten loaf. Find out where it goes.

Why this build

  • Why 4th: a baker with a gift, not a courtier at court: her art is bread, and the Alfama is her court.
  • College of Art: a loaf can be a work of art, and she makes one every morning.
  • Artisan and Crafter for the oven; halfling, as the book has her.
