Đào Thị Lựu

Human · Artisan · Courtier 5 (Art) · level 5
PeopleEast and Southeast AsiaThang LongCollege of ArtLevel 5

Water puppetry, múa rối nước, is the art of the Red River villages: lacquered wooden puppets on long poles worked by puppeteers standing waist-deep behind a bamboo screen, so the figures seem to walk on the pond. The guilds kept their techniques secret, sworn by oath, and taught them only to sons, for fear daughters would marry out and take the secrets with them. In 1668 the Trịnh lords rule the north from Thang Long in the Lê emperor's name.

Evening at a village pond outside Thang Long, the whole village eating on the bank. Behind a split-bamboo screen a dragon rises out of the water, spits a jet of spray, and chases a fisherman across the pond while the children scream with delight. When the show ends a woman wades out from behind the screen, soaked to the chest, and two girls follow her with poles. The elders on the bank don't look at the girls. Everyone else does.

Background

The head of a water-puppet guild in a village outside Thang Long, the first woman to keep the guild's book and swear its oath, which forbids teaching the tricks to anyone who might marry out of the village. She taught them to her daughters anyway and dared the elders to say so, and none has. She works the dragon herself, waist-deep in the pond behind the screen, and her puppets do things no one else's can, which the village says is the lacquer and she says is practice. She wants her troupe to play for the Trịnh lord's court at New Year, and a Mutapan lamp to light the water.

How to play

  • She is a host, a contact and a way into court: a troupe that plays for the Trịnh lord goes where no one else goes.
  • Her shows are illusions that everyone agrees to believe. She can make a pond, or a courtyard, look like anything.
  • She broke a guild's oath for her daughters, and dared the elders to object. A party with a cause like that has a friend.
  • The hook is New Year at court, and the Mutapan lamp for the water.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: the head of a guild, with the best dragon on the Red River.
  • College of Art: water puppetry is painting, carving and theater on a pond.
  • Artisan and Crafter for the carving; Musician for the orchestra beside the pond.
