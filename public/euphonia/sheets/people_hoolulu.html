A fisherman's house in Kona, fever in the air. A man of fifty-seven kneels by the sleeping mat with a gourd of pounded leaves. Before the medicine touches the fisherman's lips he pauses and says the plant's name, and its prayer, all of it, and does not hurry for the fisherman's wife, or for the chief's man waiting at the door. Background

A kahuna lāʻau lapaʻau of Kona on Hawaiʻi Island, a healer by plants and prayer. He studied twenty years before he was let near a patient, and he thinks the apprentices these days are rushed. The chiefs fight over Hawaiʻi Island and the kapu shift with them; he has treated the warriors of three rivals and been thanked by all three. When a sickness comes that no plant answers, he goes up the mountain and asks why, and sometimes he is told. He wants an apprentice patient enough to learn a plant's prayer before its use.

How to play

  • He heals by taking, and he takes from the sickness first. Play him slow and certain.
  • Cunning Folk: once a week in Kona, one true answer about who is sick and hiding it, or what the place is afraid of.
  • In a fight Communal Pain makes the party stand together; Rip Life heals one by hurting another.
  • The hook is the mountain: when no plant answers, he goes up and asks why, and the party can go with him.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: twenty years of study and twenty more of practice. Vitals (7th) is a healer who reads two patients at once.
  • Hedge Witch, as the roster has him: the healer outside the academies.
  • Cunning Folk (Euphonia.md), the background that names the Hedge Witch as its model, and Healer: twenty years of study in the uplands.
