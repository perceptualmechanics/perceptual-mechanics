Genoa's bankers lent to the Spanish crown for a century and grew rich on it; the Casa di San Giorgio, the republic's bank, keeps its strongrooms by the harbor, and its clerks know where every coffer stands. An elemental-blood is born, not made (A Player's Companion, Species, Elemental-Blood), and the Crossing belongs to people who live by doors that aren't theirs (Life on the Border, Danae, Crossing).

In Genoa, an air elemental-blood sets the point of his knife against the wall of a banker's strongroom, and a door opens on the far side of three feet of stone. When the banker's guards come running to find the strongroom breached, there is nothing there but a draft and an empty coffer.

The ledger. Every foreclosure his house has made in ten years, copied by night: names, sums and what became of them. Half the entries have a small mark beside them. Those are the ones he has paid back.

The Lanterna stone. A chip of the lighthouse's outer wall, from the night he came down the outside in the mistral. It's always cool, and always a little lighter than it ought to be.

Background

A clerk of twenty-four in a Genoese banking house, the son of a sailmaker, with hair that won't lie flat in still air and a voice that carries farther than it should, so he speaks low. At sixteen he went up the Lanterna on a dare in a mistral and came down by the outside, and something in the Hidden noticed: walls have been doors to him ever since. By day he copies bills of exchange at a desk by the strongroom. By night he takes back what his master took: the dowries the house foreclosed on, the widow's deposit it lost, the fishermen's savings it lent to the king of Spain. He leaves the rest. Paolo Squarciafico would like to recruit him and is beginning to suspect who he is. He wants to give every last scudo back and quit before anyone counts, and he wants to fly, properly, just once, over the harbor.

How to play

  • Walls are doors to him, and to whoever he brings: Doors, Keyhole and Fellow Travellers put a crew inside a strongroom without touching the lock.
  • Back Ways: a day in a city and he knows every route between two places that avoids the watch; an hour in a building and he knows every way in and out.
  • The Old Shape: a minute as half wind, flying. He has never yet done it over the harbor in daylight.
  • He steals only what was taken. A party that wants a strongroom opened for any other reason will have to argue with him.
  • You carry a piece of the Hidden: you are a Hidden creature. A place warded with iron and salt keeps you out by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in. An invitation lasts until dawn or until it's taken back, and one given to a lodger can last the stay. A Danae's Sight or a Hidden Killer's craft knows you for what you are.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: eight years since the Lanterna, the First Cycles and the Way Back. Seams (6th) would make every warp a door for him.
  • Crossing: the Crossing method's opening vignette, the air elemental-blood in a Genoa strongroom (Life on the Border, Danae, Crossing).
  • Merchant, for a banker's clerk who knows exactly where the coffers are; the elemental-blood's air lineage for the draft he leaves behind.

Danae (Crossing)
1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't come in through an opening warded with them unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in (they bar only the openings they line, so teleportation and a Door aren't barred); a companion who is already inside can (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. Within 30 ft: see thresholds between worlds, and know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or a shape-changing effect, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 15). 5 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save. Success: it moves to the nearest unoccupied space outside, unaffected. Failure: it stays, and the warp (and its season) affect it at the start of its next turn if it is still inside. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the light half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): spring (Str save or moved 15 ft horizontally; a willing creature can teleport 15 ft instead) and summer (2d6 Fire, no save; you choose who is spared). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Long Rest: unravel one or more of your warps, in place or unused (a subclass's warps count) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 30 with every warp, 10 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Doors · A Door of Your Own · Latch · Fellow Travellers. Warps can be Doors. Bonus Action: open a linked pair on solid surfaces you can see within 60 ft (a wall, a floor, a ceiling, the side of a cart; 5-ft squares, 10-ft from 5th, and only a creature no wider fits); the pair is one warp. A creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the other, facing away from its surface; a Door sends on no one put into it any other way. A Door's space is the space in front of it. A Door can open in the floor under a willing creature, which drops through. A Door of Your Own: iron and salt bar only the openings they line, so a warded house doesn't stop you, or anyone who comes through with you, passing through a Door you open in its wall, floor, or roof; a Hidden Killer's ward on it does. Inertia: a creature that falls into a Door comes out of the other still moving, up to the distance it fell, then takes one fall's damage for the whole distance (Prone if it takes any). Keyhole: one Door can open on an unseen surface within 30 ft, beyond up to 5 ft of wall, door, floor, or ceiling; you know whether the space beyond is open. Latch: once on each of your turns, passing through a pair of your Doors no more than 30 ft apart, close them behind you and regain the warp (Doors left open for friends still cost it). Fellow Travellers: when you go through one of your Doors, up to 3 willing creatures within 5 ft of you go through with you, arriving within 5 ft of the far Door (each its one trip through your warps that turn).
3Back Ways. Stealth proficiency (or another skill, if you have Stealth). A Climb Speed equal to your Speed (35 ft), and your long jumps carry you 10 ft farther. A day in a settlement: a route between any two places in it that avoids its main streets, watch posts, and toll gates, if one exists. An hour in a building: every way in or out a person could use (doors, windows, chimneys, drains, the servants' stair), even the ones its owner has forgotten, and where its walls are thin enough for a Door.
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
