The Scottish ballad of Tam Lin tells how Janet held her lover at the crossroads on Halloween night while the fairy court turned him into every terrible shape, until he was a man again (Life on the Border, Faebreaker Barbarian). Liddesdale, on the Border, was reiver country until the crowns were united; by 1668 the Elliots and Armstrongs farm it, and the Gentry still ride the dark half.

Samhain night at the crossroads below a Liddesdale farm. The Hunt comes down the road with the taken among them on pale horses. A woman steps out of the hedge, pulls one rider from the saddle and gets both arms around him. He turns into a snake in her arms, then a bear, then a bar of red-hot iron, and she holds on through all of it, swearing steadily, until first light comes over the fell. Then he is only a man again, and he is hers, and he asks where his ewe is.

Background

A Liddesdale farmer's wife, twenty-nine. Three weeks after her husband Rob went out after a strayed ewe and didn't come back, he rode past their own farm one Samhain on a pale horse with the Hunt around him, and Grizel was waiting in the hedge. She held him through the snake, the bear and the bar of red-hot iron, and at first light he was a man again, and she has the burn scars on both forearms to show for it. Now the Border sends for her. When someone rides past on a pale horse, a boy comes running from Teviotdale or Eskdale or Redesdale over the English line, and Grizel goes out to the crossroads with a blanket. Rob comes too, and carries the blankets. She wants him to stop dreaming of the hill, and to bring home the Armstrong girl who rode past the same night, whom she couldn't reach.

How to play

  • Grab the one that matters and hold it while the party does the rest. It can't fight well and it can't get away.
  • Hold a Friend is her answer to the Hunt: take hold of the friend before the Mortal Hunter does, and Take has to go through her.
  • The Night Turns: when the night comes to win someone back, she holds them through every shape until dawn. She has done it once, and she would do it again tonight.
  • By Touch: she knows the thing left in a stolen child's place by its hand in hers, and her own people in any shape.
  • The hook is the Armstrong girl, who rode past on the same night and is still under the hill.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: The Night Turns, the night at the crossroads made a rule she has, and Bear the Burning.
  • Faebreaker: the Faebreaker chapter's opening vignette, the woman who holds a rider at the Samhain crossroads (Life on the Border, Faebreaker Barbarian).
  • Farmer and Tough for a Border farm; Grappler at 4th, because holding is the whole of it. A Jeddart staff, because the Border kept them after the reiving ended.

While you rage: Reality's Grip, you can Grapple without a free hand, holding with arm, knees and weight while your hands stay on your weapon; one creature at a time this way (Hold a Friend counts); when the Rage ends, it's released unless you have a hand free. It Can't Get Away: the creature you Grapple has Disadvantage on attack rolls (against you too) and on checks to escape; it can't teleport; changing shape or size doesn't free it; Invisible gains it nothing against you, and you always know where it is. The Grip Bites: at the start of each of your turns it takes 2d6 Force (a d6 per point of Rage Damage). It isn't Grappled: it moves with you (half your Speed unless Tiny or two sizes smaller), and the hold ends if it moves away, you let go, or you're Incapacitated. While you hold it, it has Half Cover, it can't be moved, teleported or carried off against its will unless you go too, and it has Advantage on saves against being moved, teleported or changed in shape against its will. (Euphonia)

6Bear the Burning. They'll turn me in your arms to a bar of red-hot iron. Reaction: when the creature you hold by Hold a Friend takes damage from an attack or effect, you take the damage instead (your Resistances apply); any other effect (a condition, a push) still falls on it. (Euphonia)
6The Night Turns. Hold me fast till the night is done. Hold one of the taken or swapped from dusk to dawn, without letting go, through every shape the Hidden force on it (Hold Fast, Hold a Friend, or an ordinary Grapple; no Rage and no free hand needed, and it can't escape by any means). Each shape tests you once (DC 13, higher for a great power): an adder bites (Con, Poisoned until dawn), a beast mauls (2d8 Slashing, Str half), red-hot iron burns (3d6 Fire, Con half), an eel slips (Athletics, or Disadvantage on your next save that night), something pitiable begs (Wis, or Frightened until the next shape; you can hold on Frightened). At dawn the taking or swap is broken, the Hidden's charms, curses and shape-changes on it end, and anything left in its place goes back. Let go, or be Incapacitated, and the night is lost. (Euphonia)
