Master of the Mhizha Guild, and the apprentice who turned the great artificer's discoveries into a craft anyone with the hands for it could learn. With the senior medium and the spymaster she is one of the three who share what Europe would call Mutapa's ministry of magic. Mutapa eats because of her chemists; she would like that remembered when people talk about lightning.

Entry day in the guild hall. A heavy-set woman in a chemist's leather apron stands at the lectern with a vial from every batch of the week lined up beside her, reading each new journeyman's name aloud twice. A Portuguese factor in the gallery asks, loudly, how anyone knows the vials are any good. She picks one at random, unstops it, drinks it, and reads the next name.

Background

Nyarai Soko was fourteen when she started sweeping the great artificer's floor, and she was the one who wrote down how he did things so that someone else could do them too. The workshops, the calibration, the rolls, the way a Mhizha is made out of anyone with the hands for it: that is her method, and the guild still keeps it to the letter. He has never entirely forgiven her, and she has never entirely stopped hoping he will. Her own art is the Great Work done in batches: Salt, Sulfur and Mercury in vessels lined with a gray metal no acid fouls, elixirs that do what their maker meant every time, and the green salt that dresses the plateau's fields. She wants one thing she can't order: for Simbarashe to come to one entry day and watch the names go onto the rolls.

How to play

  • You win the fight in the morning. Elixirs, the Stone, Inspiring Leader and the Charged Plate are all made or given at a rest: hand them out before anyone draws.
  • In the fight, your Bonus Action is the party's medicine chest (Salt) or its edge (Sulfur and Mercury). Your Action is the gun, or a cloud you can stand in.
  • Nigredo: the gun throws acid when lightning won't do, and Arc Chain and the Overcharge still apply. Albedo means poison barely touches you.
  • Out of a fight: Fire Assay and A Drop on the Tongue make you the most dangerous guest at any table with a poisoner at it, and Green Salt is why the plateau doesn't starve.
  • Grounded is one level off; the Workshop Coat rebuilds every device and Preparation once a day on a Short Rest.
  • The Sealed Art (17th): she can set glamour in platinum, put one measure in a device for a Bonus Action release, and one in the gun for an overcharged shot; once a day she opens a door to her own sealed room.

Why this build

  • Level 17, as the roster has her: the master of the guild, second in the art only to the man she learned it from (20th). Rubedo (14th) is the Work finished, which in Mutapa makes her a senior partner rather than a saint.
  • Sage (Int +2, Con +1; Magic Initiate) because she is the one who wrote it all down. Healer (human Versatile) for the outbreaks: Mutapa's chemists are called to every one.
  • War Caster holds her clouds; Inspiring Leader at 16th is the woman at the lectern on entry day. Intelligence 20 by 8th.
  • Studded leather and the Workshop Coat (AC 14), not medium armor: she is sixty-six and works at a bench. The Workshop Coat and the Athanor are from Euphonia.md's Mhizha Magic Items; the master of the guild would have both.
