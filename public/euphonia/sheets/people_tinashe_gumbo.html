Mutapa's Mhizha build to a standard; Pohnpei's keepers raise Nan Madol's basalt by asking the stone. Tinashe came out along the Monsoon Road to learn how, and whether the method can be bought.

The edge of a Nahnmwarki's feast house on Pohnpei, waiting for leave to see Nan Madol at night. A Mutapan in a work coat sits on a canoe hull taking notes, while an iron construct holds a sunshade over him with perfect patience. He asks every passing child the Pohnpeian word for something: hammer, wall, why. The construct, he has noticed, waits at the door before he reaches for the latch. He would like to know whether the basalt does the same.

Background

A Mutapan Mhizha who came out along the Monsoon Road to learn how Pohnpei's stones were raised, and whether the method can be bought. In Sofala a wall that takes a Shaper a week takes his crew a day, and he came here half to prove it. Instead he has watched Sehpin talk a column into a wall and is no longer sure what he came to buy. Daeng Calla carried him as far as Ternate and charged him double for the construct, which he still thinks was fair. He wants to understand why his construct waits at the door before he reaches for the latch, and whether the basalt does the same.

How to play

  • He is a rival or a contact: he came to buy a secret, and is finding it may not be for sale.
  • His construct is the whole picture: an iron figure holding a sunshade over a man taking notes. Let it do something it wasn't built to do.
  • In a fight, Arc Gun and construct together; Flare when the big one swings.
  • The hook is the stones. Whoever helps him understand what Sehpin does decides what Mutapa learns.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: a journeyman on the guild's rolls, abroad on his own. Field Workshop and Flare (7th) are a man far from any workshop.
  • Constructor, as the roster has him: a builder, come to see another kind of building.
  • Artisan and Crafter for the trade; Skilled for the questions he asks children.

Features

Mhizha (Constructor)
1Spellcasting. Mhizha spells (Intelligence; half caster) through a tool in your hand: Tinker's Tools, Artisan's Tools you're proficient with, or the Arc Gun; you must hold one to cast, and that hand can perform Somatic components. 7 prepared, changed on a Long Rest; 2 cantrips. Spell save DC 15, spell attack +7.
1Arc Gun. Firing it is the whole of your Attack action, once per turn: a ranged attack (not a weapon or spell attack) with Intelligence and your Proficiency Bonus, +7, at a creature or object within 120 ft; 3d10 + 4 Lightning. Only you can fire it. You must hold it; it takes one hand, so a Shield can go on the other arm, and you draw or stow it as part of the attack. Lost (an hour out of your possession) or destroyed: build another over a Long Rest. It began as a lightning rod: the great artificer's first instrument called the strike down safely, and his second was the same rod turned sideways.
1Tinker. Tinker's Tools and 1 minute: mend one break or tear in a nonmagical object (as Mending), or build a Tiny clockwork trinket that does one simple thing for 8 hours (3 at a time). Arc Lamp (Electrical): a trinket can be one: Bright Light 30 ft and Dim Light 30 ft beyond (or a 60-ft Cone and 60 ft beyond), no flame, works underwater, shuttered or opened as a Bonus Action. A Viennese drawing room has one on the mantel, lit day and night, because no one there will admit they don't know how to turn it off. Retuning: 10 minutes retunes a drifted Mutapan curiosity (the rate is a gold piece or two, or a favor); abroad, where no one else can, it opens doors a letter of introduction wouldn't.
1Preparations. 3 per Long Rest (the Preparations column), made in 1 hour of the rest with Tinker's Tools; they last until your next Long Rest; not magical; only you can use them. Bonus Action, thrown at a point within 30 ft, DC 15: Smoke (a 10-ft-radius Sphere Heavily Obscured until the end of your next turn), Slick (a 10-ft square of Difficult Terrain for 1 minute; Dex save or Prone when it appears, on entering, or starting there; Prone targets are for the blades, not the Arc Gun), Flash (a 5-ft-radius Sphere; Con save or Blinded until the end of its next turn).
2Devices. You know every design you qualify for. After a Long Rest, 1 hour with Tinker's Tools builds 3 devices, each a different design; any you built earlier stop working. Your Preparations are made in the same hour. A device works for whoever wears or holds it. Electrical devices aren't magic (Dispel Magic and an Antimagic Field don't touch them); the rest are magic items needing no Attunement, which stop if you die. AC 15, 5 HP; lost after an hour out of your possession and that of anyone you gave it to. Reading the Job: build for what's coming; a Mhizha's ears prick up at every hint about tomorrow.
3Constructor Spells. Always prepared: Heroism, Shield of Faith; Magic Weapon, Warding Bond.
3Construct. A companion (see Construct), friendly to you and your allies. It shares your Initiative and takes its turn right after yours, moving and using its Reaction on its own. On its turn it makes its Strike if you took the Attack action on yours; otherwise it takes the Dodge action, unless you spent a Bonus Action to command a Dash, Disengage, Help, or Utilize. If you're Incapacitated, it acts as it chooses. At 0 HP it stops working until your next Long Rest, when it regains all its HP; a lost or destroyed one you rebuild over a Long Rest. It's built light, to take the first blows meant for you: at low levels, expect it to stop in the day's first hard fight and wait for morning (from 6th, Restart brings it back once). Hands for Hire: out of a fight it works a simple, repetitive task (hauling, digging, pumping, turning a mill) for up to 8 hours unsupervised, the work of two strong laborers.
4Ability Score Improvement. At Mhizha levels 4, 8, 12, and 16 (see Feats).
5Overcharge. Once per turn on an Arc Gun hit, expend a spell slot: +2d8 Lightning, +1d8 per slot level above 1 (3d8 with a level 2 slot). The platinum doesn't mind. Everything else does.
6Field Repairs. On a Short Rest your construct, if it's working, regains half its Hit Point maximum (10). Restart: Magic action with Tinker's Tools, within 5 ft, under fire or after the fight: a stopped construct starts again with 7 Hit Points (half your Mhizha level); once per Long Rest. Loaded: throw a Preparation, or use Flare, as if standing in its space. Sentry: it doesn't sleep; while you rest it keeps watch, and wakes you with a chime only you hear if a creature it doesn't know comes within 60 ft.
7Field Workshop. On a Short Rest, regain 2 Preparations, and rebuild one device lost or destroyed since your last Long Rest (not one you destroyed or gave up on purpose).
7Flare. Reaction, when a creature you can see within 30 ft makes an attack roll (at anyone, you included): expend a Preparation and throw it at the attacker as a Flash before the roll; only the attacker is affected. Con save, DC 15, or Blinded until the end of its next turn, the triggering attack included.
