Background

The müneccimbaşı, the Ottoman chief astrologer: the empire's minister of magic, who keeps the calendars, presents the almanac, and picks the auspicious hour for an audience, a campaign, a building, or a coronation. He is an astronomer in Taqi al-Din's line, who in 1577 built a Galata observatory as fine as Tycho's, and he does it by measurement: he knows the hour to the minute because he has fixed it by the stars. That the hour he picks also works is the part that makes him the minister. The Kadızadeli preachers would like him gone; the Köprülü viziers need him.

How to play

  • He picks the hour: after a Long Rest, the astrolabe finds the day's auspicious hour, and Taking the Fix knows it to the minute. Begin the thing then.
  • Experimentum Crucis (once a day): an hour testing a hypothesis about something he has studied, then three yes-or-no questions answered truly. This is what the court thinks the stars told him.
  • In a fight he is a navigator, not a gunner: Course Correction moves the party, Reading the Currents halves the blow, and Mechanism turns the room's own light or fire on the enemy.
  • Weather Eye and Correspondence: the weather for a day, and a letter to the Republic of Letters a week.

Why this build

  • Why 13th: a minister, near the top of his profession. Experimentum Crucis (13th) is the court astrologer's real work: a test, and three true answers. Explosion (15th) and Eureka! (17th) are beyond him.
  • Natural Philosopher (Navigator), not Counsellor: the Ottoman chapter offers both. The Navigator's Taking the Fix (the local time to the minute) is literally what a chief astrologer sells, and Taqi al-Din was an astronomer. A Counsellor at court would make him a politician who reads stars; this makes him an astronomer whom politics needs.
  • Sage for Intelligence and Magic Initiate (the astrolabe now needs no attunement, so the feat is for Detect Magic and the cantrips). The Talismanic Shirt from the setting's Wonders: the court's scholars began it on a day he chose.

Features

Natural Philosopher (Navigator)
1Natural Philosophy. Study (Bonus Action, or 1 minute outside a fight): a creature, object, or feature you can see within 60 ft; no check. One creature plus one object or feature at a time; studying another replaces the earlier one of its kind. A creature stays studied 1 minute; an object or feature until you finish a Short or Long Rest. Learn one fact. A creature: its AC, which save has the lowest bonus, one Resistance, Immunity, or Vulnerability (the DM chooses; or tells you it has none), or its Speeds and special senses. An object or feature: what it's made of, rough age, how it was made, or where it's weakest. Studying what you're already studying renews it and teaches another fact. You can study the place itself (from level 3, that finds Mechanism's features). Only what can be seen, heard, or measured; never thoughts or feelings. Measure: by eye and instrument, a thing's distance, height, weight, and temperature to within a tenth, and the time of day to within a few minutes.
1Ballistics. Crossbows and firearms (Pistol, Musket, the period firearms; the stonebow is a crossbow) can use Int for attack and damage. Calculated Shot: once per turn, a crossbow or firearm hit on a creature you're studying deals +4d8 of the weapon's damage type. Weapon Mastery: the mastery property of one kind of crossbow or firearm you're proficient with, changed on a Long Rest: Hand Crossbows (his pocket pistol counts as one): Vex, so a hit carries Advantage into his next turn.
1Aim. When you study a creature on your turn, your next attack roll against it this turn has Advantage if it's a crossbow or firearm attack using your Intelligence. Move as you like before or after; restudying the same creature counts, so you can Aim every turn. Breathe out, and let the number settle.
2First Causes. A minute examining something physical, then ask the DM why it happened (or is happening): a sentence or two on the likeliest cause that can be seen, measured, or tested. Too little to go on, the DM says so and the use is kept. Never about minds or feelings. 5 uses per Long Rest.
2Instruments. Expertise with two of your tools, or with one tool and Investigation.
3Mechanism. Finding the feature: study the place (Bonus Action, or 1 minute outside a fight) and the DM names one feature you could set off (part of the place: nothing you or your allies placed, lit, or made), the laws it allows, and its reach; you're now studying it. A rich place has two or three (study again for the next); a place with none costs no action. Setting it off: Utilize action, one precise act from where you stand (or at a distance: a shot, a thrown stone, a tug on a line already rigged); choose one of the named laws. Chosen creatures in a 20-ft Cube within its reach, origin within 60 ft, save, DC 18. Fail: 6d8 and the law's effect. Success: half damage only. The feature is spent; what it leaves isn't a new one until the DM says so. 5 uses per Long Rest. Mechanics (a load, slope, hanging weight): Dex, Bludgeoning; Restrained (pinned) until the end of its next turn; rubble (Difficult Terrain). Heat (fire, furnace, powder, sealed vessel): Dex, Fire; pushed 10 ft from the feature and Disadvantage on attack rolls until the end of its next turn; flammables catch fire. Pneumatics (gas, smoke, foul air): Con, Poison; Poisoned until the end of its next turn; Heavily Obscured until the end of yours. Hydrostatics (water held back): Str, Bludgeoning; pushed 10 ft from the feature and Prone; Difficult Terrain for 1 minute. Optics (strong light and a lens, mirror, or glass of the place): Con, Radiant; Blinded until the end of its next turn.
3Plotted Course. Navigator's Tools (another tool if you have them). You and your companions can't get lost except by magic, you always know north, and a fast pace costs the group no Perception. On any plane.
3Taking the Fix. With Navigator's Tools and a clear sky, 10 minutes fixes your latitude to a few miles and the local time to the minute; on land, with a telescope and a clear night, your longitude too. An hour and a day's travel make a chart of the way you came that others can follow; you read anyone's chart and know how far to trust it.
3Course Correction. Bonus Action: call a path. Up to 5 allies within 30 ft who see or hear you can each use a Reaction to move half their Speed without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Course Correction and Reading the Currents share one supply: 5 uses (Int modifier, minimum once) per Long Rest.
3Reading the Currents. Reaction, from the supply you share with Course Correction: when a creature you can see hits an ally within 30 ft, halve that damage to the ally (round down).
5Out of the Line. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you and you aren't in Medium or Heavy armor: halve that damage (round down).
6Safe Passage. Reaction, after the roll but before you know the result: a creature within 30 ft adds +5 to a Dex save against an effect you can see.
6Drift. Speed +10 ft (included); Difficult Terrain costs you no extra movement.
6Weather Eye. You predict the weather where you are for 24 hours, and any storm, fog, or change of wind coming within that time; near the sea, the tides; on a monsoon coast, whether the season is about to turn.
7Leverage. Your Shove's save DC can use Int in place of Str. Once per round, when you or a visible ally pushes a creature, it goes 10 ft farther. With a lever, rope, or pulley, you lift, drag, or brace as a creature two sizes larger: a portcullis, a loaded cart, a boom, a door four guards are leaning on.
9Correspondence. One question by letter to the Republic of Letters (history, nature, a place, a creature, an object). Someone knows: a reply in 1d6 days with as much as they know, which the DM decides. No one knows: a letter says so. The learned can be wrong. Then not again for 7 days.
10Through the Gap. Course Correction also moves you up to your Speed, and everyone who moves can pass through hostile creatures' spaces (not end there); the movement provokes Opportunity Attacks as usual.
10Pilot. Given an hour to watch the water, you bring a ship safely into any harbor or through any strait you can see. On any ship bound your way, you work your passage as pilot, with up to five companions fed and carried.
11Conservation. Once per turn, when your attack or a feature of this class drops a creature to 0 HP, roll your Calculated Shot dice (4d8): another creature you choose within 5 ft of it takes that much damage of the same type (no action).
13Experimentum Crucis. Once per Long Rest: an hour testing a hypothesis about something you've studied, then up to three yes-or-no questions, answered truthfully ("unclear" if the test couldn't settle it; still spent). The party settles the questions together, and a companion who helped can ask one.
