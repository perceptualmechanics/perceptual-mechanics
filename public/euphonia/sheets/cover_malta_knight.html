Candia is in the last year of its 21-year siege; the Venetians are desperate, and the Knights of St John have sent galleys and men from Malta. In our history the city surrenders in September 1669. A Knight of Malta took vows of poverty, chastity and obedience and proved his noble descent to get in, and the Order still runs the great hospital at Valletta: the sworn ones are nurses too.

The mine goes up under the Sant'Andrea bastion at dawn and the breach is full of janissaries before the dust is down. He is in it with the Order's banner behind him, his sword lit like a church window, and the men on either side of him are not afraid, because standing near him they can't be. They hold the breach until noon. In the afternoon the Venetians ask him to hold another one.

Dragonborn (gold)

Dragonborn, gold ancestry (SRD 5.2.1): Medium; Speed 30 ft; Darkvision 60 ft; Resistance to Fire; Breath Weapon (in place of one attack: a 15-ft Cone or 30-ft Line, Dex save DC 13, 2d10 Fire, half on a success; 4 times per Long Rest); Draconic Flight (Bonus Action: spectral wings, Fly Speed equal to his Speed for 10 minutes, once per Long Rest). Provence keeps its dragons in its saints' lives: Saint Martha tamed the Tarasque at Tarascon. A house with dragon's blood in it is a story Provence tells proudly and Spain would put to the Inquisition. The Order's commissioners found his sixteen quarterings in order and the scales nowhere in their statutes; the Order took him anyway, and the galley slaves call him Saint George's other half.

Background

Fra Bertrand de Montclar is a younger son of a noble house of Provence, a knight of the Order's Langue of Provence, with the sixteen quarterings the Order demands and a gold dragonborn's scales, which it doesn't mention; there was no land for him at home and a great deal of it for God. He made his profession at Valletta, served his caravans on the galleys against the Barbary corsairs, and did his turn on the wards of the Sacra Infermeria, where the sick eat off silver because silver is cleaner. When the Grand Master sent men to Candia he asked to go. He has been on the walls long enough to know that the city will fall, and to know exactly how many days each breach buys, and he goes into each one anyway, because the oath doesn't ask whether the city will hold.

How to play

  • You hold the breach. AC 21, 74 Hit Points, and an aura that makes everyone near you better at every save and immune to fear and charm. Stand where the line has to hold.
  • Your breath is for the breach when it fills: one swing becomes a cone of fire across the front rank. You resist fire, which matters with grenades and burning pitch coming over the wall.
  • Sacred Weapon is a Channel Divinity you spend before the fight: ten minutes of +4 to hit is most of an assault.
  • Smite on the hit that matters, not every hit. Your slots are also Warding Bond, Prayer of Healing and Revivify, and Candia needs those more.
  • You are sworn: poverty, chastity, obedience. The Order's commanders, the Venetian proveditor and the French volunteers all give you orders. Which of them you obey is the story.
  • Out of a fight, the Infermeria taught you Medicine and the wards taught you patience. Lay on Hands is your field hospital.

Why this build

  • Level 10: Aura of Courage arrives at 10th, and Candia in its last year is a siege about fear. A veteran knight, not a commander of the Order.
  • Oath of Devotion: the classic sworn knight, and the Order's own vows. The Oyo rider on the cover takes the Oath of Glory, so the two paladins don't repeat.
  • Noble (Str +2, Cha +1; Skilled): the Order demanded proofs of nobility. Dragonborn (gold): Saint George's other half, as the galley slaves have it; the breath clears a breach, and Fire Resistance stands up to the Ottoman grenades.
  • Plate, a shield and Defense: AC 21. Strength and Charisma both reach 18 by 8th: +8 to hit, DC 16, +4 to every save in the aura.
