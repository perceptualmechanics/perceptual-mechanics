The Dreamlord is the Hidden Realm itself, and its undersea civilization lies over the deeps of the Monsoon Road, where the sounding line comes up wrong. Bakari's patron is the Realm as it lives under the sea.

The deck of a Gujarati ship off the Lamu archipelago, the monsoon steady astern. The pilot drops a sounding line, hauls it in, and looks at what is on the tallow for a long moment: not sand, not shell. "Not here," he says, to nobody the captain can see, and drops a twist of cloth with something in it over the side. The captain laughs. The pilot has stopped trying to warn captains who laugh.

The promise. What he promised in the deep, and to whom, he has never said. Over three soundings on the Monsoon Road he drops a gift without being asked. A captain who stops him finds out why.

A cowrie from the deep. Pale, too large, and always wet. He came up holding it. It hums against his chest when the sounding is wrong.

Background

A Bajuni pilot of Faza, on Pate. He went over the side in a squall at fourteen and came up three days later a mile out, breathing easily, with a promise made to someone in the deep that he still keeps. His grandmother's line has always run cool and wet-eyed, and the deep, he says, remembers where his family came from better than they do. He knows which waters take a toll, because what lives under them once took him; he leaves a gift over certain soundings without explaining. He sails for whoever's ship he is on, and for something in the deep. He wants, once, to sail a passage on which nothing below asks him for anything.

How to play

  • On a ship he is the one who knows where not to anchor. Let the party learn to listen to "not here" the hard way, once.
  • Spirited Away takes a boarder under the sea for a moment; it comes back soaked and Frightened. Step into the Hidden takes him, and a friend, out of a fight when the deck is lost.
  • Manners: he always knows when the deep's bargain is binding. Hidden Tracks: he knows who else went over the side and came back.
  • The hook is the promise. Somewhere on the Monsoon Road, the deep is going to ask him for something big, and the party will be on the ship.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: a pilot of twenty years' standing, not yet a legend: Spirited Away has come; Iron and Oaths (10th) hasn't.
  • Sailor for the pilot's life (Dexterity +2, Wisdom +1; Charisma from the array and an Ability Score Improvement). An elemental-blood of the water, as the book has him, played on the human's traits until elemental-blood traits are written: the line in his family is the deep's claim on him.
  • Invocations for a man who must pass among strangers on every ship: Mask of Many Faces; Armor of Shadows because pilots don't wear mail at sea; Misty Visions, a ship's ghost out of the spray.
