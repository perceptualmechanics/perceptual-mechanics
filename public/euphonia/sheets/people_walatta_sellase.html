Wälättä Śellase

Human · Acolyte · Cleric 4 (Light) · level 4
PeopleThe Ottomans, Persia and the HornGondarLight DomainLevel 4

A mahbär is an Ethiopian Orthodox fellowship: a circle of neighbors who meet each month on their saint's day, in turn at each member's house, to pray, eat and drink, and help any member who is in need. The twenty-first of each month is Mary's day. Tej is honey wine, and Meskel, at the end of the rains in September, is the Finding of the True Cross, celebrated with great bonfires.

The twenty-first of the month, a courtyard in Gondar. A tall widow in white pours tej from a long-necked flask for priests, masons and a däbtära who are all arguing on one bench, while a spear leans by the door with nobody to carry it. She lights a candle for Mary, and the courtyard is suddenly bright as noon, which nobody remarks on. Then she asks you, quite directly, what you are short of.

Gear

A white shamma, a cross of Gondar silver (her Holy Symbol), a long-necked tej flask and a stack of berele glasses, a krar she plays badly and loves, a basket of injera, the spear by the door. Languages: Amharic, Ge'ez (the prayers), enough Tigrinya and Oromo for the market. Tools: Brewer's Supplies (her tej), Calligrapher's Supplies (Acolyte); musical instruments: the krar, the sistrum and the kebero drum (Musician).

The spear by the door. Her elder son's, who died at the frontier. She has never moved it. Her younger son will carry it home, or someone will carry news of him.

The mahbär's list. Twelve households, their saint's-day turns, and what each is short of this month: salt, a roof, a husband, a word with the abbot. She has crossed most of it off.

Background

A widow of Gondar whose tej is the best in the city and whose turn to host the mahbär of St Mary nobody forgets. Her husband was a mason on the emperor's castle; her son carries a spear in the emperor's army and is away on campaign, and his spear by the door is his brother's, who didn't come back. She knows what every household in her quarter is short of, because she asks. Her prayers bring light, and on Mary's day her courtyard is the brightest place in Gondar. She wants her son home for Meskel.

How to play

  • She is a host and an ally, and the person to ask before you try to help anyone in Gondar: she knows what they need, and what they will refuse.
  • Her courtyard on Mary's day is a scene: priests, masons, a däbtära and whoever came to the door, all on one bench. Wäldä Mika'el is often there.
  • She doesn't fight unless the courtyard is threatened. Then the light comes on.
  • The hook is her son. Bring news of him by Meskel, or bring him.

Why this build

  • Why 4th: a pious widow whose prayers bring light, not a priest: the mahbär's heart, not its head.
  • Light Domain: Mary's candle, and a courtyard that is never dark.
  • Acolyte for a lifetime in the mahbär; Musician for the hymns and the krar.
