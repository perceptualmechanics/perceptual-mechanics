Tichaona

Danae 5 (Crossing): features through level 5
AC 13 · HP 32 · Speed 30 ft (climb 30 ft) · Spell DC 15

Who You Are

Tichaona is cheerful, light and always slightly elsewhere. He laughs on roofs and at funerals, never at people. He tells the truth because he once lived where lying was impossible, and finds it easier. Temper. Spring (hope, curiosity): he wants to see the Hunt up close, and he is sure it will go well.

At home. The thatcher's son, nineteen, who mends the roofs nobody else can reach. At nine he fell asleep by the Burnt Fig under the granite hill and was gone a year; Sekai brought him back at the spring, on the ebb, with a pot of beer and a promise. He came home with an mbira song, no fear of heights, and a piece of the hill in him. He has asked to be let in at every door in Dambarare every night for nine years.

Tonight. The town could have handed him to the officer a dozen times and didn't. Tonight he pays it back: every child in Dambarare is going to be behind iron and salt by dark, and he will put them there over the roofs if he has to.

Door. The Hidden of one court (the court under the hill) know you by sight, and will parley with you where they'd raid anyone else. Debt. Something there still wants you back: the Lady who took you sends a sunbird to your mother's door every year on the morning you came home, and this year it came a day early.

Secret. The promise Sekai made to bring him home was that he would play for the court on the night it asked. It hasn't asked. He thinks tonight it will. Sekai doesn't know he knows.

The others. Sekai brought you back; you'd walk into the hill for her. Nhamo looks at you and sees his sister. Fungai watches you, and you let her: she's the only one who ever asks you in without being asked.

How to play me. You're the roof-runner. Every turn you can bend one more piece of Dambarare: Doors in an ironed house's wall put a villager behind iron in one step, and 3 can go through with you (latch them behind you to keep the warp); a winter warp stops the rider about to reach someone (ask Sekai which riders winter bites hardest); a Tilt throws it off the roof; a Pocket hides the children. Doors carry only the willing, so they move your folk and never a rider. Denial folds a blow aside, and when anyone goes off a roof, Feather Fall makes you the net. On foot, someone still has to ask you in.

Features

Hidden Creature (Danae). You carry a piece of the Hidden in your soul, so iron and salt bind you like any rider: you can't come in through an ironed and salted opening unless someone inside who isn't Hidden asks you in. Iron bars only the openings, so your own Doors aren't barred (Doors).

Spellcasting (Danae 1). CHA full caster; you learn your spells in dreams and prepare 9 after a Long Rest; 4 cantrips.

The Sight (Danae 1). Within 30 ft, you see thresholds between worlds, and you know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or shape-change, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Proficiency in Insight and Perception; you know Sylvan.

Warps (Danae 1). Bonus Action: you bend a 5-ft or 10-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft. The warp lasts 1 minute (no Concentration). You have 5 warps per Short Rest. Each warp takes one shape: a pair of Doors, a Tilt, a Pocket (all below), or a plain patch of slow ground, where each foot of movement costs 3 ft. Any shape can also carry a season (The Half). A warp affects everyone in it alike, friend or foe, and two warps can't share any space. Once on each of your turns you can put a warp where creatures stand. Each makes a DEX save: on a success it steps out unaffected, and on a failure the warp affects it at the start of its next turn, if it's still inside. Saves against your warps use your spell save DC (15).

Denial (Danae 1). Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp (end one in place, or spend one you haven't used) for +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning the hit into a miss. The air folds, and the blow arrives somewhere you aren't.

The Half: the dark half (Danae 2). Your seasons are autumn and winter, and any warp can carry one of them. A creature meets the season when it enters the warp for the first time on a turn or starts its turn there. Autumn: WIS save, or you choose one: it's Charmed by you until the start of its next turn, or it has Disadvantage on the next save it makes before the end of your next turn. Winter: CON save when it enters (starting a turn there calls for no save); on a failure its Speed is 0 and it can't take Reactions until the start of its next turn. Only one of your warps' seasons takes a creature each turn. Tempers, tonight: every rider has a temper, one of the four seasons. A rider saves at Disadvantage against the season that matches its temper, and at Advantage against the opposite one (winter against summer, autumn against spring). Sekai knows the riders' tempers: ask her.

Flee (Danae 2). Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0): unravel one or more warps, in place or unused, and be Away for 1 minute: Temporary HP = 5 + 5 per warp unraveled; attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport up to 15 ft. You keep casting. It ends early when those Temporary HP are gone or you're Incapacitated. When it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport up to 15 ft. 1 per Short Rest (The Way Back).

Danae Subclass: Crossing (Danae 3). Your subclass. Proficiency with Thieves' Tools (a bent nail and a length of wire), which can also be your spellcasting focus.

Crossing Spells (Crossing 3). Always prepared: Arcane Lock, Feather Fall, Knock, Silence; from 5th, Gaseous Form and Nondetection. Feather Fall is a Reaction: when anyone goes off a roof, you are the net.

Doors (Crossing 3). A warp can be a pair of linked Doors on solid surfaces within 60 ft (10 ft square). Step into one of your own will and you come out of the other; nobody pushed or dropped in goes anywhere, and a Door can open under a willing creature. A Door of Your Own: a house's iron and salt don't stop you or your company coming through a Door in its wall, floor or roof, and the house stays warded; but anyone who steps into an open Door goes through, rider or goblin, so close it behind you. Inertia: fall into a Door and come out moving as far again (one fall's damage). Keyhole: open one Door unseen through up to 5 ft of wall within 30 ft. Latch: once a turn, passing through a pair within 30 ft, close it and get the warp back. Fellow Travellers: 3 willing creatures within 5 ft go with you. A creature can go through your Doors only once a turn.

Back Ways (Crossing 3). Proficiency in Stealth. Climb Speed 30 ft (your Speed); long jumps 10 ft farther (18 ft with a run-up). After a day in a settlement, you know a route between any two places in it that avoids its main streets, watch posts and toll gates. After an hour in a building, you know every way in and out (doors, windows, chimneys, drains, the hole in the thatch) and where its walls are thin enough for a Door.

Ability Score Improvement (Danae 4). CHA +1 and DEX +1 (taken).

Fae Weaving (Danae 5). After a Long Rest, with one of your Danae or Crossing tools, weave up to 3 nonmagical objects that tool could make (Medium or smaller; not food, potions or ammunition; worth up to 50 GP each). They are real and work, feel strange in the hand, and come undone at your next Long Rest. Fairy gold: any of them turns to dross when it changes hands for value. This morning: a light ladder, longer than any in Dambarare, and two more things, which you name when the DM asks in the first scene.

The First Cycles (Danae 5). Tilt: you choose which way is down inside; a creature entering it on a turn, or starting its turn there, makes a STR save or is thrown 10 ft that way and knocked Prone; a willing creature walks with your down, up a wall or under a thatch. Pocket: bigger inside than out, room for eight Medium creatures; enter by moving in, leave for 5 ft of movement; inside and outside have Total Cover from each other, and when it ends those inside spill into the nearest spaces. The Way Back: Flee returns on a Short or Long Rest.

Magic Initiate (Druid) (Origin feat (Taken)). Druidcraft, Guidance, and Speak with Animals (once per Long Rest without a slot; CHA). The dogs of Dambarare tell you things.

Human (Species). Resourceful: Heroic Inspiration after each Long Rest. Skillful: Acrobatics. Versatile: Alert (add your Proficiency Bonus to Initiative; after rolling, you can swap Initiative with a willing ally).

Token of Method (Magic item (Uncommon, attuned)). When you have no warps left, draw on the key (no action) to regain one expended warp; once per dawn.

Equipment

A knife (a Dagger) and a second Dagger; Climber's Kit (a thatcher's); Leather Armor (a thatcher's hide jerkin); Quarterstaff; Token of Method (Uncommon; attuned): a skeleton key that fits any lock once, and every lock it has fitted ever after; it fitted the customs house door when he was nine; A measure of glamour set in silver (Common magic item; property: Step), gathered at the Burnt Fig and set by Fungai, under protest; it tarnishes when used; An mbira (Musical Instrument) that plays one song nobody taught him; Mason's Tools (a hammer for dressing granite), Woodcarver's Tools, Thieves' Tools; Arcane Focus: a packet of Sekai's doorway medicine on a cord, which he wears to show Dambarare he is on its side; Explorer's Pack (Rope); Bedroll, Tinderbox, Pouch; Salt he carries for other people's doors; Traveler's Clothes, no shoes on a roof; 7 GP.
