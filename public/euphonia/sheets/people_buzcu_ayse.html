Buzcu Ayşe

Human · Merchant · Sorcerer 4 (Frost) · level 4
PeopleThe Ottomans, Persia and the HornIstanbul, by the Spice BazaarFrost OriginLevel 4

Istanbul chills its sherbet with snow: the snow-carriers cut it on Uludağ above Bursa in winter, pack it in pits under straw and bring it across the Sea of Marmara by boat all summer, and the Topkapı's confectioners and every sherbet-seller in the city buy it by the block. A Frost sorcerer in Istanbul is a fortune (A Player's Companion, More Subclasses, Frost Origin), and a guild's enemy.

A cellar by the Spice Bazaar in August, the street outside white with heat. A woman in a quilted coat sits on an overturned basket among sacks of straw, with her hands flat on a cask of water, and her breath fogs. The cask creaks, then cracks, and when she lifts the lid it is a single block of clear ice. Up the steps, a confectioner's boy from the palace is already waiting with a mule and a purse, and behind him, less patient, two men from the snow-carriers' guild.

Gear

A quilted coat she wears in August, a cellar key, a pair of iron ice-tongs and an ice-pick (as a dagger), straw and sacking, a mule called Kar (snow), a purse that is heavier than it was last year, a copper sherbet cup from her old tray that frosts over when she holds it. Languages: Turkish (in place of Common), Greek (Eminönü's boatmen), enough Arabic for the Egyptian sugar merchants. Tools: Navigator's Tools (Merchant), which she uses to read the boat schedules from Bursa so she knows when the snow is coming in and can undercut it.

The sherbet cup. Copper, dented, from the tray she sold from before. It frosts over in her hand. She drinks from it in front of the preachers' men, slowly, to make a point.

The palace order. A standing order from the Topkapı confectionery for four blocks a week through the summer, in Ferhad Usta's hand. It is worth more than the snow-carriers' whole Bursa boat. They would very much like to see it.

Background

A boatman's widow of thirty-one from Eminönü who sold sherbet from a tray until the summer her ice stopped melting and an uncle noticed before anyone else did. Now she sells the ice itself. She chills a cellar by the Spice Bazaar for a day at a time, freezes a cask before breakfast and sells it by the block to the sherbet-sellers, the coffeehouses and the palace confectioners' back door, cheaper than the snow-carriers bring it down from Uludağ and cleaner, because it was never in a pit. The snow-carriers' guild has noticed. So have the preachers, one of whom has asked in a sermon whether cold that comes from a woman's hands is lawful to drink. Her ice is the best in the city and everybody knows it. She wants a shop of her own with her name over the door, and she wants the snow-carriers to stop following her mule.

How to play

  • Your Release is a stop: Freeze takes the Speed and the Reactions, and at 3 Pressure it can hold a man where he stands.
  • The Mark: a Bonus Action and a pond, a cistern or a cask freezes solid enough to walk on. In August that is worth more than gold, and in a fight it is a floor where there wasn't one.
  • She is a businesswoman first. She sells ice, she knows every confectioner and coffeehouse in the city, and she knows who is being paid by the snow-carriers.
  • Subtle Spell: nobody sees her do it. In a city where a preacher has asked whether her ice is lawful, that matters.
  • The hook is the guild. The snow-carriers want her out of business, the preacher wants her out of the city, and the palace wants its four blocks a week.

Why this build

  • Why 4th: three summers since her ice stopped melting; her origin's whole kit (Rime, Freeze, the Mark) and two Metamagic options. Icehouse and Hoarfrost (6th) will make her rich.
  • Frost Origin: Classes in 1668, Sorcerer: "A Frost sorcerer in Istanbul sells ice for sherbet" (The Setting, Characters), and the fifth row of the Frost Origin's First Frost table (A Player's Companion).
  • Merchant and Skilled for a woman who sells by the block and reads the boat schedules; Alert, because the guild's men are always behind her.
