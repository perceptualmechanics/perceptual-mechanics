Background

What's left of a Counsellor when everything is cut away but the computation: a mind turned to murder. Matthias Kessler is a taster and apothecary's man at the Habsburg court in Vienna, paid very well to keep his masters alive, and some of the people who pay him pay him for the other thing. Most of the Hollowed were made, by a war, a house, a teacher, or one long night; he remembers the before the way you'd remember a country you once visited. Talk about boundaries before he sits at your table.

How to play

  • He wins before the fight: Taster and Cause of Death read every cup, glove, and body; The Quiet Trade makes him the apothecary's assistant nobody looks at twice.
  • In a fight, Observe, and wait for the Bloodied hit: his own blade, the dose, and The Hollow Kill rerolling every Exploit die. Five doses a day, so spend them on the hits that matter.
  • Keep the coldness in character, and agree with the table how dark he goes. He's the setting's Moriarty with bloody hands, and he still has to travel with the party.

Why this build

  • Why 14th: a legend in a trade that doesn't want one. The Hollow Kill and Unmade (14th) are the image on the cover: the kill, and the silence after it.
  • Criminal for Intelligence, Dexterity, and Alert. Dexterity to 18 for Calculation's AC (19) after Intelligence reached 20.
  • Nothing he does is magic: Vivisection is a man taking you apart out loud. Witch-finders have checked.
