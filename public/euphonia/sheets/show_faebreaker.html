Alasdair Rua MacAskill

Human · Farmer · Barbarian 6 (Faebreaker) · level 6
ShowcaseLevel 6Path of the FaebreakerSkye and the western Highlands, 1668

The Faebreakers are the ones who go in after the taken. The Hidden Killer hunts the Hunt; the Faebreaker goes after what the Hunt carried off, and holds it until the night turns, through every shape the Gentry put on it. On Skye in 1668 the clans are still counting what the wars cost them, and the Gentry still come down to the shielings in the dark half.

A shieling on the hill above the sea loch, the eve of Samhain. Everyone is singing but the red-haired man by the door, who hasn't touched his cup. When the door blows open and the light goes strange, he is across the floor before the fiddler stops, and the thing that came in for the youngest girl finds a hand on its arm it can't get out of. It turns to smoke. The hand stays where it was.

Iron and Salt (Adventuring Gear, cheap and common). A pouch of salt and the iron rings on his fingers. Ten minutes wards every opening of a room or small building (a great house: 10 minutes a floor or wing), and while every opening is warded a Hidden creature can't enter the place by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't Hidden asks it in aloud (freely, for pay, by a lie or Charmed: it counts; it admits the one asked and its company, until dawn or until taken back; a lodger's can last the stay). No save crosses a ward. An unwarned householder who hears a voice at the door makes a Wisdom save against the asker's spell save DC (DC 13 if the asker has none; Advantage while the Hidden are at the ebb) and asks it in on a failure; one told plainly what is asking succeeds unless Charmed. Moving the iron or breaking the salt ends the ward on that opening (a Utilize action for anyone who isn't Hidden; no Hidden creature can do it by hand, by a thrown object or by magic), and a new hole has no ward until someone gives it one. At a shieling on Samhain eve he does the door himself, and then sits up anyway.

Background

Alasdair Rua MacAskill, twenty-five, a cattleman of Skye. When the Gentry took his sister Mòr from the shieling, he went into the hill after her and held on through every shape they turned her into: a snake, a bar of red-hot iron, a deer that kicked his ribs in. At first light she was his sister again, and he carried her home. She hasn't said a word to him in four years, and he has never asked why. He goes back in for other families anyway, because somebody has to and his hands remember how. He wears an iron ring on every finger, and the backs of his hands carry the burn from the iron night, which he says is from a branding.

How to play

  • The grip is a choice of who. Grab the healthiest enemy, not the one about to die, and hold it while the party kills the rest. It can't fight well, can't get away, and it hurts.
  • Once it's held, stop swinging Reckless: Grappler gives you Advantage on it already, so you don't give the others Advantage on you.
  • Hold a Friend is your answer to the Hunt. When a Mortal Hunter looks at your Bloodied friend, take hold of them first: Half Cover, and Take has to go through you. Holding is your motion too: drag the rider off its horse, or the friend across the ford.
  • Bear the Burning only when you have the Hit Points. Raging, you halve the blade and the hoof; you don't halve the fire or the elf-shot.
  • Out of a fight, By Touch is the whole job: you know the thing left in a stolen child's place by its hand in yours, and you know your own people in any shape. When the night comes to win someone back, The Night Turns is yours: hold them through every shape until dawn (wrap them in the Green Mantle and the night ends at midnight).

Why this build

  • Level 6, as the roster has him: Bear the Burning, his body between his friend and the blow, is what a man who held his sister through a red-hot iron would have. Since 2026-11-10 The Night Turns is a level 6 feature, so his sister's night is now a rule he has, not only backstory. Pull Them Back comes at 10th.
  • Farmer (Str +2, Con +1; Tough): a cattleman of the shielings. Alert (human Versatile): the man at the ceilidh watching the door. Grappler at 4th (+1 Str): Advantage on everything he holds, which makes Hold Fast his offense too.
  • Broadsword and targe: the Highland kit of 1668; the targe is AC 15 with Unarmored Defense, and the Faebreaker's grip needs no free hand, so he holds with the shield arm and cuts with the other.
  • Strength 18 and Constitution 15: +7 to hit, escape DC 15, 71 Hit Points with Tough.

At dawn the taking or swap is broken, the Hidden's charms, curses and shape-changes on it end, and anything left in its place goes back. Let go, or be Incapacitated, and the night is lost.
