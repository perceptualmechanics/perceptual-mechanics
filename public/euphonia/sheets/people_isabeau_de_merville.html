The queen, Marie-Thérèse, keeps a household of her own at the Louvre and Saint-Germain, with Spanish ladies who came with her in 1660 and French ones the king chose, and its feuds over precedence, stools and who hands the queen her chemise are as fierce as any at court. Binding is the most domestic of the Danae's methods: the one sent for when a promise has to hold (Life on the Border, Danae, Binding).

At the Louvre, far from any court of the Hidden, a queen's lady-in-waiting is sent to settle a feud between two of the queen's household. She ties a knot in a loose piece of twine, and the two recalcitrant courtiers suddenly feel a profound bond between them.

The green thread. Knotted round her wrist when she was found under the oak at eleven, by a hand nobody saw. She wears it on a cord. She has never untied it, and no knife will cut it.

A list of knots. Every binding she has tied in the queen's household, with who, and what was promised. Nineteen. Two have broken. She knows exactly when, and roughly where.

Background

A lady-in-waiting to the queen, twenty-eight, of a Norman family with a good name and a bad roof, who has been sent to settle the queen's household quarrels since she was nineteen, because the quarrels she settles stay settled. She ties things. A loose bit of twine from a parcel, a ribbon from a sleeve, a thread pulled from a hem; two ladies who were at each other's throats over a stool discover they would rather die than hurt one another, and the household never learns why. She was lost for a night and a day in the forest of Saint-Germain at eleven and found with a green thread knotted round her wrist, and she has been very careful about promises ever since. The queen's confessor thinks she is a saint. The king's mistress's people think she is a spy. She wants a marriage of her own choosing, which at court is the hardest promise to tie, and she wants to know who knotted the green thread.

How to play

  • She is sent for when a promise has to hold. Handfast, outside a fight: a truce, a marriage or a contract, and each side knows when the other breaks it.
  • Knot and talk in one breath: the two who were about to draw on each other find they would rather not.
  • Reading the Season: a Bonus Action and she knows each courtier's temper and what lies under it, which at the Louvre is most of what there is to know.
  • A geis can be broken, and it hurts to break it. Most courtiers won't, which is the point.
  • You carry a piece of the Hidden: you are a Hidden creature. A place warded with iron and salt keeps you out by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in. An invitation lasts until dawn or until it's taken back, and one given to a lodger can last the stay. A Danae's Sight or a Hidden Killer's craft knows you for what you are.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: nine years settling the queen's household, Heartstrings and Handfast, and Reading the Season.
  • Binding: the Binding method's opening vignette, the queen's lady-in-waiting at the Louvre (Life on the Border, Danae, Binding).
  • Noble, for a Norman family with a good name and a bad roof; Alert for a lady who is always first to a quarrel. The dark half: autumn charms.

Features

Danae (Binding)
1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't come in through an opening warded with them unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in (they bar only the openings they line, so teleportation and a Door aren't barred); a companion who is already inside can (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. Within 30 ft: see thresholds between worlds, and know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or a shape-changing effect, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 15). 5 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save. Success: it moves to the nearest unoccupied space outside, unaffected. Failure: it stays, and the warp (and its season) affect it at the start of its next turn if it is still inside. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the dark half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): autumn (Wis save or Charmed until the start of its next turn, or Disadvantage on its next save before the end of your next turn) and winter (Con save, only on entering, or Speed 0 and no Reactions until the start of its next turn). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Long Rest: unravel one or more of your warps, in place or unused (a subclass's warps count) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 32 with every warp, 12 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Courtly Grace. Proficiency in one of Deception, Intimidation, Performance, or Persuasion; Advantage on Charisma checks against a creature you've knotted.
3Knots. Warps can be knots. Bonus Action: tie a creature within 60 ft to another creature or a fixed point within 30 ft of it and speak a geis. Each unwilling creature makes a Wis save; if all succeed, no knot (the warp is still spent); in a two-creature knot, if one succeeds the other is tied to the ground instead. A knotted unwilling creature is bound, repeats the save at the end of each of its turns, and has Disadvantage on saves against your warps, its save to end the knot included. Stay (no more than 30 ft from what it's tied to), Spare (can't target it with an attack or harmful effect), or Heed (Disadvantage on attacks against anything else, or break the geis instead). Breaking a geis, as it's about to do what the geis forbids: 2d8 + 4 Psychic first, the knot comes undone, then it acts.
3Kind Words. As part of tying a knot (once per knot), speak to one creature in it that can hear and understand you: Wis save or Frightened or Charmed (your choice) by you until the end of its next turn. At the start of any of its turns it can defy you at the geis's price, ending the condition.
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
6Heartstrings. Disadvantage on saves against Kind Words. When they succeed against a creature knotted to another creature, the other is affected too, without a save.
6Handfast. Outside combat, 10 minutes, no warp: tie a binding between willing creatures who make a promise in your presence (a marriage, a contract, an apprenticeship, a truce, a sworn friendship). Each knows when another breaks it, and roughly which way the others lie, at any distance on the plane. It holds until all agree to untie it, or you do. Cha-modifier bindings at once; a new one unties the oldest.
7Reading the Season. Bonus Action: up to 4 creatures you can see within 30 ft are read for 1 hour; the DM tells you each one's temper (one for each group of like creatures) and what lies under it. Advantage on Insight against them, and on Cha checks that play to their temper.
7Open Book. Detect Thoughts PB times per Long Rest without a slot; a creature you've read has Disadvantage on the save.
