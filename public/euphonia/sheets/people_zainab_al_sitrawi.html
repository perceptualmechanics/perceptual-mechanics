Bahrain's pearl banks are the richest in the Gulf, worked each summer by divers who go down on a stone and a rope, with a nose clip of turtle shell and a basket at the waist, forty and fifty times a day. In 1668 the islands belong to Safavid Persia, governed from Bandar Abbas and the mainland; most of the islanders are Shia Baharna, villagers, gardeners and divers, and the Arab merchants of the coast buy what they bring up.

On the beach at Bahrain, a pearl diver opens her hand toward the Gulf. Water rises clear out of the sand among the wounded, and over it drifts a pale light like the moon on a pool, which settles on the sailor who can't see and gives him back his eyes.

The seed pearls. Forty-one small pearls, one for each day of the season she was gone, which her brothers brought up and strung while they waited. She wears them on dive days.

A jar of sweet water. From the shore past the banks, which she brought back in her mouth and spat into a jar. It hasn't gone stale in four years. She hasn't drunk it.

Background

A diver's daughter from Sitra, twenty-six, who dived the pearl banks with her brothers until the summer she went down on one breath and came up somewhere else. She was gone a season. Her mother kept her place at the meal and wouldn't let anyone mourn, and in the autumn she walked out of the sea at Sitra with salt in her hair and water in her hands that healed. The boat captains won't take a woman diver now, but every boat that goes out to the banks wants her on the beach when it comes back, and the village sends for her when the well goes brackish. She has seen where the sea goes: out past the banks to a shore where the water is sweet. She wants to dive again, for herself, with her brothers, and she wants to know whether the shore she saw is the one the preachers mean.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • The Wellspring is her best healing and costs no slot: set it where someone is about to fall, and the water lifts them before they roll.
  • Water-Taken: she breathes water, swims as fast as she walks, and knows whether a well is safe and where the fouling came from. The spirits of every spring on the islands know her.
  • Out of a fight, Between Fights heals the Bloodied once a rest, and the village sends for her when the well goes brackish.
  • The hook is the far shore. She wants to know if the sweet water she saw is the one the preachers mean, and a scholar, a mujtahid or a Revelator who has been there could tell her.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: four years since the sea gave her back, the Astral and Hold the Threshold. Elysium and The River Rises (7th) are next summer.
  • The River of Life: the River of Life's opening vignette, the pearl diver on the beach at Bahrain, and the fourth row of its calling table (The Classes, Revelator, the River of Life).
  • Guide, for a diver who knows the banks by the stars; Magic Initiate (Druid) for the small things the sea taught her. Positive Energy at 5th, for Surge of Life.
