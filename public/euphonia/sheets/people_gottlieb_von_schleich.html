Background

A Freiherr from a corner of Franconia, and envoy to the Perpetual Diet at Regensburg for an imperial county so small that it shares one vote with a whole bench of counts. The Diet opened in 1663 and never closed, so Schleich has had five years with nothing to do, and he has filled them by going everywhere: every reception in Regensburg and Vienna, every wedding, every lying-in-state, every opera and every hunt, at the edge of every conversation that matters. Three courts have licensed him as a wizard, because none of them could find a reason not to. He is damp, attentive and agreeable; he is never quite rude enough to be shown the door, and he always knows which door you came in by. He writes it all down in a small green book. The table's slow discovery is that he is never wrong, and that somebody is paying for the green book: his county can't afford his coat.

How to play

  • The party meets him in every city, and he has always heard. Read the Room on entering: he has the room's measure before anyone has a glass.
  • Network: an asset in every court of the Empire. Dossiers: he can pass as anyone he has watched, and forge anyone's hand.
  • He offers help nobody asked for, and it is always good. Let him be useful for a long time before the table wonders why.
  • His wizardry is small and courtly: Comprehend Languages, Detect Magic, Illusory Script for the green book. The licenses are the point, not the spells.

Why this build

  • Why Counsellor 10 / Wizard 2: about twelfth level, as the book has him. Read the Room (Spymaster 10th) is a man at every reception; two Wizard levels are the licenses three courts couldn't find a reason to refuse.
  • Spymaster: the green book is a Network, and somebody is paying for it.
  • Noble for a Freiherr; Alert and Observant for a man who is always at the edge of the conversation, and always first to the door.
