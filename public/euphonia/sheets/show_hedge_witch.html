Passage news. What he knows about the lines: which bridge the Inca cut, which pass the Spanish picket sleeps through. He sells it to each side in exactly equal amounts, and a party that pays him gets the same. A DM can price it.

Background

A Kallawaya healer from Charazani, north of Lake Titicaca, who walks the war's roads with a mule, a woven bag of medicines, and close to a thousand plants in his head by name, use and price. He learned from his uncle, who learned from his. He treats Inca runners and Spanish muleteers alike; both armies let him through because both need him, and he sells each the other's passage news in exactly equal amounts. He takes life to give it when there's no other way, and he doesn't pretend that's free. He would like an apprentice who can remember the plants and keep his mouth shut, before he forgets either.

How to play

  • Your healing comes out of someone else. Rip Life is a Bonus Action heal that hurts an enemy for the same amount; Ill Wish on Constitution first makes it land.
  • Communal Pain turns the party's punishment into a weapon: stand together, take half of the worst hit each turn, and release the stored Pain as one large blow or one large heal.
  • He has four Prowess Dice for Rip Life and Communal Pain; Ill Wish costs none. A Short Rest returns two.
  • Out of a fight he is the best guide across the line alive: Survival, Stealth, a mule who knows the way, and Cunning Folk, which reads any charm or hex sign he finds at a shrine.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: his roster level. Communal Pain (6th) is the Hedge Witch's centerpiece; Blood Price (10th) is ahead.
  • Cunning Folk (Euphonia.md), which names the Physician's Hedge Witch as its model: Magic Initiate (Druid) for the mule, and the Herbalism Kit he would have anyway. Skilled by Versatile, for the guide.
  • Constitution 16: he stands in his own Communal Pain aura taking half of the worst hits, and holds Concentration on Bestow Curse. Mage Armor rather than leather: he is 63.
  • The Kallawaya are a living tradition (their medicine is still practiced around Charazani); his craft here is Euphonia's version, and his rites are his own.
