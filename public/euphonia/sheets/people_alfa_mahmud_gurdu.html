Alfa Mahmud Gurdu

Human · Sage · Wizard 15 (Abjurer) · level 15
PeopleWest and Central AfricaTimbuktuAbjurerLevel 15

Timbuktu's scholars practice the science of letters, the Islamic world's learned magic, Hermetic and older at it. The wards on the Gurdu family library are written in letters no Hermetic examiner can read: Abjuration, in the Wizard's rules, in another alphabet.

A family library near the Sankore mosque, in the cool before noon. A very old man in white lies on a mat with a manuscript propped on his chest, eyes shut. A student in the corner reads a line aloud. "Accusative," says the old man, without opening his eyes, and the student reads it again. The door behind you has a line of letters painted over it. You notice, a moment too late, that you can't read them, and that you can't move your foot across them either.

Gear

The walled room. Half unwalled: the family takes down one course of brick a year and catalogs what is behind it. This year's shelf holds a book that should not exist. He has not told his sons.

The borrower's receipt. For a book lent to a scholar in Djenné thirty years ago. The scholar has died; his heirs say they have no such book. He knows the book is warded. He wrote the ward.

Background

Keeper of a Timbuktu family library that has outlasted the Moroccan conquest and the Arma, and a master of the science of letters. When the Moroccans carried Timbuktu's scholars and books away, his grandfather walled up a room, and the family has been unwalling it shelf by shelf ever since. The wards on the library work in letters no Hermetic examiner can read. He would like a book fetched back from a borrower in Djenné, and will pay in knowledge, which he considers the more generous currency. He also wants a copyist with a steady hand and a good memory, which in his experience never come in the same person.

How to play

  • He is a mentor and a patron who pays in knowledge: a spell copied into a Wizard's book, a question answered by Legend Lore, a page read in an alphabet no one else can.
  • He does not get up for most things. When he does, the room learns why the Moroccans never found the walled room.
  • Spell Resistance and Mind Blank: no examiner's spell gets into his head or his library.
  • The hook is the book in Djenné, and the copyist. A Wizard with a good hand and a better memory has found a teacher.

Why this build

  • Why 15th: the master of a library that outlasted a conquest. Spell Resistance (14th) and Symbol are the wards of a lifetime.
  • Abjurer, as the roster has him: the science of letters, written as protection.
  • Sage and Magic Initiate for a scholar's family; Skilled and Observant for seventy-five years among manuscripts.
