Tanci is Suzhou's storytelling song: a long romance told over weeks in a teahouse, half sung to the pipa and sanxian, half spoken, broken off each afternoon at the best moment so the audience comes back. Suzhou is the Qing empire's city of silk, gardens, scholars and pleasure, and its people say heaven is above, and Suzhou and Hangzhou below.

Afternoon in a teahouse by the Chang Gate, every table full. A woman in a plain blue gown plays the pipa on a little stage and sings the young scholar's letter to his love, and half the room is servants who should be on errands. She reaches the line where the letter is found by the wrong person, and stops. The room groans. She smiles, sips her cold tea, and says, "Tomorrow."

Background

A Suzhou tanci singer who plays the pipa in a teahouse by the Chang Gate, and has told the same love story every afternoon for eleven weeks, so that half the city's servants arrange their errands around it. Everyone talks under the music, and she hears all of it: who is courting whom, which magistrate is being paid, which silk house is failing. She keeps most of it. She wants to sing the last song on Tiger Hill at the Mid-Autumn gathering, the one the old man sings, and the old man has not said he will stop.

How to play

  • She is a contact for anything in Suzhou: everyone talks under the music, and she hears it.
  • Enthrall and Hypnotic Pattern hold a room. A party that needs a teahouse not to notice something should ask her to sing.
  • She is the voice for the last song on Tiger Hill, if the old man ever lets go of it.
  • The hook is the old man. Find out why he won't stop, and she may not want him to.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: the best tanci singer at the Chang Gate, with an audience that rearranges its day for her.
  • College of Lore: a romance a hundred chapters long, and a city's secrets in her head.
  • Entertainer and Musician for the stage; Skilled for the listening.
