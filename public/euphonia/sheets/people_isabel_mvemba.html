Background

A Kongo noblewoman widowed at Mbwila. She swore over her husband's body to bring the king's crown and scepter home from Lisbon, and the oath took, the way oaths do here: she has been stronger, steadier and harder to refuse ever since. She lives in a borrowed house in Mbanza Soyo, far from São Salvador, and receives visitors as if nothing had fallen. She needs a crew that can pass in Lisbon, find where the crown is kept and get it out, and she is honest that what her house does with it afterward is her house's business. She prays every morning for the wisdom to use it well, and every evening for the ship. She wants to hold the crown once in São Salvador and set it on the altar where it belongs.

How to play

  • She is a patron with a sword: she will come to Lisbon herself if she must, and she will be the hardest person in the room to refuse.
  • Her auras make the party steady: no charm takes near her, and every save is better beside her.
  • Locate Object: she has seen the crown. Within range, she will know where it is.
  • Be clear with the table: her house will use the crown. She is honest about it; whether the party is comfortable with it is their question.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: three years since Mbwila, and an oath that has carried her from São Salvador to Soyo. Aura of Devotion (7th) is the steadiness people notice in her.
  • Oath of Devotion, as the roster has her: an oath to a crown and an altar.
  • Noble and Skilled for a Kongo great house; Tough because she walked out of Mbwila.
