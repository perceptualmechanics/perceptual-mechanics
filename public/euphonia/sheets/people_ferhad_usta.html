The Topkapı Palace's kitchens fed thousands a day, and the helvahane, the confectionery, was its own department, making helva, sherbets, jams, pastes and medicinal sweets. Sherbets were cooled with snow brought down from Mount Uludağ near Bursa. Pari are the Persian and Ottoman beings of beauty and air the hearthfolk of this world are kin to.

The back door of the palace confectionery at the evening prayer, steam and the smell of roses. A slender elf in a white jacket hands a cup of violet sherbet, cold as a mountain, to a porter who held the door for him that morning. Then he turns and looks at you for a long moment, the way he looks at a syrup to see whether it has set, and hands you one too.

The recipe in his head. The zülbiye helva. He has never written it down. Three viziers' cooks would pay a great deal to have it written.

A cup from the dream. He woke one morning with a cup in his hand he didn't own: pale stone, faintly warm, smelling of a flower that doesn't grow in Istanbul. He keeps it hidden in a drawer.

Background

An elf of Istanbul, second master of the confectionery in the Topkapı's kitchens, whose grandmother, the family says, was a pari. He makes the violet sherbet the palace cools with Uludağ snow, and a zülbiye helva the cooks of three viziers have tried and failed to copy. He hands a cup of sherbet every evening to whoever held a door for him that day. He wants the helva for the next Feast of the Sacrifice to be the best the sultan has ever tasted. Lately he has started to dream of a sweeter table than this world sets, and it frightens him.

How to play

  • He is a patron with the palace's ear: sweets go to every table in the Topkapı, and he knows who eats what.
  • Be kind to him, and he will remember. A party that holds a door for him gets the sherbet, and the gossip that comes with it.
  • He is a cook to protect: three viziers' kitchens want his recipe, and one of them may not ask nicely.
  • The hook is the dream and the cup. His grandmother's people may want him back, and he doesn't know if he wants to go.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: second master of the palace confectionery, with a gift for sweetness that may not be entirely of this world.
  • College of Art: sugar work is an art, and his helva is a masterpiece.
  • Artisan and Crafter for the kitchen; high elf, as the book has him, with a pari grandmother.
