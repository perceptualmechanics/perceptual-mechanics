Sympathetic Miniature, Uncommon. From the Counsellor's items (Euphonia.md): a thumbnail portrait painted from life is the strongest connection a scryer can have to its subject; holding it and concentrating for 1 minute, she knows whether the subject lives, and the direction to it. The vicereine, painted true. The only one of her likenesses she hasn't painted wrong, and the only one she'd burn the house down to keep out of anyone's hands.

The Likenesses. Five at a time (her Charisma modifier), each in a locket on a chain inside her bodice: she knows if a sitter dies, and roughly how, by the way the paint goes. Nobody scries through them but her.

Background

Twenty-seven, a Nahua cacica of a noble Xochimilco family, who paints portrait miniatures for the viceroy's court and so holds the likeness of everyone worth scrying in New Spain. The vicereine's ladies queue for her, because her miniatures catch something the sitter didn't mean to show, and three services want her sketchbooks for the same reason. She keeps her family's records in Nahuatl, as Nahua towns have kept their archives under Spain, her cases in Spanish, and her opinions to herself. The lawsuit over the Xochimilco lands has been in the Audiencia since her grandfather's time; she means to win it. And every likeness she has ever painted is a road a scryer could take, so she has begun, very quietly, painting them slightly wrong.

How to play

  • In a fight she paints the field: Highlight lights whoever the party is killing and shadows whoever is in trouble, and moves with the fight for no more Ease. Trompe-l'oeil takes the place of a stab: a wall when the charge comes and a door when it's time to go.
  • Out of one she paints people. An hour's likeness is Advantage on Insight against the sitter forever, and a scrying link only she can use.
  • Anamorphosis: what she writes or paints can be legible only to whom she chooses. Her family's case papers look like a landscape to anyone else.
  • Commissions open doors: a house that owns her work receives her as a guest. Most of the great houses of New Spain own her work.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: the roster's level. Trompe-l'oeil and Anamorphosis (6th) are a painter at the height of her craft; Perspective (10th) and the masterpiece are a career she's still building.
  • College of Art: she paints with light, which is what the vicereine's ladies queue for. She studies no Mysteries; she has enough secrets.
  • Noble for a cacica of an old house; Alert because she sees the knife before anyone else in the room does.
