Background

A Chamorro makåhna in Hagåtña, a healer and speaker to the ancestors. The ancestors' skulls in her house tell her things, and she has heard from the Carolinian navigators about every ship that has passed in her lifetime. Chief Kepuha has welcomed the Jesuits, and she respects Kepuha. She has not yet decided whether to be the priests' best friend or their worst problem, and neither, she suspects, have they. She wants to keep her daughter, who is curious about the priests, from deciding anything quickly.

How to play

  • She is a contact or a foil for the mission: curious, careful, and the person the town listens to about medicine.
  • The ancestors talk to her. Augury and Divination are them answering.
  • Every ship that has passed in her lifetime, she has heard about from the navigators. Ask her about the sea.
  • The hook is her daughter and the priests. Whatever the party does with the mission, she will be watching.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: a healer of standing in Hagåtña, with Natural Recovery and Elemental Fury.
  • Circle of the Land, as the roster has her, with the Tropical land.
  • Hermit and Healer for a makåhna's training.
