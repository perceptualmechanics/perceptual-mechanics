The Kadızadeli preachers hold that the Sufi lodges, coffee, tobacco and the talisman-makers are innovations the Prophet never knew. Here his prayers work, as the dervishes' do: God is generous to both, which each side takes as proof of something different.

Friday, a neighborhood mosque in Istanbul. A young preacher with a voice that fills the dome preaches against coffee, and fifty men nod. On the steps afterward a porter is groaning over a foot crushed under a cask; the preacher kneels, prays, and the bone sets under his hands. "Baraka!" the crowd says. The preacher stands up, visibly annoyed. "It is God," he says, "not me, and not the saint at the lodge," and walks off to carry an old dervish's shopping home for him.

Background

A young Kadızadeli preacher, honest, learned and certain that the lodges, the coffee and the talisman-makers are innovations the Prophet never knew. His prayers work, which he takes as proof he is right and the dervishes take as proof God is generous. He is not cruel: he would close the lodges, burn the talismans and send the astrologers back to their arithmetic, and carry a dervish's shopping home for him on the way. The trouble is that his followers don't always wait for the court. His mother wears a charm against the evil eye and won't take it off; he wants her, once, to listen to him.

How to play

  • Play him sincere. He believes every word, he is kind to the people he is trying to shut down, and his prayers work, which makes him impossible to dismiss.
  • His followers are the danger. A scene with him is often a scene with a crowd that has stopped listening to him.
  • As a rival he is a cleric who can heal, light and silence a room; as a foil he is the honest version of an argument the party may be on the other side of.
  • The way to him is his mother. Anyone who can get her to take off the charm, or get him to sit down and listen to why she won't, has a friend for life.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: twenty-nine, a neighborhood pulpit, not yet the court's. His prayers work, and that is new enough to frighten him a little.
  • Light Domain, as the roster has him: a preacher who wants to drive the darkness out of every corner of the city, and doesn't see the shadow he casts.
  • Acolyte and Skilled: the madrasa, and a voice trained to fill a dome.
