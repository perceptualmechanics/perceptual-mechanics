Ambuya Sekai

Cleric 5 (Hidden Domain): features through level 5
AC 16 · HP 43 · Speed 30 ft · Spell DC 15

Who You Are

Sekai talks to everything: cattle, the dead, the officer, the dark. She is kind in a way that frightens people a little, because she means it and she keeps accounts. Temper. Autumn (longing): she misses the water, and she would never go back.

At home. The *njuzu* took her under the pool below the old town at twelve and kept her seven years. Her family didn't weep, so she came home, on a reed mat with a basket of medicines, a *n'anga* and a midwife. Under the water she met the Lady's people, who came down to the pool as the *njuzu*'s guests, the Hunt's riders among them. She has delivered half of Dambarare since. The king's officer calls her old-fashioned and wants her pool for his new street.

Tonight. Shamiso was brought to bed eight days ago, and a new mother is what the Hidden like best to take, to nurse their own. Sekai delivered that baby. She is not giving it up, and she is not giving up Shamiso.

Door. Your network knows who's safe and who's informing in any district, and hides its own. Debt. The king's officer wants the pool for his new street, and you keep telling him it belongs to someone.

Secret. She knows the rider who plays for the Hunt by his true name: he was kind to her at the pool. If she speaks his name before the Hunt, he must answer her truly, and the court will know who let it slip. The court's huntsman collects for a spoken name the same night: he will come for her first. And the price she paid to bring Tichaona home was his song: he would play for the court on the night it asked.

The others. Nhamo she has known since he was a squalling thing. Fungai she trusts with the doors and nothing else. Tichaona she brought back from the hill herself, when he was ten, and she has watched him every day since for what came back with him.

How to play me. You're the go-between. Talk first: before anyone rolls Initiative, offer parley, and a minute of talk is a minute nobody is hitting you. When talk fails, the good people answer you: Turn Aside leads the riders astray (at the flood they save at Disadvantage), and Bring Back calls home friends who are down, Charmed or Taken anywhere but thin ground. Spirit Guardians keeps the foot off you; Magic Circle wants 100 GP of silver you haven't got, unless someone finds it. You know the temper of every rider of the court under the hill, from the pool: the DM tells you them when the riders come, and Tichaona needs to hear them.

Features

Spellcasting (Cleric 1 (SRD)). WIS full caster: 4 Cleric cantrips (one more from Thaumaturge) and 9 prepared spells, changed after a Long Rest; your basket of medicines is your Holy Symbol.

Divine Order (Cleric 1 (SRD)). Thaumaturge: one extra Cleric cantrip (Thaumaturgy), and add your WIS modifier (+4) to Intelligence (Arcana) and Intelligence (Religion) checks.

Channel Divinity (Cleric 2 (SRD)). 2 uses per Long Rest, one back on a Short Rest. Divine Spark: Magic action, one creature within 30 ft: it regains 1d8 + WIS HP, or takes 1d8 + WIS Necrotic or Radiant (CON save for half). Turn Undead: each Undead within 30 ft makes a WIS save or is Frightened and Incapacitated for 1 minute, ending if it takes damage. Your Hidden Domain asks (Strike a Bargain) spend the same uses.

Hidden Domain Spells (Hidden Domain 3). Always prepared: Calm Emotions, Charm Person, Lesser Restoration, Protection from Evil and Good; from 5th, Magic Circle and Remove Curse.

The Go-Between (Hidden Domain 3). The Tide: you always know it where you are, and whether a creature you can see within 30 ft is a Hidden creature. Tongue: you know Sylvan. Parley: before Initiative is rolled, call out to Hidden creatures that can hear you and offer parley under the old courtesies (iron put away, a gift held out, no names given). They hear you out unless they have a reason not to (the DM decides; the old courtesies are strong), and for up to 1 minute, while you talk, they don't attack you or anyone you speak for. It ends if you or anyone you speak for attacks, casts a spell or draws iron; once a parley ends in a fight, you can't offer those creatures another until a Long Rest. Bargains: Advantage on Insight to read a Hidden creature and on Persuasion to strike a bargain with one, and you can use WIS for that Persuasion (+7 in all). A bargain you strike binds both sides: the price is named by the DM, steeper or cheaper by the tide (at the flood, sending them away is cheap and getting someone back is dear), and whoever breaks it owes.

Strike a Bargain (Hidden Domain 3). Magic action, one use of Channel Divinity. Turn Aside: each creature of your choice you can see within 30 ft makes a WIS save (DC 15), at Disadvantage at the flood; on a failure the good people lead it astray, and it's Incapacitated until the start of your next turn. A Hidden creature that broke a bargain struck through you fails automatically. Bring Back: choose any number of creatures you can see within 30 ft at 0 HP (not dead) or Charmed or Frightened: each regains 9 HP and its Charmed and Frightened end; at the ebb, 18 HP, and you can also choose the Bloodied. You can also call back anyone Taken, whatever took them, if you are within 30 ft of the place they vanished and that place isn't thin ground: they reappear where they left and regain HP as above.

Ability Score Improvement (Cleric 4 (SRD)). WIS +1 and CON +1 (taken).

Sear Undead (Cleric 5 (SRD)). When you use Turn Undead, roll 4d8: each Undead that fails the save takes that much Radiant damage.

Magic Initiate (Druid) (Origin feat (Cunning Folk)). Druidcraft, Produce Flame, and Goodberry (once per Long Rest without a slot; WIS). The berries keep a frightened child quiet for a night.

Halfling (Species). Brave: Advantage on saves against Frightened. Halfling Nimbleness: move through the space of any creature larger than you. Luck: reroll a 1 on a D20 Test and use the new roll. Naturally Stealthy: you can take the Hide action behind a creature at least one size larger than you. You are of the forgotten folk, not a Hidden creature: iron means nothing to you.

Adder Stone (Magic item (Uncommon, attuned)). Bonus Action to raise it to your eye (it keeps that hand busy): you see Hidden creatures within 60 ft in their true forms, through any illusion, glamour or shape-change, and see them when they are Invisible. Looking through it at one of the Hidden naming a price, you see the catch in the bargain: the DM tells you how it could be kept to the letter and still go against you, or that it has none.

Equipment

Adder Stone (Uncommon; attuned): a stone from the Marodzi, holed clean through by water; she wears it on a cord; Chain Shirt (her late husband's war coat: iron rings sewn onto hide) and a Shield (a small hide shield); Club (as a Mace); Holy Symbol: the small basket of medicines she came home with from the pool; Priest's Pack (blanket, candles, a tinderbox, an alms box, 2 blocks of incense); Herbalism Kit, Healer's Kit; Book: a guild clerk's notebook in which every birth she has attended is written down; 10 Candles; Potion of Healing (Common magic item; she brewed it); A small pot of beer (a gift held out); Doorway medicines, bundled roots and leaves, enough for every door in the old town; Salt knotted into her cloth; a pocket of roasted groundnuts; Traveler's Clothes, a cotton cloth over her shoulders, and sandals; 19 GP, in gold dust and copper.
