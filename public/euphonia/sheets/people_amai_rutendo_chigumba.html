Amai Rutendo Chigumba

Human · Farmer · Counsellor 8 (Majordomo) · level 8
PeopleMutapa and the Indian OceanSofalaMajordomoLevel 8

A Shona king's granaries fed the court and, in hard years, the people: grain was tribute, and its return in famine was what a good king was for. Sadza, a stiff porridge of sorghum or millet, is the meal; "Amai" (mother) is how anyone in Sofala addresses a woman of her standing.

Dusk at the granary door in Sofala's new quarter, a line of journeymen with bowls. A broad woman with a ladle serves each one sadza and relish and says his name before he can say it; the ones who arrived this month she asks about their mothers. A guild master in good clothes comes to the end of the line with his own bowl. She serves him exactly what she served the boy in front of him, and he thanks her.

Background

Keeper of the king's granary at Sofala, who decides who eats from it and has never once asked anyone's business. Half the guild's journeymen ate from her pot in their first month in the city, and they still bring her the first of their wages; she uses it to feed the next month's. She knows every newcomer's name, where they came from and who they owe, and she has never told anyone anything. She wants the new granary's walls up before the rains and the sickness, and puts anyone who offers to help on the end of a hod.

How to play

  • She is an ally, and the best-loved person in Sofala: half the guild ate from her pot, and none of them will hear a word against her.
  • She knows every newcomer in the city. Ask her who arrived this month, and she will decide whether you need to know.
  • Crew: every journeyman who ever ate at her door.
  • The hook is the new granary. Help build it before the rains, and Sofala is the party's home.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: the king's granary-keeper, with a household that is half a city.
  • Majordomo: the granary is a household, and she is its right hand.
  • Farmer and Tough for a woman who carries her own sacks; Healer for the sickness season.
