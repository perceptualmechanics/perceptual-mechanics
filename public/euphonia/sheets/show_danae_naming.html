Background

Kathrin Fehrenbach is a Black Forest woodcarver who was taken into the forest at nine and given back at ten with the Names of things. Everything she carves holds: a gate she names stays shut, and a child she names sleeps. She speaks the giant's idiom. She knows the giant has a Name, and that carving it would make it hers to command or to unmake. She wants to carve it, and never to, and she would like someone else to decide which; nobody wants to. Ursel Hättich, Wenzel Kühn and Verena Iselin know her.

How to play

  • Name first. A Named creature can't hide from you, tells you its weakest save, and saves against your warps at Disadvantage for as long as its stave lasts; since 10th your staves last until you let them go.
  • Then Call it into the warp you want it in. Seven warps a Short Rest, with the knives.
  • Rename is the fine work: a door named a wall, a sword named a feather, a barn named your home. Once a Short Rest, a minute at a time.
  • Unmake is 14th, two levels on: the stave burned and the world forgetting what the thing is. That is the carving she is afraid of. Whether she ever cuts the giant's Name is the campaign's question, and not hers to answer alone.
  • You carry a piece of the Hidden: you are a Hidden creature. A place warded with iron and salt keeps you out by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in. An invitation lasts until dawn or until it's taken back, and one given to a lodger can last the stay. A Danae's Sight or a Hidden Killer's craft knows you for what you are.

Why this build

  • Why 12th: the roster's level. Rename and Lasting Staves (10th) are the woman whose gates stay shut; the Middle Cycles (11th) give her all four seasons. Unmake, the thing she fears, is 14th: the roster keeps her short of it, and so does this sheet.
  • Hermit for Charisma and Constitution and a life at a bench above the village; Crafter, the human's second origin feat, for a carver; Resilient for Concentration.
  • Woodcarver's Tools are Naming's own tool, and hers are Wyrdwright's: +1 to her DCs, a seventh warp, and Fae Weaving up to 240 GP an object.

Features

Danae (Naming)
1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't enter a place warded with them by any means, teleportation included (three Danae features are exceptions, and each says so), unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in; a companion who is already inside can (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. Within 30 ft: see thresholds between worlds, and know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or a shape-changing effect, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 18). 7 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save. Success: it moves to the nearest unoccupied space outside, unaffected. Failure: it stays, and the warp (and its season) affect it at the start of its next turn if it is still inside. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +4 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the dark half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): autumn (Wis save or Charmed until the start of its next turn, or Disadvantage on its next save before the end of your next turn) and winter (Con save, only on entering, or Speed 0 and no Reactions until the start of its next turn). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Short or Long Rest: unravel one or more of your warps, in place or unused (a subclass's warps count) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 47 with every warp, 17 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Define. Bonus Action, no warp: define a creature, object, or place within 60 ft, cutting its Name into a stave (not a warp: it can't be unraveled). A stave lasts until a Long Rest or you release it; keep up to 5 (cutting another releases the oldest). Named creature: you know its type, Resistances, Immunities, Vulnerabilities, and weakest save; it can't hide from you, and Invisible doesn't help it. Cha save, or Disadvantage on saves against your warps (Call included) while the stave lasts; on a success you still learn all that, but can't Name it again until a Long Rest. Named object (one you've held, one at a time): Bonus Action, summon it to your hand from anywhere on the plane unless someone holds or wears it. Named place: you always know its direction and distance. Ask the Name: PB times per Long Rest, one question about what you Named, answered truly in a few words or an image; once per thing per Long Rest.
3Call. Bonus Action, expending a warp, with a warp of yours in place within 60 ft: speak the Name of a Named creature you can see and choose that warp; Cha save (a willing creature can fail) or it is teleported into the warp's space.
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
6Quick Cut. When you cut a Name, make a warp in the same Bonus Action (a warp for it, as usual).
6Wrong Name. You know when a creature tells you a false name, for itself or for a person, place, or thing you can see.
7Reading the Season. Bonus Action: up to 5 creatures you can see within 30 ft are read for 1 hour; the DM tells you each one's temper (one for each group of like creatures) and what lies under it. Advantage on Insight against them, and on Cha checks that play to their temper.
7Open Book. Detect Thoughts PB times per Long Rest without a slot; a creature you've read has Disadvantage on the save.
9The Door Ajar. While Away, each Flicker can leave a free warp (any shape, a season you know) in the space you left.
9Thresholds. Spend 1 minute at a threshold you can see with The Sight to open it: Plane Shift without a slot or Material component, for you and up to eight willing creatures, only to or from the Hidden, arriving at the threshold's other side. Once per Long Rest.
9Loop. New shape: a creature trying to leave by any means makes a Wis save or steps back in from the opposite side, losing the movement (or the slot) it spent, and can't try that Loop again until its next turn; one that succeeds isn't affected by that Loop again.
10Rename. Magic action, once per Short or Long Rest, 1 minute (Cha save for a creature or a held object): a creature's Kind for detection and wards (a Hidden creature can be named not Hidden, passing iron and salt and other wards against the Hidden), an object's Purpose (Shut, Open, Harmless, or Plain; never magic, a costly component, or treasure), a place as a Threshold to the Hidden (10 minutes) or your Home (uninvited creatures can't enter). Nothing else changes.
10Lasting Staves. Your staves no longer end on a Long Rest; they last until you release them, and a Named creature's Disadvantage lasts as long as its stave.
11The Middle Cycles. You know all four seasons (still one per warp).
