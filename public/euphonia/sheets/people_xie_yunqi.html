Xie Yunqi

Human · Noble · Warlock 4 (Dreamlord) · level 4
PeopleEast and Southeast AsiaThe Siming hills, ZhejiangDreamlordLevel 4

The Qing required every man to shave his forehead and wear the queue; Ming loyalists who refused went into the hills, and the Siming hills above Ningbo held out longer than most. Many scholars of the gentry would not sit the new dynasty's examinations. In Chinese stories a fox spirit is often a scholar's guest, and a good poet.

A borrowed hut in the Siming hills, past midnight, one lamp. A young scholar in a robe gone thin at the elbows trades lines of poetry with a stranger, and loses. The stranger's cup never empties, and his shadow on the wall has a tail. At dawn the stranger is gone, the cup is full again, and behind the hut there is a road that wasn't there yesterday. The scholar picks up his brush and writes the couplet down before he forgets it.

The cup. Plain glazed earthenware, left behind by the stranger. Whatever was in it last, it is full of again at dawn. Xie drinks tea from it, carefully.

The couplet. The stranger's last line, written down at dawn on the back of a letter from his father. He has never managed to cap it.

Background

The son of a gentry family of Ningbo whose father took the queue to keep the family alive and sat the new dynasty's examinations. Xie went up into the hills with three books and a brush and refused. He was cold, hungry and writing bad poetry when a stranger knocked after dark and asked to share the lamp, and capped every couplet better than he could. In the morning there was a road behind the hut. Xie leads people down it now, whom the Dreamlord sends: a deserter from a bannerman's garrison, a bride running from a marriage, a magistrate who couldn't sleep for what he had signed. Under the hill they eat well and nobody asks for a queue. Some of them come back. He has to decide, each time, whether he is rescuing them or keeping them, and he has noticed he is getting worse at coming back himself.

How to play

  • He is a door out. Step into the Hidden takes him and a friend out of most trouble, and the road behind the hut takes people out of the world.
  • Mask of Many Faces is how a man who won't wear the queue gets through a Qing checkpoint: he wears it for an hour.
  • Manners: he knows when a bargain under the hill is binding, which is usually. He reads the guest-law the way he reads the Odes.
  • Play him as a good host and a worse poet who is improving, in hills full of people with reasons to hide.
  • The hook is the road. Someone the party needs has gone down it, and Xie has to decide whether to bring them back.

Why this build

  • Why 4th: a year since the night of the couplets. Spirited Away (6th) hasn't come yet.
  • Dreamlord: the Dreamlord chapter's opening vignette, the young scholar in the loyalists' hills and the stranger whose shadow has a tail (Life on the Border, Dreamlord Warlock).
  • Noble for a gentry son; Skilled for the brush and the poems; Alert for a fugitive.
