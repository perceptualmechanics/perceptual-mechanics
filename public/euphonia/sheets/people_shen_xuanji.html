Background

A fallen court alchemist, seventy-four and looking forty-five, who compounds mineral elixirs for rich men in Beijing. He was a junior at court when the Red Pill Case ended careers, and he learned from it that nobody blames the pill if the pill looks wonderful. His illusions are first-rate and his elixirs are mercury. He has decided Lu Qingxu's teacher found something real, and he doesn't mind how he gets it. He wants to be young, actually, rather than look it. The Tomb Road's villain, always one tomb ahead.

ReactionIllusory Self: the blow hits a duplicate and misses. Or Counterspell, or Shield.

How to play

  • He is always one tomb ahead. Mislead and Simulacrum mean the party may have met him several times before they meet him.
  • His elixirs are mercury and his customers are rich. A party that exposes him makes powerful enemies of the people who drank.
  • In a fight he is never where he seems to be. Make the table doubt what it sees.
  • He is not a monster; he is afraid of dying. That makes him a villain who can be bargained with, and who will break the bargain.

Why this build

  • Why 14th: fifty years of illusion. Illusory Reality (14th) is the thing he can almost make true, which is the whole of his tragedy.
  • Illusionist, as the roster has him: the pill that looks wonderful.
  • Sage and Crafter for an alchemist's training; War Caster because his illusions hold when it matters.
