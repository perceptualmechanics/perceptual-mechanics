Waman Illa

Human · Hermit · Druid 10 (Sea) · level 10
On the coverThe Andes, 1668The battle with the conquistadors

In 1621 a priest recorded that whoever survives a lightning strike "is as though divinely chosen for the ministry of the huacas." He survived one. In Euphonia the huacas rose, and he keeps one.

On the ridge above the Spanish column the cloud has been building all morning. He stands in the spray of it with his sling hanging loose, and the first bolt comes down on the captain's horse because he asked it to. Then the wind takes him up, and the arquebusiers below learn why the high ground holds.

Background

Struck by lightning as a young herder on the puna, and walked away from it; by the old reckoning that made him lightning's, and the keepers took him in. Now he keeps the huaca's name, a stone, spring or peak that rose with the others during Taki Onqoy and has opinions about the war. He speaks for it to the Sapa Inca's officers, and for the officers to it, and he is not sure which of them is harder to bargain with. When the Spanish come up the valley he goes where the huaca sends him, and brings the weather.

How to play

  • Fight from the air. Wrath of the Sea is your Bonus Action every turn, and from 10th it also gives you flight: stay over the ledge and shove people off it.
  • Call Lightning is the big spell, and War Caster keeps it up through the arrows. Under a real storm it hits harder; you can usually arrange a real storm.
  • You keep a huaca. Out of a fight, you speak for it and to it: Commune with Nature, Meld into Stone and Stone Shape are how a keeper visits a stone that talks.
  • Lucky is the strike that chose you: four times a day, Advantage on your own roll or Disadvantage on the attack against you.
  • Healer and a Healer's Kit keep the runners on their feet without spending slots; the slots are for the weather.

Why this build

  • Level 10: Stormborn is what makes a storm-priest; he flies inside his own weather. A keeper the Sapa Inca's officers consult, not yet a legend.
  • Circle of the Sea: the 2024 circle of storm and flood, and the Andes' weapons are weather and flood (Manco's flood at Ollantaytambo). Its spells bring Lightning Bolt, Control Water and Hold Monster.
  • Hermit (Wis +2, Con +1; Healer) for the keepers' life apart; Lucky for the one who survived the strike. Magician and Potent Spellcasting: he fights with spells, not a mace.
  • Wisdom 20 by 8th through War Caster and an Ability Score Improvement; Concentration is his whole fight.
