Juana de la Luz Quispe

Human · Acolyte · Sorcerer 6 (Light) · level 6
ShowcaseLevel 6Light OriginLima, 1668The viceroyalty at war

This year Rome beatified Rosa of Lima, the city's own Dominican tertiary, and Lima is full of beatas, laywomen in religious dress who live by prayer and good works under no convent's rule. The Holy Office of Lima has always watched them for alumbradas, the "illuminated," who claim the light comes to them directly. With the Sapa Inca ruling from Cusco again and the viceroy's war going badly, the inquisitors are watching everyone a little harder. A beata who actually shines is a problem the tribunal has not been asked to solve before.

The notary of the Holy Office brings her the letter at dusk, as he always does, and stands with his back to her, as he always does, so he can say afterward that he didn't see. She takes off her veil. The gold comes up in her eyes, and the scraped-away lines come up out of the paper underneath the ones a forger wrote over them, and she reads him the name that was there first. It is his superior's.

Gear

A beata's habit of undyed wool, and a veil she wears low over her eyes; a rosary of Huamanga stone as her Arcane Focus; a Healer's Kit, well used; Calligrapher's Supplies and a reading glass; a small breviary; the stub of a candle from the winter she read every night without one; she has never lit it, and she has never let it out of her pocket. Languages: Spanish (in place of Common), Quechua from her mother, and Latin from the breviary. Tools: Calligrapher's Supplies (Acolyte).

Sunstone (Origin Stone, Light; Wondrous Item, Common; A Player's Companion). A yellow stone that sheds Dim Light in a 10-ft radius on command; through it she can find the sun on a clouded day. Lima has eight months of grey sky. She uses it more than she'd like to admit.

Background

The daughter of a Spanish chandler and a Quechua mother from Jauja, born in a room with the shutters closed where, her mother says, the midwife didn't need a lamp. Her father's candles stopped selling in their own house, which was the family's first sign. She took the habit of a beata at seventeen, nursed the sick at the hospital of Santa Ana, and was careful for ten years. Then, at a deathbed, the room lit up, and the family still argues about what it meant. The Holy Office has opened a file on her as an alumbrada. Meanwhile the same tribunal's notary brings her erased documents to read, because nobody in Lima reads a palimpsest as she does, and the archbishop's visitor of idolatries would like her to look at the quipus his men take from the sierra. She would like to know which of them means to burn her, and is trying not to find out by the light.

How to play

  • Illuminate is your Release, and it climbs: whatever you light can't hide, the next attack on it has Advantage at 1 Pressure, every attack at 2, and at 3 it may be Blinded. Release on what the party most needs to hit.
  • Nothing Hidden sees the Invisible within 10 ft whenever your Pressure is 1 or more, which is almost always in a fight. Undead and Fiends hate you, with reason.
  • Blaze when they close on you: the glare spoils a second swing, and the light is a road. Across the cloister into the lamplit nave, or onto the sunlit roof, in one step.
  • Palimpsest is your living: scraped letters, washed ledgers, forged wills. Everyone in Lima with a document to hide has a reason to fear you, and a reason to hire you first.
  • You make a poor liar; don't try. Persuasion, Insight and the truth are your tools, and Subtle Spell is how you shine without the tribunal seeing.
  • You are a healer before you are a weapon. Cure Wounds, Guidance and Battle Medic keep the party going without your slots.

Why this build

  • Light Origin: the text's churches deciding whether a girl with gold in her eyes is a saint, a fraud or a witch; in Lima, the year of Rosa's beatification, with the Holy Office watching the beatas for alumbradas, that question is a file with her name on it.
  • Level 6: Nothing Hidden and Palimpsest come at 6th, and Palimpsest is why the tribunal's own notary comes to her.
  • Acolyte (Cha +2, Wis +1; Magic Initiate (Cleric)) for the beata's life; Healer (human Versatile) for the hospital of Santa Ana.
  • New character: no roster row has a Light sorcerer, so she is invented: Juana de la Luz Quispe, her father's Christian name and her mother's Quechua one. Rosa of Lima appears only as the year's event, with no words given her.
