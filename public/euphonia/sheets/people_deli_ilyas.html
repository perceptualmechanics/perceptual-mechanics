Deli İlyas

PeopleThe Ottomans, Persia and the HornThe Bosnian frontierPath of the Wild HeartLevel 5

The delis, the mad ones, are the Ottoman frontier's volunteer light horse: scouts and raiders in the service of the beylerbeys of Rumelia and Bosnia, who ride ahead of the army in leopard and wolf skins, with eagle feathers fixed to their shields and caps. Since the peace of Vasvár in 1664 the Habsburg border is quiet on paper; the Venetian border in Dalmatia, with the war on Crete still going, is not.

A ford on the Una at dawn, mist on the water. A rider comes out of it alone in a leopard skin, with an eagle's wing fixed to his shield and a lance across his saddle, and stops in the middle of the river to look at you. Then he grins, lifts the lance in a salute you don't understand, and asks in three languages whether anyone here has seen the Venetian patrol, because he has been looking for it since Tuesday and is starting to take it personally.

The leopard skin. His grandfather's, from the long war of the 1590s, patched at the shoulder where a Habsburg pike went through it and his grandfather. He wears it at the front, where it can be seen.

The eagle wing. New every spring, from an eagle he climbs for himself. The old ones hang in his mother's house. She has started to run out of wall.

Background

A deli of the Bosnian frontier, twenty-six, who rides scout for the pasha of Bosnia along the Venetian border in Dalmatia and the quiet Habsburg one north of it. The name means mad, and he has earned it twice: once at nineteen, when he rode alone into a Morlach camp to bring back his cousin, and once at twenty-three, when he did it again for the same cousin. He wears the leopard skin his grandfather wore, and the eagle wing on his shield is new every spring. He laughs in a fight, sings on the march and is unfailingly polite to women, horses and old men, in that order. He is not cruel, and he does not burn villages, which makes him a poor raider and a good scout. He wants to be sent to Candia before the siege ends, so he can say he was there, and his mother wants him married before he is.

How to play

  • He is a scout first: Stealth, Survival, Perception and a horse that talks to him. He knows where the Venetian patrol is, or will by Tuesday.
  • In a fight he rides in laughing. The Wolf makes the men beside him better; the Eagle gets him out again.
  • He is unfailingly polite. Play the courtesy and the madness as the same thing: both are about not being afraid.
  • The hook is Candia. A party going to the siege can take him along, and he will be grateful for the rest of his life, which may not be long.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: seven years on the frontier, Extra Attack and Fast Movement.
  • Path of the Wild Heart on his sheet, for the leopard skin and the eagle wing; his Reading starts him as a Berserker, the path the site's builder offers. Classes in 1668, Barbarian: "the Ottoman deli scouts in leopard skins" (The Setting, Characters).
  • Soldier and Savage Attacker for a frontier horseman; Alert, because scouts who aren't don't come back.
