The articles. His copy of the ship's articles: a surgeon's share and a half, and the schedule that pays 600 pieces of eight for a right arm, 500 for a left, and 100 for an eye or a finger. He has saved more of each than the schedule has paid for, and the crew knows it.

The Hall's note. Lanternhall's promissory note for his fees, folded small inside his spellbook, the sum, which the Hall's bursar knows to the batzen. Every case note he sends to Basel has a line at the end about the debt, and none of them has a payment in it.

Camp Physic, buccaneer style. When the crew makes camp ashore, he sites it: the boucan downwind, the water upstream, the sick under their own sailcloth. The crew has Advantage on saves against fevers they'd catch there, and they think it's the soap.

Background

A dwarf of Cornwall's old mines who walked to Basel, trained at Lanternhall under Ilyanor Sael, and rode two seasons with its Field Service before he signed articles with the Brethren of the Coast at Port Royal as ship's surgeon, for the pay and the sea air. He left Basel owing the Hall for his fees and would like to pay it back before the Hall sends someone to collect in person. Meanwhile he writes careful case notes in Latin for the Library of Cases and sends them east by any ship going, as a sort of apology. He is the only man in Port Royal who washes his hands, and it has made him a figure of superstitious respect.

How to play

  • The bow is the medicine chest. One shot a turn heals someone 150 ft off without a roll; the other is just an arrow. Plague arrows go on the enemy's healer or the thing that regenerates.
  • Covering Shot is the reason to keep a Prowess Die back (he has four, two back on a Short Rest): the Reaction heal brings a downed ally up with 7 Temporary Hit Points.
  • Ashore, Camp Physic keeps the crew alive between fights, and a dwarf's Poison Resistance and Darkvision make him the one who goes into the hold.
  • Water Breathing is prepared because this is a ship. So is Death Ward, for the captain.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: his roster level. Extra Attack and Covering Shot (6th) make the Combat Medic; Field Pharmacology (10th) is ahead.
  • Sage, for a Lanternhall man, and Magic Initiate for the hand-washing cantrip and Shield. A dwarf, as the roster has him: Dwarven Toughness and Poison Resistance suit a ship's surgeon.
  • Longbow, not the pistol, for medicated shots: the pistol's Loading allows one shot an action, and its 30-ft range is too short for an Arrow of Healing. The pistol's there for flavor and Vex. Masteries: Longbow (Slow) and Pistol (Vex).
  • No shield (the bow takes both hands); the Ward in Euphonia.md is the item that fixes that, later.
