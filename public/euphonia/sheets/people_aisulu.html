Background

A Kazakh batyr of giant blood, a goliath who rides watch for her camp against Dzungar raiders and the Wild Hunt alike. On the steppe the camp's own riders ride out against the Hunt, and she rides first. Her brother Kenzhe was taken by the pari three winters ago and came back riding ahead of the Hunt. She hasn't told the camp that the Hunt's best rider has her brother's laugh. She wants her brother home and himself again, and she knows the second is harder.

How to play

  • She rides first and takes the hardest blow. The Wolf makes her whole line better.
  • Animal Speaker: she talks to her eagle and her horse, and both have opinions about the Hunt.
  • Aspect of the Wilds: the Owl, so she sees in the dusk where the Hunt rides.
  • The hook is Kenzhe. When she meets her brother, she will have to decide whether to fight him or follow him.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: the camp's first rider, with Feral Instinct and Instinctive Pounce.
  • Path of the Wild Heart, as the roster has her: the eagle, the wolf and the horse.
  • A goliath, as the roster has her; Soldier and Savage Attacker for a life on the watch line.
