Domingas da Costa

Human · Merchant · Cleric 5 (Life) · level 5
PeopleThe AmericasSalvadorLife DomainLevel 5

Black lay brotherhoods, such as Our Lady of the Rosary of the Black Men, were the heart of free and enslaved Afro-Brazilian life in Salvador: they buried their members, cared for the sick, held feasts and pooled money to buy members their freedom. Women were members and officers, and kept the chest.

Sunday after Mass on the steps of the Rosary church in Salvador's lower city. A small woman in a white turban sits with the brotherhood's book open on her knees and counts the week's coins aloud, twice, so everyone can follow. A man with an iron collar scar sits beside her and watches the total. When it's done she looks up, smiles, and offers you a cake from her tray. "Buy two," she says. "One is for him."

The chest key. Iron, worn smooth. Three people hold keys; the chest opens only when all three turn. She has never once been late.

Her carta de alforria. Her letter of freedom, kept in the brotherhood's chest with the others. She takes it out on her feast day and reads it aloud.

Background

A free woman from Angola who sells cooked food from a tray in the lower city and keeps the key to the Rosary brotherhood's chest. She bought her own freedom a coin at a time, over eleven years, and has helped buy six others since, every coin counted twice on the church steps where everyone can see. Her prayers heal, which the brotherhood knows and the priest pretends not to; she blesses the food on her tray and nobody who buys from it gets the flux. She wants the seventh, the sister in the hook, free by October.

How to play

  • She is a patron: the brotherhood's chest pays in food, favors and a welcome in every Black brotherhood on the coast.
  • She counts every coin twice where everyone can see. Treat her honestly and the lower city is open to the party.
  • Lucky and Life Domain make her a quiet miracle in a crisis. She doesn't call it one.
  • The hook is the seventh freedom. Help her raise the price, or find out why the price keeps rising.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: eleven years to buy herself, and prayers that heal: a pillar of the brotherhood, not its priest.
  • Life Domain: the brotherhood buries, heals and frees, and she does all three.
  • Merchant and Lucky for a woman who made her freedom at a tray; Skilled for the docks.
