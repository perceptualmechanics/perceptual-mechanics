Background

Domenego Cargnelutti is a caul-born benandante of Venetian Friuli who goes out in spirit on the Ember Days to fight the witches for the harvest, armed with a stalk of fennel and with the dead at his back. His grandfather fought the night battles, and his father did, and every generation the inquisitors' questions change a little, until the man fighting witches is somehow the witch. He has started to wonder whether that is the point. All he wants is for the inquisitor to write down what he actually says. The dead who ride behind him have opinions about the Holy Office. He knows Fra Damiano Ferretti, Alvise Trevisan and Ser Marin Zorzi.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • The Tree fights: two attacks, one of them your Planar Strike, and Tyr's Hand puts Wisdom behind the weapon too. Stand in the line.
  • Shillelagh makes the fennel a Wisdom weapon, and Tyr's Hand makes the billhook one; cast Shillelagh before the fight if you can, so your first Bonus Action opens a vista. Command the spirit and you can swap places with it: into the furrow where the witch is, or out of it.
  • Against witches, Protection from Evil and Good and Detect Evil and Good for what comes out of the dark, and Urd's Thread for the save that matters.
  • Out of a fight, the danger is the Holy Office, not the witches. Insight tells you what the inquisitor wants to hear. His conscience makes him say what happened anyway.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: the roster's level, and the level the Tree gets Extra Attack: the fennel and the Planar Strike in one turn. Valhalla and Rise Each Dawn, the hall of the dead and getting up off the field, are 7th.
  • Farmer for Wisdom and Constitution and a peasant's skills; Tough, because he fights four nights a year and works the rest. Lucky for the caul.
  • Planar Adept (1st, every Revelator's now) for Shillelagh: the fennel stalk is what the benandanti carry, and Shillelagh makes it hit like a weapon. Tyr's Hand (3rd) brings his Fighting Style, Defense, and the mastery of the club and the billhook. Positive Energy at 5th, for the harvest he fights for. The breastplate is an old piece from the cernide, the Terraferma's peasant militia.
