Gear

A dagger she has not drawn in twenty years, a writing case with the soapstone-bird seal, a cipher book in Shona, Arabic and Portuguese that exists only in her head, Calligrapher's Supplies, a disguise kit and a forger's kit, a ledger of what every agent has been paid and when. Dead Drop, Uncommon (Requires Attunement by a Spymaster). The Spymaster's keepsake (The Classes, Keepsakes of the Disciplines): a pair of small lockboxes; once a day, a pound of objects placed in one appears in the other, anywhere on the plane. The other box is in Venice, in a Gujarati broker's strongroom; three people know that, and one of them is the broker.

The Bird's seal. A soapstone bird the size of a thumb, cut after the great birds of the old stone city. A letter under it is honored by every factor of the House, which is why she keeps it on a cord around her neck and not in a drawer.

Background

Mutapa's spymaster, who runs the whole foreign service from one room with a tenth of her rivals' assets: a handful of factors in the ports, the Bird's correspondents, and a few Mhizha abroad who write home. The House of the Bird pays her and the crown is the only thing she answers to. Her agents call her Shiri, bird, after the soapstone bird on her seal, and she has never corrected them. She and Simbarashe Moyo are the two people near the king who think the shewstones a catastrophe, and they disagree about everything else on principle. She hires strangers because strangers are what she has, and she pays on time, every time, which in her trade is a kind of tenderness. She wants one more agent in Europe who can pass at court, and would settle for one who can pass at dinner.

How to play

  • Most scenes with Shiri happen by letter. Her factor finds the party, the letter comes under the soapstone bird, and the fee is waiting at the Bird whether the job went well or not.
  • If a party climbs the stairs, she has already Observed them through a dozen reports (Dossiers works on what people leave behind). Let her know one thing about each of them she shouldn't.
  • Network and The Club: she has an asset in every port the Bird reaches, and a favor answers within a day. The shewstones that carry those answers are the thing she most wishes had never been made.
  • She is a patron who is honest about the job and silent about everything around it. Need to know is a kindness too, in her view.

Why this build

  • Why 16th: the most important spymaster alive, with the fewest agents. Without Portfolio and The Club (14th) are a career; what she knows about Toledo, and when she learned it, may decide 1668.
  • Scribe and Skilled: a clerk's start, which is where the Bird finds its people. Alert, because in her trade the one who hears first is the one who lives.
  • Her Arcana is the shewstones' theory, learned from Simbarashe Moyo across many arguments.
