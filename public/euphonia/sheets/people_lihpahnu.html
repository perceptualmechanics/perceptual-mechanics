Lihpahnu

Human · Farmer · Druid 6 (Land) · level 6
PeopleOceaniaMadolenihmw, PohnpeiCircle of the LandLevel 6

On Pohnpei, prize yams are grown for the chiefs' feasts and carried in on poles; a yam so large it takes several men to carry brings its grower great honor, and the size of a family's yams is a matter of fierce, friendly rivalry. Sakau (kava) is pounded and drunk at every feast.

Red earth above the lagoon, a terraced yam garden. A broad woman with mud to the elbows straightens up, wipes her face with the back of her wrist, and looks you over. "You're here for my sister," she says. "Everyone is. Sit. Eat first." Behind her a yam vine as thick as your arm climbs a pole twice your height.

Background

Sehpin's elder sister, who grows the family's prize yams for the chiefs' feasts and considers this the family's real work. She loves Sehpin, finds the stones a little showy and wants to grow a yam so large the Nahnmwarki remembers her name and not her sibling's. Her garden answers her the way the basalt answers Sehpin, and she says so to anyone who will listen. Anyone who wants Sehpin goes through her kitchen, and she feeds them first and asks questions after.

How to play

  • She is the way to Sehpin, and a host: anyone who comes for her sibling eats in her kitchen first.
  • Her rivalry with Sehpin is warm and funny. Take her side, and she will remember.
  • She is no fighter, but she is strong, and nobody threatens her garden twice.
  • The hook is the great yam. Help it reach the feast house, and the Nahnmwarki will remember her name.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: the finest yam-grower in Madolenihmw, with a garden that answers her.
  • Circle of the Land, Tropical land, Warden: she digs, she carries, she grows.
  • Farmer and Tough for the garden; Crafter for the yam poles and feast mats.
