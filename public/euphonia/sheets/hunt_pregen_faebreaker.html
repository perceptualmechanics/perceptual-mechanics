Nhamo

Barbarian 5 (Path of the Faebreaker): features through level 5
AC 14 · HP 55 · Speed 40 ft (Fast Movement)

Who You Are

Nhamo says little and holds on to everything: grudges, friends, a cup, a man's arm in a brawl. He laughs only at his own size. He has never once let go of something he decided to keep. Temper. Winter (resolve): he decided thirty-one years ago what he would do if the Hunt came, and he is calm about it.

At home. Nhamo went for a soldier at fifteen, in Mutapa's wars against the Portuguese prazo armies on the Zambezi, and came home last year with a war axe, a Portuguese captain's hat and a habit of sleeping with his back to a wall. Before he went, when he was nine, he lay in the long grass under the hill and watched the Hunt go by with his sister riding in it, and did nothing, because he was nine. He learned the Faebreaker's grip at war, from a Zambezi soldier who had held a friend through a night the Hidden wanted him.

Tonight. His sister Fadzai rode into the hill thirty-one years ago. If the Hunt comes tonight, nobody from Dambarare rides with it. Not while he can stand.

Door. Any mercenary colonel's camp feeds you, and most will hire you. Debt. A Portuguese trader's captain, whose men you beat on the Zambezi, has come up to the fair with the caravans, and he is asking about you.

Secret. On the Zambezi, a Danae among the king's soldiers read his temper and told him his sister still lives under the hill and is not a girl any more. He hasn't told anyone. He thinks one of the riders tonight may be her.

The others. Sekai set his arm when he was eight. Fungai's grandmother taught him to bar a door. Tichaona came back from the hill and Fadzai didn't, and Nhamo has never held that against the boy, quite.

How to play me. You hold on. Rage, hit the rider you mean to keep, and grab it with Take Hold; it can't flicker away, its attacks are at Disadvantage, and your grip bites it every turn. When a rider reaches for a friend or a villager to carry them off, let go and take hold of them instead: nothing takes what you hold without beating your Athletics. Drag what you hold into Fungai's ward.

Features

Rage (Barbarian 1 (SRD)). Bonus Action, 3 per Long Rest (one back on a Short Rest), not in Heavy armor. While it lasts: Resistance to Bludgeoning, Piercing and Slashing; +2 damage on hits with Strength weapons and Unarmed Strikes; Advantage on STR checks and saves; no spells or Concentration. It lasts until the end of your next turn and goes on each turn you attack, force a save, or spend a Bonus Action to keep it, up to 10 minutes.

Unarmored Defense (Barbarian 1 (SRD)). With no armor on, your AC is 10 + DEX + CON (14). A Shield still adds its bonus.

Weapon Mastery (Barbarian 1 (SRD)). You use the mastery of 3 weapons: the war axe (Greataxe: Cleave), the hand axes (Handaxe: Vex) and the spear (Spear: Sap). You can swap one after a Long Rest.

Danger Sense (Barbarian 2 (SRD)). Advantage on DEX saves unless you are Incapacitated.

Reckless Attack (Barbarian 2 (SRD)). When you make your first attack on your turn, you can attack recklessly: Advantage on STR attack rolls this turn, and attack rolls against you have Advantage until your next turn.

Primal Knowledge (Barbarian 3 (SRD)). One more skill (Animal Handling). While raging, you can make Acrobatics, Intimidation, Perception, Stealth or Survival checks with STR.

Hold Fast (Faebreaker 3). While your Rage is active. Reality's Grip: you can Grapple a creature without a free hand (arm, knees and weight; your hands stay on the axe), one creature at a time (see Hold a Friend); when Rage ends, it goes free unless you have a hand free to keep the grapple, and if you are Incapacitated every hold you have ends. Take Hold: once on each of your turns, right after you hit a creature with a melee attack, try to Grapple it without using an attack. It Can't Get Away: what you've Grappled has Disadvantage on attack rolls (against you too) and on checks to escape you; it can't teleport; changing shape or size doesn't free it; Invisible gives it nothing against you, and you always know where it is. The Grip Bites: at the start of each of your turns it takes 2d6 Force (a d6 per point of Rage Damage). Nothing Takes What You Hold: a creature you hold, either way, can't be Taken unless the taker wins a contest: its d20 + spell attack bonus against your STR (Athletics).

Hold a Friend (Faebreaker 3). Bonus Action: take hold of a willing creature within 5 ft. It's your one hold, so you let go of anything else (outside Rage, it takes a free hand). It isn't Grappled: it moves with you (at half your Speed unless it's Tiny or two sizes smaller), and the hold ends if it moves away, you let go (no action), or you're Incapacitated. While you hold it, it has Half Cover, it can't be moved, teleported or carried off against its will unless you go with it, and it has Advantage on saves against being moved, teleported or changed in shape against its will.

By Touch (Faebreaker 3). While you hold a creature, by Hold Fast or Hold a Friend, you know whether it is a Hidden creature and whether an illusion or shape-change disguises it (not what its true form is), and you know anyone you know well by touch, whatever shape or glamour it wears.

Ability Score Improvement (Barbarian 4 (SRD)). STR +1 and CON +1 (taken).

Extra Attack (Barbarian 5 (SRD)). You attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Fast Movement (Barbarian 5 (SRD)). Your Speed is 10 ft faster while you aren't wearing Heavy armor (40 ft).

Alert (Origin feat (Veteran of the German Wars)). Add your Proficiency Bonus to Initiative. After you roll, you can swap your Initiative with a willing ally's.

Savage Attacker (Origin feat (Human: Versatile)). Once on each of your turns when you hit with a weapon, roll its damage dice twice and use either roll.

Human (Species). Resourceful: Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest. Skillful: Insight. Versatile: Savage Attacker.

Green Mantle (Magic item (Uncommon, attuned)). While you hold a creature by Hold a Friend and it is wrapped in the mantle, it has Advantage on saves against Charmed and Frightened. (Held through The Night Turns, a power you haven't yet, it would win the night at midnight.)

Equipment

A long-hafted war axe from the Zambezi wars (a Greataxe), its blade bright from the whetstone; 4 hand axes (Handaxes); Spear and Shortsword (from the wars, tucked into the thatch over the hearth); Green Mantle (Uncommon; attuned): a plain green cotton cloth worn as a cloak, mended many times; Potion of Healing (Common magic item), from Ambuya Sekai, who says not to ask what is in it; Explorer's Pack (Rope, Bedroll, Tinderbox, Rations); Carpenter's Tools, Shovel, Healer's Kit; A spare iron hoe blade and a fistful of iron nails; A bag of salt from the coast caravans; Salt knotted into a corner of his cloth; Traveler's Clothes, and a Portuguese captain's hat he won't wear in front of the king's officer; 26 GP and a Portuguese captain's silver button nobody at the fair will take as coin.
