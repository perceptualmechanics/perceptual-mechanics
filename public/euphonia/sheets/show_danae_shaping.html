Background

A dwarf of Selmecbánya, the silver town of Upper Hungary, who went down into the old workings and came back up, years later, with a piece of the Hidden and the stone listening to them. The border captains hire them to raise and mend the end-castles of the border-castle line faster than Vienna's war council can pay for it. The Peace of Vasvár says there is no war; the raiders in the reeds, on both sides, haven't read it, and the Hungarian lords who think Vasvár sold them are talking in low voices in each other's houses.

How to play

  • Stones are the plan: walls make cover, pillars lift a raider out of the fight, openings drop one in a pit. Stones don't choose sides, so place them with your friends in mind.
  • Quarry is a fort in one action, once a Long Rest. Build it around where the party already stands; anything that isn't a wall is a ramp, a stair, or a pit.
  • Quick Weaving makes the ladder or the key you need for a minute, for a warp. Fae Weaving each morning: five things a mason's or jeweler's kit could make, up to 140 GP each.
  • Permanent stones are the trade: an hour each, five standing at a time, and the border captains pay for every one.
  • You carry a piece of the Hidden: you are a Hidden creature. A place warded with iron and salt keeps you out by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in. An invitation lasts until dawn or until it's taken back, and one given to a lodger can last the stay. A Danae's Sight or a Hidden Killer's craft knows you for what you are.

Why this build

  • Why 14th: Quarry (14th) is the fort in the meadow, the Danae chapter's own picture of a Shaper, and why the border captains send for them. Deep Ground (6th), the first stones that can go under a raider's feet, and Dream Made Real (10th), a working boat or winch out of an illusion, are the rest of a fortress engineer's kit.
  • Dwarf, after the Danae chapter's Shaper on the Hungarian border: darkvision for the mines, Toughness for the wall. Hermit for Charisma and Constitution and the years under the mountain; Healer for the wounded; Resilient for Concentration.
  • The light half: summer burns the reeds and spares friends, and spring throws a raider off the wall-walk (all four seasons since 11th). Their Token of Method is Shaping's lodestone.
  • A new person: no roster row is a Shaping Danae, so this one is new and the roster is untouched.

Features

Danae (Shaping)
1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't enter a place warded with them by any means, teleportation included (three Danae features are exceptions, and each says so), unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in; a companion who is already inside can (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. Within 30 ft: see thresholds between worlds, and know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or a shape-changing effect, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 18). 7 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save. Success: it moves to the nearest unoccupied space outside, unaffected. Failure: it stays, and the warp (and its season) affect it at the start of its next turn if it is still inside. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +4 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the light half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): spring (Str save or moved 15 ft horizontally; a willing creature can teleport 15 ft instead) and summer (3d6 Fire, no save; you choose who is spared). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Short or Long Rest: unravel one or more of your warps, in place or unused (a subclass's warps count) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 49 with every warp, 19 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Stones. Warps can be stones: solid ground, a wall (Total Cover), a bridge, or a 5-ft-deep pit beneath the space. Real, not illusion: AC 15, 30 HP per 5-ft Cube. No stone on a creature (a pillar or a pit can be, once you have Deep Ground at 6), and none that leaves a creature no unoccupied space to reach. Anything precious made this way is fairy gold and can't be made permanent.
3Quick Weaving. Bonus Action, expend a warp: weave one Fae Weaving object now, even before you have that feature; it lasts 1 minute.
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
6Deep Ground. Two more stone shapes: ramp or stair; pillar (lifts a creature 10 ft; it sinks back when the stone ends). Your 5-ft pit can now open beneath a creature (it falls in, Prone; the ground rises back when the stone ends). Pillars and pits can be made on a creature, by the warp rule (Dex save). One shape per stone. An hour of work makes a stone permanent: it stops being a warp, which comes back at your next rest (up to your Cha modifier of them; another ends the oldest).
7Reading the Season. Bonus Action: up to 5 creatures you can see within 30 ft are read for 1 hour; the DM tells you each one's temper (one for each group of like creatures) and what lies under it. Advantage on Insight against them, and on Cha checks that play to their temper.
7Open Book. Detect Thoughts PB times per Long Rest without a slot; a creature you've read has Disadvantage on the save.
9The Door Ajar. While Away, each Flicker can leave a free warp (any shape, a season you know) in the space you left.
9Thresholds. Spend 1 minute at a threshold you can see with The Sight to open it: Plane Shift without a slot or Material component, for you and up to eight willing creatures, only to or from the Hidden, arriving at the threshold's other side. Once per Long Rest.
9Loop. New shape: a creature trying to leave by any means makes a Wis save or steps back in from the opposite side, losing the movement (or the slot) it spent, and can't try that Loop again until its next turn; one that succeeds isn't affected by that Loop again.
10Dream Made Real. When you cast an illusion spell of level 1+, one object or surface in it becomes real for as long as the illusion lasts (Large or smaller, nonmagical, deals no damage, not in or around a creature): solid, bears weight, blocks movement, fairy gold, and it can be a working thing (a rowboat, a cart, a winch, a lock and its key).
11The Middle Cycles. You know all four seasons (still one per warp).
13Name the Season. Magic action, a creature you've read within 30 ft: Wis save or its temper turns to a season you choose for 1 hour, and its attitude shifts one step in the direction you choose, unnoticed. Cha-modifier times per Long Rest.
13The Hill. New shape: time runs at the hill's pace. A creature that starts its turn inside makes a Wis save or, until its next turn, has half Speed, no Reactions, and an action or a Bonus Action. One that leaves after failing doesn't remember its time inside.
14Quarry. Magic action, once per Long Rest: up to twelve 10-ft-Cube stones at once within 60 ft, in any shapes you know (ignoring the once-a-turn limit on creatures), for 1 hour, without expending warps. With 8 hours of work the whole shape becomes permanent, as one of your permanent stones.
