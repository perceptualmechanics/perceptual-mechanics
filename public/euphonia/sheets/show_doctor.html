Background

A Venetian doctor who read medicine at Padua, went north to Lanternhall in its first years to see what the fuss was about, and came home to the Republic's worst posting by choice: the Sabbionera bastion at Candia, where the Ottoman sappers have been mining under the walls for longer than she has been a doctor. She keeps a practice in the casemates (the garrison talks, and she knows which company is short of powder, which captain is drinking, and which bastion the Turks are digging toward before the engineers do). The Republic pays her late. Fra Bertrand's knights come to her bastion to be patched, because her men get up.

How to play

  • Link the squad before the shooting, with one Bonus Action: the chain lasts until a Long Rest, and a man leaves it only by dying or getting more than 60 ft from everyone in it.
  • Your Bonus Action heals at range down the chain. Bolster the man who's about to be hit; heal the man who was.
  • Stand Back is your Reaction every round now. Keep a Prowess Die for it; a man in the chain who drops gets a die, and nobody's link breaks at 0.
  • House Call is how you stay alive: when a janissary reaches you, you're already somewhere else. In the casemates, the Practice answers two questions a week that the garrison won't answer for its officers.

Why this build

  • Why 10th: a Lanternhall-and-Padua doctor two years into the worst posting in Europe. On Your Feet (10th) is the Euphonia vignette: the first man down is up before the dust settles. The Living Chain (14th) is ahead; her chain is four men, not five.
  • New: no roster row is a Doctor, so she is invented from the Physician chapter's opening vignette (the Venetian on the Sabbionera bastion).
  • Schooled (a university), for Padua; Magic Initiate (Cleric) for Bless before an assault and a voice that carries. Tough, because the bastion is shelled every morning. Intelligence 20 by 8th.
  • Mage Armor in place of armor: AC 14 is low on purpose. A Doctor doesn't hold the line; she leaves it with House Call.

Features

Physician (Doctor)
1Physician's Eye. Medicine uses Intelligence, and you have Expertise in it (included).
1Healing Prowess. 4 × d8; Bonus Action, touch yourself or another creature: it regains d8+5. However it travels (an arrow, a cloud, a blade), Healing Prowess is once on each of your turns; a feature that only expends a Prowess Die, without saying it uses Healing Prowess, isn't a use of it. All back on a Long Rest, 2 on a Short Rest.
1Diagnosis. Bonus Action, one creature you can see within 30 ft (you can choose yourself, as willing): willing, or an ally that can't refuse (Incapacitated or at 0 Hit Points), automatic (current and maximum Hit Points, every condition, disease, Poisoned, or curse, and its lowest save); otherwise Intelligence (Medicine) vs DC 10 + ½ CR (10 + ½ level without a CR): unharmed, injured, Bloodied, or near death; conditions, disease, poison, curse; Necrotic and Poison Resistance, Immunity, or Vulnerability and disease Immunity; its lowest save (the stat block's, or the ability modifier if none is listed; on a tie the DM names either). A failure: not again on it until a rest. Everyone you diagnose is your patient until a Long Rest, any number at once; healing a patient with a Physician feature or spell rerolls 1s on the healing dice.
2Treatment. Utilize action, one use of a Healer's Kit, your patient within reach: ending Poisoned needs no check (unless an effect with a save DC imposed it: then that DC); otherwise Intelligence (Medicine) vs Blinded or Deafened 12, a disease 13 (beaten the slow way: never caught again), Paralyzed 15, a level of Exhaustion 15 (once per creature per Long Rest). If a spell or other effect with a save DC imposed the condition, the DC is the higher of the table DC and that save DC. A success ends the effect, and any spell imposing it on the creature; either way the kit use is spent, and not the same treatment on that creature again until it rests.
3Vital Stream. Bonus Action: link any number of willing creatures you can see within 30 ft, up to 4 besides you (your chain; no order). A creature stays in it while within 60 ft of you or of any other member; a link ends on death, on falling out of that range, or if you're Incapacitated other than by dropping to 0 Hit Points (all of them), never at 0 Hit Points, yours or a member's; otherwise until a Long Rest. Range: Healing Prowess reaches anyone in the chain (spells don't travel it). Heal or Bolster: the healing can be Temporary Hit Points instead. Triage: when you use Healing Prowess on a member, another member gains 5 Temporary Hit Points. Stand Back: Reaction when a member drops to 0, Healing Prowess on it (whenever you have a Reaction and a die: On Your Feet). The Title: introduce yourself as a doctor where someone inside is sick or hurt and you're let in to see the patient, no papers asked until they're seen; officials without a doctor of their own believe your certificate that a person is too ill to travel, stand trial, or marry.
5To the Wounded. On your turn, while you move toward a creature that isn't hostile to you and is Bloodied or at 0 Hit Points (every foot bringing you closer), your Speed increases by 10 ft and your movement doesn't provoke Opportunity Attacks. You've crossed worse floors than this to reach a patient.
5Bedside Manner. Creatures within 30 ft add +5 to each Hit Point Die spent on a Short Rest.
6Practice. In any settlement, a day's work and a room set up a practice: a comfortable living, and after a day of it two true answers from the DM (Who here is sick, pregnant, or drinking? Who has been beaten, and who is paying for it? Who is hiding a condition, and from whom? Who here is dying, and who would be glad of it?), again each further week. A household that has called you to a bedside lets you in again.
6House Call. Reaction when you would take damage or a hostile creature ends its turn within 5 ft: a Prowess Die to teleport within 5 ft of a chained creature within 30 ft (you still take the damage).
7Vitals. When you use Healing Prowess, Diagnosis on a different creature as part of it (the same Bonus Action, or attack). Advantage on saves against disease and the Poisoned condition.
9Last Words. You know how long a creature at 0 HP, or dying of a wound, poison, or disease, has left (to within a minute or a day). Utilize action, touching a dying creature: for 1 minute it is conscious and can speak (no moving, no actions), and makes no Death Saving Throws (damage still causes failures); it ends early if the creature regains HP, and then it is as it was. Once per creature per Long Rest.
10On Your Feet. Revivify on a chained creature (linked for 1 minute after death) with no slot or diamond, prepared or not, once per Long Rest; it returns with 1d8+5, no die spent. Stand Back whenever you have a Reaction and a Prowess Die.
