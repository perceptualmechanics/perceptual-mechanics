New Julfa, the Armenian suburb of Isfahan, sends its sons to every port that has silk, silver or gold, and keeps them honest with partnership contracts and a court of merchants at home that reads every ledger. In Euphonia, Sofala is Mutapa's sea gate: the gold comes down from the plateau, the lamps and curiosities come down with it, and the customs house sees every bale and every license.

In the customs house at Sofala, an Armenian factor turns a Mutapan lamp over in his hands while it lights itself. He names its price in gold by the mithqal, in Venetian ducats and in Mughal rupees, and then, without changing his face, writes a letter to his partners in New Julfa that will move more silver than the whole shipment of lamps is worth.

The three letters. Every letter he sends home goes by three ships on three routes: by Mocha, by Surat and by Hormuz. His partners have learned to read whichever arrives first and burn the others.

Background

A New Julfa merchant of forty-six who came to Sofala for three years and has stayed twelve. He has a desk in the customs house by courtesy of the Mutapan collector, whose sons he taught to keep double-entry books, and a house above the harbor where the coffee is better than anyone's in the port. He prices everything that comes down from the plateau in three currencies before the bale is open, and his letters home go by three routes, so that one at least arrives. He reads a Mutapan lamp the way other men read a face, and he has never once said aloud what he thinks one is worth. His wife is in New Julfa with the children; he writes to her every week, and to the partners every day. He wants one season in which the silver and the monsoon arrive on time together, and then to go home before his daughter marries.

How to play

  • He is a patron who pays on time. Underwrite the party after a Long Rest, and the first time one of them drops, they get back up.
  • Assay and Letters of Credit: he knows what a cargo is worth before it is unloaded, and his house's paper is good in Isfahan, Surat and Venice.
  • Debts: half the captains on the Sofala quay owe him one reasonable thing. A party that needs a berth, a silence or a delay asks him.
  • He reads Mutapan curiosities better than anyone outside the guild, and Mutapa's spymaster knows it. So does Venice.
  • The hook is the letter. Somewhere between Sofala and New Julfa, one of his three letters is being read by someone else, and the prices in it are moving markets.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: twelve years in Sofala, Underwrite and Debts; Follow the Money (10th) is two seasons away.
  • Factor: the Factor's opening vignette, the Armenian factor in the Sofala customs house (The Classes, Counsellor, Factor). Shushan Ghukasian heads a different New Julfa house, and buys from him.
  • Company Factor for a man who keeps a house's books in a foreign port; Skilled twice, for the languages and the lamps.
