Cecilia dalla Pietà

Courtier 3 (College of Psyche): features through level 3
AC 14 · HP 21 · Speed 30 ft · Spell DC 13

Who You Are

Cecilia was raised behind a grille, singing to people who never saw her face, and she still talks to strangers as if they can't see her horns. She sees what everyone wants and what everyone fears, and she can't quite stop herself from saying so.

In Venice. Left as a baby in the Pietà's foundling drawer, she was raised as one of its girls of the choir, singing behind the grille for the city's best families. The governors finished her for society too: letters, manners, the forms of every house in Venice. She sits at the bedsides of the Incurabili by the physicians' leave, and the patients who won't speak to them speak to her.

Your way in. The scryer who wrote the Ten's report is Orsola, her choir-sister from the Pietà, taken into the Ten's service for her gift. Orsola left notes the clerks can't read, in the girls' old choir cipher (which she can read), and Spain's ambassador is looking for her.

Secret. She means to find Orsola before Spain does and get her out of Venice, report or no report. The Cipher Wheel's twin is in Orsola's hands, and Cecilia hasn't dared set a word on hers since the silence began.

The crew. Elia Pardo's patients ask for you by name. Bettina came to hear you sing and stayed to ask about Orsola, which you didn't like.

How to play me. You read every room before you speak in it. In a fight you let them come to you: the one who swings at you gets Stirred, and stumbles, and your daggers say the rest. When a blow would land, Not a Hair Out of Place leaves it a hair short.

Features

Accomplishments and Regard (Courtier 1). Four accomplishments (two at 1st, one more at each of 2nd and 3rd): Correspondence (Letters): Forgery Kit (she already has Calligrapher's Supplies), her letters are read first, and she can tell when one was opened and resealed on the way; Patronage (Society): the Pietà's governors receive her, lend modest sums and speak for her to the Ten's clerks; A Secret (Mysteries): Hideous Laughter, once per Long Rest without a slot; Fencing Style (Arms): Defense (+1 AC in armor). Regard 4. The Read: watch a group for 1 minute, or as a Study action in combat, to learn about up to 4 of them: attitude toward you, who holds authority and who real power, and whether any hides hostility. Never a Misstep: she knows the forms of any house, and has Advantage on Charisma checks to be accepted as belonging.

Ease (Courtier 1). 5 Ease, all back on a Short or Long Rest; each option costs 1. Make It Look Easy: after a failed ability check, add +3. With Ease: before a Charisma check, treat the d20 as 14 (10 + Regard). First Through the Door: on Initiative, add +3 and move up to half your Speed without provoking. Not a Hair Out of Place: Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss; the DM tells you the total first.

Effortless Arms (Courtier 1). Charisma for attack and damage with Finesse weapons.

Learned Magic (Courtier 1). Half caster, Charisma (DC 13, +5). Always knows Prestidigitation, Mending and Message, plus two cantrips from the Courtier list. Four spells prepared from the Courtier list (Enchantment, Illusion, Divination, Abjuration; no Shield: Not a Hair is the courtier's guard). Any tool, instrument or weapon she's proficient with is a focus.

Expertise · Ease in a Fight · Weapon Mastery (Courtier 2). Expertise in Performance and Persuasion. Ease in a Fight: Make It Look Easy now works on saving throws too, and The Riposte: Reaction when a creature you can see misses you with a melee attack, 1 Ease for one weapon attack against it. Mastery: Dagger (Nick) and Dart (Vex).

Telling Cut (Courtier 3). Once on each of her turns on a weapon hit: +1d4 + 3 Psychic, and the target can't make Opportunity Attacks against her until the start of its next turn. A Light Touch: when the cut lands, she can spend 1 Ease for +1d8 of the weapon's type.

People, Not Canvases (Psyche 3). Insight and Medicine; the physicians of any hospital let her work beside them (the Incurabili already do).

Wanting and Fearing (Psyche 3). She sees which passion is strongest in any face: fear, desire, grief, rage or joy. Bonus Action, a creature within 60 ft: Stir (free on a creature that has attacked her since her last turn, otherwise 1 Ease): a Hostile creature has Disadvantage on its next attack roll or saving throw before the start of her next turn. Or Settle (1 Ease): a creature has Advantage on its next saving throw before the start of her next turn, and its Charmed or Frightened condition ends.

Magic Initiate (Cleric) (Feat (Schooled)). Guidance, Light; Bless always prepared, 1 free cast per Long Rest (CHA).

Tiefling (Infernal) (Species). Darkvision 60 ft, Fire Resistance, Fire Bolt, Thaumaturgy, free Hellish Rebuke 1/Long Rest (CHA).

Equipment

Leather Armor; 2 Daggers; 10 Darts; Viol (her violin: she still plays, and the Pietà taught her well, but it's no longer her magic); Entertainer's Pack (with 3 Costumes: Sensa masks); Calligrapher's Supplies; Forgery Kit; Disguise Kit; Book (the Pietà's partbooks); 8 sheets of Parchment; Robe (the coro's red); Lamp and Oil; Quarterstaff; Amulet (holy symbol: the Pietà's silver medal); Cipher Wheel (Common magic item: a brass wheel of lettered rings; when she sets a word of up to six letters on it, the twin's rings turn to the same word, once a day; her choir-sister Orsola holds the twin and hasn't sent a word in a month; set a word tonight and see what comes back); 2 GP, 5 SP.
