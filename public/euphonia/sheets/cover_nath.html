The dhuni. The sacred fire he keeps. Lit, it burns without fuel for a day and a night (Prestidigitation-small magic, his own); its ash is his focus. A table may want the dhuni to be more: a place where his Font answers best.

Inoculation, the old way. He knows the variolation healers in India already practice (the Inoculation Lancet in Euphonia.md, without the lancet): ten doses against smallpox, and the slow way's immunity that magic can't give. Lanternhall hasn't heard of it yet.

Background

A yogi of the Sandhyānāthāha, the Naths: the Font found inside his own body, and the body perfected by yoga and alchemy, which is what the siddhas have pursued for centuries and Basel has only just named. He has stopped aging, and he no longer needs to eat, drink, or breathe, though he does all three out of courtesy. He walks the pilgrim roads and cave shrines of the Mughal north, holds land by an old grant, and lets the world come to him: rajas who want to live forever, dying men who want one more year, a district that wants its locusts kept off. Kings honor the Naths warily, for their curses. He is the Immortalist still chasing the deathless body, where Lanternhall's Ilyanor Sael put the work down two centuries ago and turned to other people's.

How to play

  • The Font is the purse: his own 13 Hit Point Dice, 6 back per Long Rest and one per Short Rest. Spend them on the levels 1–5 spells; save slots for Heal and Regenerate.
  • The siddhis are spells in the book: Fly, Enlarge/Reduce (the great body and the small one), Call Lightning for the weather, Speak with Dead, and Dispel Evil and Good for the possessed. The curse the kings fear is Bane, then Bestow Curse.
  • Transfusion: once a day, give a slot to a willing caster out of your own Hit Point Dice.
  • Out of a fight he is a physician first: Diagnosis, Treatment, and the slow way's immunity, which no cure from a slot can give.

Why this build

  • Why 13th: the siddhi half made. He has stopped aging (9th) and no longer needs food, water, or air (13th); the deathless body that drops to 0 and simply stays Stable (17th) is still ahead of him. Old enough to have walked the roads longer than Lanternhall has stood; not yet a legend.
  • Immortalist, as the book sets it: most Nath yogis are Immortalist Physicians. The Monster Within is never the Nath default.
  • Fire: the vital heat the ascetic keeps, and the dhuni fire. Acolyte for an initiate of a Nath establishment, Intelligence +2, and Magic Initiate (Cleric) for Command, a guru's word.
  • Tough, because his body is the work. Mage Armor from the book in place of armor; War Caster to hold Call Lightning.
