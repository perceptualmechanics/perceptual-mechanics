Her son's last letter. From Mocha, three months old. She has read it so many times she could recite it, and has, to the Zamorin's harbor master.

Background

A Mappila pepper broker on the Calicut beach who sells the Zamorin's officers news of every ship, and every ship news of the Zamorin's officers. She belongs to the Spymaster's Club in the only way anyone does, by trading courtesies with people who read letters. She sends Kesavan Vaidyan the patients who come in on ships. She wants her son's ship back from Mocha before the southwest monsoon closes the coast.

How to play

  • She is a contact who knows every ship on the coast: Network declares an asset on any ship in the roads, and a favor asks it what it knows.
  • Dossiers: she can pass as any broker she has Observed, and forge any captain's hand.
  • She trades news both ways. A party that gives her a good piece gets one back; a party that lies to her is sold to the Zamorin's officers by noon.
  • The hook is her son's ship. Anyone sailing to Mocha this season has her attention.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: a broker of the beach, not yet of the Club. Dossiers and Profile (6th) are the trade.
  • Merchant and Lucky: pepper is a gamble every season.
