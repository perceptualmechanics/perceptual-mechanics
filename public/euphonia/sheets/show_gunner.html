A Makassarese woman who learned Mutapan artifice in Sofala from a Gujarati who learned it in the capital, and now keeps the walls of Somba Opu against the VOC. She isn't a Mhizha in the guild's eyes, and the guild would very much like to know where she is. The Dutch call her gun the ghost on the wall.

Night on the walls of Somba Opu. A woman in a dark baju sets a squat brass device on the parapet with a click, checks its level twice, and walks back along the wall to where her rice is going cold. Out in the dark a Dutch working party starts digging again. The thing on the parapet turns its head, and fires, and fires, and she eats.

Background

Daeng Nai grew up in Makassar when it was the freest port in the islands, where English, Danish, Portuguese and Gujarati ships traded side by side and the sultan let them. She went to Sofala on a cousin's ship and found a Gujarati who taught anyone who could pay, and she paid in pepper. She came home with an arc gun, a turret, and no name on any guild's rolls. Now the treaty the sultan signed last November hasn't held, the Company is back under the walls, and Arung Palakka's scouts have orders to take her alive. She retunes a merchant's lamps for powder money and complains about them. She has written to the great artificer; he will never answer.

How to play

  • Set the turret down first. After that, every Attack action is two attacks: the gun and the turret.
  • You don't need to stand where the turret stands. Forward Observer lets you see from it and shoot from its space, no action, while your body stays behind the parapet; the turret only needs you within 120 ft.
  • Faerie Fire gives the turret Advantage too. Heat Metal is for a Dutch officer's breastplate.
  • Rangefinder: you know how far, how high, and whether the wall will hold. On a siege, that's half the job.
  • Alert puts you first in the order; Lucky's three points get you out of the scouts' hands at least three times.

Why this build

  • Level 6, as the roster has her: a self-taught engineer at the start of her war. Forward Observer and Watch (6th) are the ghost on the wall; Shells (10th) are a few years and a lot of platinum away.
  • Guard (Int +2, Wis +1; Alert) for the woman who keeps the walls. Lucky (human Versatile) because the people hunting her want her alive.
  • A breastplate (no Stealth Disadvantage, for the night) and a rattan shield (AC 17): the Arc Gun takes one hand. Intelligence 18 at 4th; Constitution 15 (it once paid the second turret's Hit Point Die; both turrets are free now, so it's just Hit Points).
  • Makassar in 1668 is history: the Treaty of Bongaya was signed in November 1667, the war began again in 1668, and Somba Opu held into 1669. The sultan and the Bugis prince appear only by name.
