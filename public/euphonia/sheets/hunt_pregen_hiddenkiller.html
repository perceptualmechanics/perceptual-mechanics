Fungai

Ranger 5 (Hidden Killer): features through level 5
AC 16 · HP 44 · Speed 30 ft · Spell DC 13

Who You Are

Fungai is brisk, rude when frightened, and never wrong about a door. She counts things: nails, people, the hours to dark. She is never late. Temper. Summer (anger, confidence): she is furious that the Hunt picked her first night as the Killer, and sure she can see it off.

At home. The smith's daughter, and his hand at the anvil since she could lift the tongs. Her grandmother Manyara has been Dambarare's Killer for fifty years, and her knees have gone; when the dark half began, Manyara handed Fungai the iron bell and the bounds. Fungai has walked the bounds every dusk since and seen nothing. This morning, with the moon setting in eclipse, she read the tide.

Tonight. The tide is at the flood, and a Hunt is gathering behind the granite hill. It's her first Hunt as Dambarare's Killer, Manyara is watching from her doorway, and Fungai would rather die than ask Manyara what to do.

Door. Any border village takes you in and asks you to look at its doors. Debt. Somebody your family owes was taken, and nobody has brought them back: Nhamo's sister Fadzai, who was minding your mother, a child of two, the night she rode into the hill.

Secret. She doesn't believe Tichaona came back alone. She has seen him walk through a wall. She has told no one, because the officer would have him sent to Sofala to be studied, and because he has never once failed to ask to come in.

The others. Nhamo is the only man in Dambarare who does what she says the first time. Sekai thinks she's too young; Sekai is right. Tichaona she watches.

How to play me. You saw it coming. Read the Tide has already told you the Hunt is coming, and from where; Sekai knows when. Your day is doors and ground: ward the open ground in front of the door Shamiso sleeps behind, doorstep included (one ward only), and see iron and salt on every door your people sleep behind. When the riders come: Hunter's Mark on the rider in the glamour, two arrows, Cold Iron on the first that hits (it can't flicker away), and ring the iron bell when a rider vanishes.

Features

Spellcasting (Ranger 1 (SRD)). WIS half caster: 6 prepared Ranger spells; swap one after a Long Rest. Focus: a stick of msasa wood.

Favored Enemy (Ranger 1 (SRD)). Hunter's Mark is always prepared, and you can cast it 3 times without a slot (Long Rest). Hunter's Mark: Bonus Action, Concentration up to 1 hour; your hits on the target deal +1d6 Force, and you have Advantage on Perception and Survival checks to find it; when it drops to 0 HP, move the mark as a Bonus Action.

Weapon Mastery (Ranger 1 (SRD)). You use the mastery of 2 weapons: Longbow (Slow) and Shortsword (Vex). Swap one after a Long Rest.

Deft Explorer (Ranger 2 (SRD)). Expertise in Survival; two more languages (Sylvan, Swahili).

Fighting Style (Ranger 2 (SRD)). Archery: +2 to attack rolls with ranged weapons (in the Longbow's +9).

Iron and salt (The Hidden, in the rules). Every house in Dambarare keeps an iron nail in its doorpost, which bars that door and nothing else; a house is barred only when every door, window and the gap under the eaves is ironed and salted. The customs house has had no iron since the king's officer came, and the gathering places need more than the town keeps. Ten minutes of iron and salt bars a room or small building until dawn. A Hidden creature can't come in through an ironed and salted opening unless someone inside who isn't Hidden asks it in, and an invitation to one rider admits its whole company. Iron bars only the openings: a rider who teleports, as Fadzai can once she is Away, comes in anyway; your ward on a house stops that. A Charmed householder's invitation counts. Moving the iron or breaking the salt ends the bar on that opening, and a new hole (torn thatch) is open until someone irons and salts it. The full rule is in The Setting (Iron and Salt).

Hidden Killer Spells (Hidden Killer 3). Always prepared: Protection from Evil and Good; from 5th, See Invisibility.

Read the Tide (Hidden Killer 3). When you finish a Long Rest, or after 10 minutes watching the land, you learn for the area within 1 mile of you whether the Hidden there are at the flood or the ebb, and whether Hidden creatures are abroad there or gathering at its edge: roughly how many (a few, a band or a Hunt) and which way the nearest lie. Not what they are. You never have Disadvantage on Initiative for being surprised by Hidden creatures, and you have Advantage on Survival checks to track them.

Prepare the Ground (Hidden Killer 3). 10 minutes (it can be done during a rest): ward a building, or open ground up to 60 ft on a side, naming the creatures the ward ignores (name your allies, and remember to name Tichaona: he is a Hidden creature). It lasts until you finish a Long Rest (one made during a Long Rest lasts until the end of the next) or until you ward another place: one ward at a time. It doesn't bar entry (iron and salt a door for that). Forewarned: you know when anything it doesn't ignore enters, and it wakes you; when combat starts with you or any enemy in it, you and your allies have Advantage on Initiative and Hidden creatures in it have Disadvantage. No Glamour: Hidden creatures in it can't be Invisible, you and your allies see their true forms through any illusion or shape-change, and they can't teleport into or out of it. Bread and Salt: you and your allies have Advantage on saves against Charmed while in it. Nightmare Ward: Magic action, once per Short or Long Rest: for 1 minute a 10-ft Emanation from you is warded as above (no Forewarned), ignoring you and your allies; it doesn't end a ward you've made.

Cold Iron (Hidden Killer 3). Once on each of your turns, when you hit a creature with an attack roll using a weapon, deal +1d6 damage of the weapon's type; against a Hidden creature, +2d6, and it can't teleport until the start of your next turn. While a creature carries your Hunter's Mark, you know whether it is a Hidden creature, Invisible gives it nothing against you, and you see its true form alongside any illusion or shape-change.

Ability Score Improvement (Ranger 4 (SRD)). DEX +1 and WIS +1 (taken).

Extra Attack (Ranger 5 (SRD)). You attack twice when you take the Attack action.

Alert (Origin feat (Border-Born)). Add your Proficiency Bonus to Initiative. After you roll, you can swap your Initiative with a willing ally's.

Skilled (Origin feat (Human: Versatile)). Proficiency in three more skills: Animal Handling, Intimidation and Medicine (already in your numbers).

Human (Species). Resourceful: Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest. Skillful: Athletics. Versatile: Skilled.

Iron Bell (Magic item (Uncommon, attuned)). Magic action: ring it. Until the end of your next turn, each Hidden creature you choose within 60 ft that can hear it loses Invisible, can't gain it, and can't teleport. 3 rings per Long Rest. Rung at a door, it tells you whether a Hidden creature has crossed that threshold since the last dawn.

Equipment

Longbow and a quiver of 40 arrows, every iron head forged at her father's forge; Shortsword (a short stabbing blade) and a Scimitar (her grandmother's curved blade; she prefers the short one); Studded Leather (a hide coat studded with forge nails); Iron Bell (Uncommon; attuned): the old iron bell of the fair's gate, rung for every caravan for forty years, which the customs house replaced and Manyara kept; Potion of Healing (Common magic item); Sling and 20 iron shot; Smith's Tools, Hunting Trap, 10 Iron Spikes, a bag of forge nails; Hooded Lantern, 2 flasks of Oil; Pouch of salt, and two more she carries for other people's doors; Salt knotted into her cloth; Explorer's Pack, a stick of msasa wood (her Druidic Focus); Traveler's Clothes, a gray cotton cloth; 21 GP.
