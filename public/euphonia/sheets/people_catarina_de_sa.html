Background

An Afro-Portuguese prazo heiress whose family held land below Tete until Mutapa kept its mines and the leases went to the crown's tollkeepers. She kept the family's achikunda, its Portuguese, its Shona and its grudge, and turned them into the best smuggling operation on the coast. Nobody caught with platinum in Sofala has ever been able to say where it came from, because she makes sure they never knew. She pays her runners' widows, and keeps a file on each runner as thick as the Bird's. A Lisbon lawyer has written to say a European court might honor her deed; she has not decided whether she wants it back. What she does want is to sleep one night in her grandmother's house below Tete, which Mutapa gave to a tollkeeper.

How to play

  • She asks after everyone's family, and she knows them. Use it: warmth and a file, in the same breath.
  • Need to Know is why nobody can ever say where the platinum came from. Every runner knew exactly their part, and the moment magic asks one of them, she knows.
  • The Hum on the Sofala waterfront tells her who did what this month. A party that needs something moved, found or kept quiet on the Monsoon Road comes to her table sooner or later.
  • The deed is the hook. Whoever offers to get her land back is offering to make her choose between her runners and her grandmother's house.

Why this build

  • Why 11th: the coast's established smuggler, with Cold Read and The Hum. The Sit-Down (14th), where every underworld receives her as an equal, is a door she hasn't decided to open.
  • Criminal: the family business became a crime the day Mutapa kept its mines. Alert, because the tollkeepers search the market at noon.
