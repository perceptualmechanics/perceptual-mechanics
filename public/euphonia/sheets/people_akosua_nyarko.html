Akosua Nyarko

Human · Guide · Ranger 7 (Hidden Killer) · level 7
PeopleWest and Central AfricaA forest village behind AccraHidden KillerLevel 7

Every forest has its owners. The mmoatia, the little forest folk of the Akan whose feet point backward, teach herbs to the priests they take, and laugh at everyone else. Akosua is her village's Hidden Killer: her work is knowing where the owners' land begins, and making sure the village doesn't forget.

The harmattan haze over the forest's edge behind Accra, a shrine where the path enters the trees. A wiry woman with a flintlock older than she is crouches over a line of footprints a foot long that point the wrong way, back the way they came, and laughs quietly. Then she tells you to step exactly where she steps, and not to answer if anything in the trees calls your name.

Background

An Akan hunter who walks the forest's edge for her village, keeps the owners' rules and deals with what doesn't. She has met the mmoatia three times, two more than most hunters; they taught her one herb and laughed at her for a day. She guides parties through the forest for a fair price and the promise that they'll do exactly as she says at the shrines. She wants to learn what the mmoatia teach the priests, without paying the priests' price.

How to play

  • She is a guide first. The forest is safe with her if the party keeps her rules at the shrines, and she will tell them which rules those are.
  • Read the Tide and Prepare the Ground make a night camp in the forest a fortress against what walks there.
  • Mark the thing with backward feet, shoot once with iron, and laugh if it runs. She doesn't chase into the owners' land.
  • The hook is the priests' price. She wants their knowledge without their bargain; a party that can make a better bargain with the mmoatia is a party she will walk anywhere with.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: three meetings with the mmoatia, and Inside Out: the guest who hears her name called from the trees gets a second chance not to answer.
  • Guide and Magic Initiate (Druid): a hunter's life, and a little of the forest's magic.
  • Archery for the musket: Euphonia's flintlocks are the core rules' musket, and her grandfather's is as good as any.
