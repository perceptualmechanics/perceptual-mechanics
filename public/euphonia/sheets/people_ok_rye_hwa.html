Background

A Korean mudang outside Seoul's walls, who bargains with the spirits for her neighbors, pays the state's tax on shamans and is despised by the scholars who send for her at night. Several of them send for her when a child is sick. She has argued with the spirits on behalf of a castaway Dutch sailor, a magistrate's widow and a drowned ox. She uses the Jesuits' almanac to argue with the spirits about the date. She wants her daughter to inherit the drum and the clients without the contempt.

How to play

  • She is a go-between for the spirits: Parley before a fight, a bargain struck, and a spirit that keeps its word or answers for it.
  • Every scholar in Seoul despises her by day and some send for her by night. A party that needs a door opened in Joseon finds that she knows who.
  • She laughs at danger and at yangban. Let her dance on the blades.
  • The hook is her daughter. A world where the drum passes on without contempt is what she is bargaining for.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: thirty years at the drum, and Rowan and Red Thread: the ward she ties on whoever she is worried about.
  • Hidden Domain, as the roster has her: the go-between who bargains with the spirits. The domain's rules; Korea's spirits.
  • Entertainer and Musician: a gut is a performance as much as a rite.
