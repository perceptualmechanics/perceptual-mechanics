Background

A capitão-do-mato, a hunter of people who escaped slavery, whom the sugar planters of Pernambuco hire to lead their expeditions against Palmares. He was made, not born: a war in his youth and a long night after it took everything but the computation. He has studied every expedition that failed against Palmares and knows exactly why each did, which is why the planters pay him and why Ana Kitumba knows his name. What he wants he will not say aloud. He keeps a garden of medicinal plants he waters at dawn, and lets no one else touch it.

How to play

  • He is a villain from a hard part of the record. Use as much of him as the table wants, and agree on it first.
  • He has studied every failed expedition. Deduction and Total Recall: he will not make their mistakes.
  • He is a hunter, not a soldier: Survival with Expertise, poison, and patience.
  • Ana Kitumba is his opposite. When they meet, it should matter.

Why this build

  • Why 9th: Total Recall: every failed expedition, word for word.
  • Hollowed, as the roster has him: the war and the long night took everything but the computation.
  • Criminal and Alert for a hunter of people; Tough for the forest.
