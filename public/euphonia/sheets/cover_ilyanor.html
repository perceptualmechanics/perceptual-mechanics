Background

Ilyanor Sael, Attending Physician and Chair of Vital Studies at Lanternhall. He found the Font in his own blood two centuries ago, as a student at Corvane, before the College taught medicine at all; he refused the Rite at 4th and has taught other Immortalists since. Sixty years ago he argued the College into a medical faculty. He transcribed arcane vigor from a hedge-healer who had never written it down, wrote anaesthesia for the Theatre, wrote both halves of defibrillation, then designed a rod that casts only the half that starts a heart. He designed the Field Service's Ward, and makes the part of each that the Stillroom's bench can't. He lives in two rooms over the Library of Cases and does not leave the Hall: bring him the problem and he tells you what it is. Bjorn Sael-Rosenberg is his great-nephew. He is the Immortalist who put the work down: the deathless body the Naths still chase on the pilgrim roads, he stopped reaching for, and spent the centuries on other people's.

How to play

  • He doesn't go out. A party brings him the problem; one question, answered honestly, per week a student works the Long Ward. At the table he is the man who already knows, and says so.
  • In the Hall, nothing he Diagnoses is a mystery for long: the patient, then a second creature with each heal. Send the Ward to the bed that needs it.
  • The Font pays for the levels 1–5 healing, out of his 19 Physician Hit Point Dice (a Long Rest brings back 10 of his 20 dice, a Short Rest one); slots are for Heal, Regenerate, and True Resurrection.
  • Forbiddance is always prepared and cast over the Hall every day for thirty days, so it stays until dispelled: nothing teleports in, and the kinds of creature he named burn when they enter.
  • The Whole Ward: when the party is down or spent, Mass Heal on your level 9 slot brings everyone within 60 ft back at once (700 Hit Points to share) and takes a level of Exhaustion off each creature it heals. It is the fight's last word, not its opening.

Why this build

  • Why 20th: he is the setting's legend of medicine, two centuries of medicine in Basel, and the Hall's since the day it opened, and this is Lanternhall's own build: Immortalist 19 / Mhizha 1, the Font found, the Rite refused.
  • The Final Rite: by physician.md, declining the Rite at 4th closes the Final Rite, so Resignation (Regrowth and an Ascendance boon at 19th) isn't his; he resigned two centuries earlier, the hard way. If a table wants him to have Resignation's benefits, the rule or his history has to change.
  • The Mhizha level: he once turned up unannounced at a Sofala workshop, the most famous doctor in Europe standing at the door with his hat in his hands, and swept its floors for a season to learn how a lamp lights itself. The guild, which never sells its training, decided somehow that he hadn't bought it; he left with the knack and a habit of calibrating everything he touches, and the master who let him in still tells the story at every guild dinner.
  • The level gives him Tinker (the Ward's fittings and the Rod's, mended at the bench), the retuning of the Hall's Mutapan lamps, and two Mhizha spells; Int 13 to multiclass, long since met. The Ward gives its own Shield training, so he needs nothing from the Mhizha for that. Standard array: Int 20 by 8th, Con 19 with the Boon, so the Font's Hit Point Dice are worth spending.
  • Radiant, for a hall named for its lamp. Font-Touched: Bless, Daylight, Legend Lore (he is the Library of Cases), and Forbiddance, the reason he never needs to leave.
