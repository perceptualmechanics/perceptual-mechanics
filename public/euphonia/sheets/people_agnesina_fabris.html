Background

A bookbinder's widow of San Polo who kept the accounts, sewed the gatherings and never once climbed onto a roof, until her husband died of a winter fever and on his last night something tall and bright stood at the foot of the bed and asked her for nothing at all. It has stayed in touch. When she calls, an angel lands like something with somewhere else to be, does one errand and goes back. She has used them to carry a letter across the city in a minute, to stand over a sick neighbor and, more and more, to catch Matteo, the bindery's apprentice, who runs the roofs at night and jumps too soon. She doesn't know whose heaven sends them: they answer when she prays to the saints, and once when Simon Horowitz said a word in Hebrew she didn't catch. She wants to know, and why it chose a Catholic bookbinder.

How to play

  • The angel comes in bursts: one errand, then it goes. Guard is her favorite, because what she does is catch people.
  • Tongue of Fire: she speaks Celestial and knows the names and ranks of the messengers. She is still learning whose they are.
  • Devil's Sight and Spider Climb make Venice's roofs at night her own street.
  • She is a mentor to the young and reckless, and kind about it. She also never lets Matteo jump first.
  • The hook is the angel's heaven. Simon Horowitz thinks he knows, and won't say; someone could find out.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: a year and a half since the night her husband died, and two level 3 slots: the angel and Revivify.
  • Envoy: the Envoy chapter's opening vignette, the woman on the San Polo roofs who catches Matteo (The Classes, Envoy Warlock).
  • Acolyte for a devout widow; Magic Initiate (Cleric) for her rosary prayers. Repelling Blast because, on a roof, pushing is better than hitting.
