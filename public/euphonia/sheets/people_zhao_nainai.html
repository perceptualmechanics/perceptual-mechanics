Zhao Nainai

In north China a household that keeps a fox immortal speaks of it as "Old Master Hu" or "the immortal," never as a fox, and keeps its tablet in a side room with incense, eggs and wine; a woman the immortal chooses speaks and heals for it (Life on the Border, Hidden Domain Cleric). The domain's rules are the book's; the fox immortal and her village's ways are her own tradition's.

The side room of a farmhouse outside Tianjin, incense thick in the air, a family on its knees with a feverish child. An old woman sits very straight before a red paper tablet and closes her eyes. When she speaks again her voice is older and more courteous than her own, and it asks after the child's grandfather by name, and what exactly he did to a burrow under the threshing floor in the spring of the Dragon year.

Old Master Hu's tablet. Red paper in a wooden frame, the characters in a hand she does not recognize as her own, though she wrote them. The incense before it never quite goes out.

The grandson's essays. Eight-legged essays for the county examination, in a stack under her pillow. The immortal has read them. Its comments, in her hand, are not encouraging.

Background

Granny Zhao, sixty-one, through whom the fox immortal of her household has spoken for forty years in return for incense, eggs, wine and the use of her voice. Nobody calls it a fox to its face: it is Old Master Hu. When a family brings a sick child she goes into the side room and lets it speak, and it asks after their ancestors, explains courteously what was done and what would make it right, and tells her what to boil. The child gets better. The immortal is fair, if old-fashioned, and settles quarrels about wells and boundaries as well. The magistrate's runners pretend not to know; the magistrate's wife comes on the fifteenth of every month. She wants her grandson to pass the county examination. The immortal has declined to arrange it, and suggested he study.

How to play

  • She is a go-between who carries a household's requests to the immortal and brings back its answers: Parley, a bargain struck, and an immortal that keeps its word.
  • Bring Back is what families come to her for: the sick child, the Charmed bride, the man who came home from the fields not himself.
  • The magistrate's wife comes monthly. A party that needs a quiet word in the county yamen finds that Granny Zhao knows who.
  • Play her as an old village woman with a voice in her that is older and much politer than she is.
  • The hook is Hu Lanxin. When the fox spirit passes through, the immortal greets her as a junior relation and asks pointed questions about her age.

Why this build

  • Why 8th: forty years of the immortal's voice, and Rowan and Red Thread.
  • Hidden Domain: the Hidden Domain chapter's opening vignette, the north China woman who speaks for the fox immortal in the side room (Life on the Border, Hidden Domain Cleric).
  • Acolyte for a household shrine's keeper; Lucky for the household the immortal keeps lucky.
