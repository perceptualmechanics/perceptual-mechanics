Tonderai Shumba

Human · Artisan · Mhizha 10 (Lionskin) · level 10
On the coverMutapa, 1668The king's lions

The capital calls them Lionskins, after the lion masks the first suits wore at the king's gates; the suits are metal. A Guardian's helm wears a mane of drawn copper wire that lifts and crackles when the suit is charged, and the capital calls its gate-guards the king's lions; the old crown's commander was also its captain of the gates, and the suits that stand in the capital's gateways are his heirs. A charged suit hums like a lion heard a mile off at night.

The Portuguese have rushed the gate twice. The third time the lion in the gateway lets the first man reach him, takes the blow meant for the boy beside him, and the copper mane stands straight up. Then the lion speaks, at three hundred feet, and the line behind the man stops.

Background

Tonderai Shumba was born in the capital the year it started to grow, apprenticed to a smith, and sent to the gates at twenty because he was big and could keep a suit in calibration. Shumba, the lion, is his family's totem, their mutupo, which is why the lion suit was his. He has stood a gateway for ten years: the old town's at first, now the new city's, which moves outward every season. He knows every merchant house's carters by name and which of them pays the gate fee in full. The court says the gate is the king's; the houses say the road is theirs; he says the gate is shut until he opens it, and so far both believe him.

How to play

  • Stand in the gap. Nothing moves you or knocks you down, and the Guardian's Reaction takes the hit meant for whoever is beside you, then charges the suit for your next shot.
  • Fire the gun every turn, even point-blank; once charged, add the 2d6 to the next hit. The fist is for Opportunity Attacks.
  • The roar: your speaking-horn carries to 300 ft. A gate-lion who talks is a gate nobody rushes twice.
  • The Charged Plate goes to whoever stands the gate with you, never into the suit.
  • Tailored: off duty the suit passes for a guard officer's coat. People who look closely see the armor; people who look twice see the lion.

Why this build

  • Lionskin, Guardian model: the lion-headed suit is the Guardian's, the gate-guards of the capital. Infiltrator is the lioness, for a different figure.
  • Level 10: a mid-career gate captain; Improved Models (he can't be moved) and Sealed Seams are the gate's features. Flight waits for 14th.
  • The suit (2026-11-10): base AC 18 whatever it was made from, with no Strength requirement, and +1 for the Guardian model: AC 19. No shield, since the suit gives nothing while one is held. Artisan (Int +2, Str +1) kept his Strength 14 from when chain mail needed it; it carries the ledger now. Tough (human Versatile) for the one who takes the hits.
  • Intelligence 20 by 8th: the gun, the gauntlets and the Guardian's Reaction all run on it.
