Gear

A physician's robe and cap, a brass-caged sand-glass, his reed flute (he doesn't play it in front of the musicians), a casebook copied from the hospital's (the originals don't leave), spectacles, a writing case, Alchemist's Supplies, Healer's Kit, spellbook (Hebrew script, Ottoman Turkish notes, and Arabic medical terms). Languages: Ladino, Hebrew, Ottoman Turkish, Arabic, Persian (read), Latin. Tools: Alchemist's Supplies, Calligrapher's Supplies, kudüm (drum), ney (reed flute), tanbur (Musician), Healer's Kit.

The sand-glass. A half-minute glass in a brass cage, worn smooth where his thumb turns it. He counts a pulse against it and against the musicians' drum at once, and he can tell a fever from a fright from a lie by the difference. At a table, it's the prop for Diagnosis and Reading the Nerves.

The case books. His copies of the hospital's case books from 1488 on, and the commentary he is writing beside them: which mode for which melancholy, which hour, which water. With an hour in them, he can find the case that matches yours, if anyone has had it.

Background

A Sephardic physician of Edirne, the senior doctor of the darüşşifa that Sultan Bayezid II founded in 1488, where the mad have been treated with music, running water, and quiet for longer than Basel has had a ward. He prescribes the musical mode, the hour and the water for each patient as another doctor prescribes a purge, and his nerve-craft does the rest. Letters from Amsterdam, Livorno and Venice ask him about the man who took the turban at Edirne two years ago; he answers every one that he does not diagnose people he hasn't examined. The case books go back to 1488, he has read them all, and he wants to finish his commentary on them, and name every musician who ever played there, before his eyes go. He thinks Basel's Physicians are very promising, and young.

How to play

  • You heal from across the room and break minds from farther. Reflex is a 30-ft Bonus Action heal that also gives back a Reaction or a free step out.
  • Spend Prowess Dice on the save, not the heal: Disadvantage on your Hold Person, or a reroll for the ally who just failed against fear.
  • Frayed Nerves rewards opening with a condition spell: the first creature to fail takes extra Psychic and can't react, and everyone near you gets Temporary Hit Points.
  • At the hospital he is a doctor first: The Quiet Room eases a fear or a mania for a day, and he can tell a seizure from a possession and explain it to a mufti, a rabbi, or a cathedral.

Why this build

  • Why 12th: his roster level: the senior physician of a hospital older than Lanternhall by nearly 170 years. Frayed Nerves (10th) is in hand; The Body Electric (14th) is not.
  • Musician by Versatile: a doctor who prescribes the mode and the hour should be able to play them, and the Heroic Inspiration is The Quiet Room's cousin at the table.
  • Sage, Magic Initiate (Wizard) for Shield, and War Caster at 12th for the Concentration his holds live on. Intelligence 20 at 8th.
  • The hospital, its music and its water are history (Evliya Çelebi described the musicians in the 1650s). Ishak and his case-book commentary are invented.
