The silver inventory. The Infermeria's patients eat off silver, and the chirurgeon keeps the book: every dish, cup and spoon on the great ward, signed out and signed back. One line is in red: a dish signed out to a Provençal knight on his ward turn, never returned. A party bound for Candia will be asked to carry a third letter.

Paré's hand. A worn copy of Ambroise Paré's surgery, in a French he reads slowly, open at the woodcut of the iron hand with jointed fingers. He has built two from it. The second one works. It's the drawing he copies his pegs and hooks from, and the reason Pegs and Hooks is the first thing a maimed knight asks him about.

Background

The senior chirurgeon on the Order of St John's great ward at Valletta, where the knights command the hospital and the Maltese do the work. He has taken more limbs off knights than the Turks have, and fitted most of their owners with something to stand on afterward. He trained the old way, as a barber-surgeon's boy in Birgu, and the Saw is the guild's instrument, not Lanternhall's; when Lanternhall sent its books he read them and found most of it already in his hands. He serves the sick on silver because silver is cleaner, and he counts it: one dish has been missing since a Provençal knight did his turn on the ward, and he has written to Candia about it twice. He calls the Birgu cunning woman a witch in front of the knights and sends her the fevers he can't break.

How to play

  • Stand in front. You have 59 Hit Points, AC 17, four Prowess Dice, and a self-heal on a turn you hit something; the healing you hand out comes from the hospital, not your hands.
  • Open with the Field Hospital: from then on every Bonus Action is a 2d6 heal to anyone within 40 ft, and Under the Knife runs alongside it, since it isn't Healing Prowess.
  • Push what you hit and follow it (Under the Knife), or take its sword arm (Amputation): a crippled arm drops its weapon and has Disadvantage on its first attack each turn. Once it fails, you've done it for the rest; save the Amputation for the one that matters.
  • After the fight he is a surgeon: the table version of Amputation ends a gangrene or a curse in a limb, and Pegs and Hooks puts the patient back on two feet by morning. He knows when any of them takes a fever, up to a mile off.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: his roster level. Extra Attack, Pegs and Hooks and Field Hospital (6th) are the redesigned Chirurgeon's frontline kit; 7th adds Vitals and the first level 4 slot (Aura of Life for a breach).
  • The Chirurgeon is a bruiser who heals himself: Under the Knife heals him, not an ally, so the build is a breastplate, a shield, and Tough. Since 2026-11-10 the Saw runs on Intelligence (18), which also drives the spells and the DC; his Strength 16 stays from the old build, for Athletics and hauling the wounded.
  • Artisan, for the barber-surgeons' guild he came up through; Crafter, because a ward's crutches and pegs come from somewhere.
  • Longsword (Sap) and handaxe (Vex) for the two Slashing masteries: Sap keeps the man in front of him missing; a handaxe goes after one that runs.
