Étaín of the Ford

Elf (wood elf lineage) · Soldier · Barbarian 10 (Bordermarch) · level 10
On the coverThe BordermarchesSamhain to Beltane

The Bordermarches are the borderlands where the Hidden and the real overlap, and the Wild Hunt rides out of them to raid in the dark half of the year, hardest at the flood, the bright fortnight around the full moon. The Bordermarch barbarians are its rank and file. Their rage is the ríastrad, the warp-spasm of the old heroes: the body turns in its skin, and the hero's light rises from the brow.

Out of the thorn-hedge at the first dark of Samhain comes a rider whose body has turned in its skin: one eye sunk, one starting from her head, a light standing up from her brow like a mast. The great spear in her hands reaches the gatepost from the middle of the lane. Behind her the hounds; behind the hounds, someone running with a bucket.

Elf (wood elf lineage)

Elf (2024), wood elf lineage: Darkvision 60 ft; Fey Ancestry (Advantage on saves against Charmed); Keen Senses (Insight); Trance (a Long Rest in 4 hours, awake). Wood Elf: Speed 35 ft; Druidcraft; Longstrider (3rd) and Pass without Trace (5th), each once per Long Rest without a slot, with Wisdom (not while raging). She is a wild elf of the Bordermarches, not one of the courts' refined elves, and she remembers what she is, which makes her a Hidden creature to a Killer's iron and bell.

Gear

A great spear with a bronze head as long as a forearm (as a pike, +9, 1d10 + 5 Piercing, 1d10 + 8 raging; reach 10 ft, 15 ft in the ríastrad; Push); three flint-headed javelins (+9, 1d6 + 8 raging; Slow); a bronze sword (as a greatsword, Graze); a bronze axe (as a handaxe, Vex); no iron anywhere on her, and no armor; a cloak of hound-skin pinned with a thorn; a child's wooden horse, from a raid three hundred years ago, that she has never been able to throw away. Languages: Elvish, Sylvan, and the villages' tongue (in place of Common), enough to take what she wants. Tools: A gaming set (Soldier): knucklebones of something that wasn't a sheep.

Hero-Light Torc, Uncommon (attuned, Barbarian). A twisted gold torc, warm on cold mornings and hot to the touch when the ríastrad is near. Your allies always know where you stand with the Deep Ríastrad: it glows red while it lasts, and white when you're cooled. When you're cooled from the Deep Ríastrad, you regain Hit Points equal to your Barbarian level. The polite version of the hero-light.

Water-Bearer's Bucket, Common (carried by whoever rides beside her; in the Hunt, Wat Ellery). An oak bucket bound in copper, always cold, with a dipper chained to the handle. It holds 3 gallons and fills itself with cold, clean water at dawn and after each Short Rest. Poured over her as a Utilize action, it cools her from the Deep Ríastrad, which spares her the Exhaustion. The Hunt took it from a border village. The village would like it back.

Her horse. A pale horse of the Hunt (as a Warhorse: Large, AC 11, 19 HP, Speed 60 ft). It has never been shod, and it won't cross running water unless she asks it twice.

Background

An elf of the Bordermarches, born on the side of the border where the year turns the other way. She has ridden with the Hunt since before the parish churches were built, and remembers each raid the way the villages remember a bad winter: by what it took. She doesn't hate the villages. She doesn't think about them much between Beltane and Samhain, and in the dark half she thinks about them the way a fox thinks about a henhouse. The court elves call her kind wild, and mean it as a warning; she calls them tame, and means it as one too. She has a friend in the Hunt, a Mortal Hunter, who carries the bucket, and has twice saved her from what she does when she goes deep.

How to play

  • The ríastrad is safe; going deep is the danger, and it's a choice. Save it for the fight that matters, ideally the last before a rest.
  • Reach is your ground. Fifteen feet of great spear in the ríastrad means you fight from the middle of the lane while the party stands two steps back, where Friend or Foe can't pick them.
  • Someone carries the water: in the Hunt it's Wat Ellery, a Herefordshire boy the Hunt took in old King Henry's time, who runs behind her with the oak bucket. When the last enemy falls, the Deep Ríastrad runs one more turn, and your first swing goes at the nearest creature in reach, whoever it is; tell the party before the fight to stand off. The bucket ends it and spares you the Exhaustion, so being cooled is the good ending. Anyone's Cold damage ends it too, an enemy's Ray of Frost from 60 ft included, which is the Killers' trick.
  • Strike and Vanish after each hit puts you among the next enemies, away from your friends: use it to keep the nearest creature a foe.
  • You know the tide, the open nights, and where the border is thin, and you can't be lost. Out of a fight you are the Hunt's guide as much as its spear.

Why this build

  • Level 10: the Hunt's rank and file, as the subclass says, but a veteran of it: Strike and Vanish is the Hunt's raid (strike, and gone across the border). At 14th the Hunt Rides would make her Large; a table can raise her for that.
  • Elf, wood elf lineage: The Bordermarch elves are wild elves; the wood lineage's speed and Pass without Trace fit raiders. She is a Hidden creature, so the Hidden Killer's iron finds her.
  • Soldier (Str +2, Con +1; Savage Attacker): she is the Hunt's soldiery. Strength to 20 by 8th: +9 to hit.
  • A pike: the Ríastrad's +5 ft reach on a reach weapon gives her 15 ft, which is what makes Friend or Foe playable: the party can stay out of her reach and still be near.
