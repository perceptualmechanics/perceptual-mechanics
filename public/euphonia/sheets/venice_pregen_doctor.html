Dr. Elia Pardo

Physician 3 (Doctor): features through level 3
AC 12 · HP 24 · Speed 30 ft · Spell DC 13

Who You Are

Elia is courteous, tired and incapable of walking past a cough. He trusts soap, Galen about half the time, and nobody's promises.

In Venice. His family has lived in the Ghetto for three generations. He took his degree at Padua and walked the wards of al-Mansuri in Cairo, and came home with a healer's art the Ten never licensed. A doctor is the one man the gates open for after dark. He has watched the Ghetto's golem for years: it lets through anyone who tells it out loud who they are and why they have come, and it stops everyone else.

Your way in. Two days ago he sat with a feverish copyist named Anzolo Furlan on the Lazzaretto until the health office sent him back to the city at dusk; Anzolo died that night, with no one to hear him. Anzolo's mother has asked Elia to bring back her son's last words. Simon Horowitz is his neighbor, and his patient.

Secret. At Anzolo's bedside he healed with magic in front of a quarantine guard. Somebody has since put his name in a lion's mouth for unlicensed magic, and the Ten's men will come at dawn. The purse and the ship off the Lido are his way out. The ship is Spain's. Spain expelled its Jews in 1492 and the law still stands, so on that deck he is safe only for as long as her officers say so. He hasn't found another way out yet.

The crew. Bettina found you the way the sick do: she asked who in Venice still comes out after dark. You've stitched up Kudzai once already this week and haven't asked how he got the burn.

How to play me. You're the doctor on the job. Look the crew over before you set out and they're your patients for the night; link two of them with Vital Stream, and your healing reaches them from across a room or from the street under the roofs. In a fight your hands are never still: a wound closed here, a paralyzed friend brought back there, a word over someone who's just gone down.

Features

Spellcasting (Physician 1). Arcane healer with a spellbook (a casebook). INT is your spellcasting ability. You prepare 6 spells from the book after a Long Rest and can cast its Ritual spells straight from the book.

Physician's Eye (Physician 1). You use INT instead of WIS for Medicine checks, and you have Expertise in Medicine (+7).

Healing Prowess (Physician 1). Bonus Action: touch yourself or another creature and spend a Prowess Die; it regains 1d6 + 3 HP. You have two dice (one at 1st level, four from 5th); all return on a Long Rest, half, rounded down (one), on a Short Rest. Once on each of your turns, however it travels.

Diagnosis (Physician 1). Bonus Action, one creature within 30 ft (you can choose yourself, as willing). Willing (or an ally at 0 HP or Incapacitated): automatic; you learn its HP and maximum, its conditions, any disease, poison or curse, and its weakest save. Otherwise INT (Medicine) vs DC 10 + half its CR: on a success you learn whether it's unharmed, injured, Bloodied or near death, its conditions and afflictions, its Necrotic and Poison resistances, and its weakest save (fail, and you can't try it again until a rest). Everyone you diagnose is your patient until a Long Rest, any number of them: reroll 1s when you heal a patient with Physician magic or features.

Treatment (Physician 2). Utilize action: spend a Healer's Kit use to treat a patient within reach. A patient is anyone you have Diagnosed since your last Long Rest, so Diagnose first. Poisoned ends with no check, unless an effect with a save DC caused it (then check against that DC). Otherwise make an INT (Medicine) check: Blinded or Deafened (DC 12), one disease (13), Paralyzed (15), or one level of Exhaustion (15, once per creature per Long Rest); if a spell or effect with a higher save DC imposed it, use that DC. A disease cured this way can't be caught again. One try at each treatment until the patient rests.

Physician Specialty: Doctor (Physician 3). Your specialty.

Vital Stream (Doctor 3). Bonus Action: link willing creatures within 30 ft into your chain, up to 2 besides you. A link holds while that creature is within 60 ft of you or of anyone else in the chain, so a friend on the roofs is still on it; it ends if the creature dies or strays more than 60 ft from everyone, or if you're Incapacitated for any reason but dropping to 0 HP; dropping to 0 HP, yours or a member's, never ends a link. Links last until a Long Rest. While you have a chain: Range: Healing Prowess reaches anyone in it. Heal or Bolster: give the roll + 3 as Temporary HP instead. Triage: when you use Healing Prowess on the chain, one other member gains 3 Temporary HP. Stand Back: Reaction when a member drops to 0 HP, use Healing Prowess on it (1 per Short Rest). The Title: introduce yourself as a doctor where someone inside is sick or hurt, and you're let in to see the patient before anyone asks for your papers; your certificate that someone is too ill to travel, stand trial or marry is believed by any official without a doctor of his own.

Magic Initiate (Wizard) (Feat (Schooled)). Fire Bolt and Minor Illusion; Shield always prepared, once per Long Rest without a slot. INT.

Skilled (Feat (Human Versatile)). Proficiency in Stealth, Investigation and Alchemist's Supplies.

Resourceful (Human). You gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest.

Equipment

Leather Armor; Light Crossbow and 20 Bolts; Dagger; Healer's Kit (10 uses); Spellbook (his casebook); Arcane Focus (a crystal lens); Scholar's Pack; Calligrapher's Supplies; Book (a Padua anatomy, annotated in three hands); 8 sheets of Parchment; Robe (a physician's black gown); Lamp and Oil; Quarterstaff (walking staff); Herbalism Kit; Potion of Healing (Common magic item: a jar of Venetian theriac; 2d4 + 2); 14 GP.
