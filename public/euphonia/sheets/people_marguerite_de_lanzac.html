Marguerite de Lanzac

Human · Noble · Courtier 5 (Grace) · level 5
PeopleEuropeVersailles and Saint-GermainCollege of GraceLevel 5

In July 1668 the king gave the Grand Divertissement royal at Versailles, still a hunting lodge half-rebuilt, with a play, a supper, a ball and fireworks. A courtier's fortune in these years is a place in a royal household: a salaried post that comes with lodging, access and the right to be seen. The Marquise de Valcroix's salon in the Marais is where the court goes to be clever.

Versailles at dusk, the parterre half dug and the palace half scaffolding. A young woman in a gown that fits almost perfectly crosses the gravel toward the fountain, on her way to nowhere in particular, and the conversations on the terrace go quiet in the order she passes them. At the fountain she turns, and bows to a duke exactly as deeply as she should and not a hair deeper, and asks after his spaniel by name. He tells her about the spaniel for twenty minutes. He has never been happier.

Background

A Quercy gentleman's daughter who came up to court three years ago with four quarterings, her father's debts and her mother's manners, and has lived on invitations ever since. The gown is the Marquise de Valcroix's, and so was the one before it. What Marguerite has is Grace: every room she walks into gets happier, and she can't say why any more than the room can. She can't afford to gamble, so she never loses; she can't afford enemies, so she has none yet. The night she crossed the parterre, three dukes asked who she was and a cardinal decided she was dangerous. She wants a paid place in a princess's household, so she can send money home to Quercy and own a gown.

How to play

  • At a reception she is the whole evening: a minute's Conversation (1 Ease) moves a guest one step friendlier without its noticing, The Read tells her who has the authority and who has the power, and The Hostess spends her Ease on a friend's clumsy toast.
  • Speak a maxim at dawn for the day you expect. "Never let your anger show" for an audience; "Leave them wanting more" for a departure.
  • The Hunt: ministers who would never receive her ride beside her for an hour after a stag, and talk.
  • In a fight she keeps everyone's head. A Word in Time puts her composure on the ally about to be hit.
  • The hook is the gown. Somebody wants to know why the Marquise de Valcroix dresses a girl from Quercy, and somebody else wants to give her a place in a household, for a price.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: three years at court and Extra Attack. Poise Under Fire (6th) is next season.
  • College of Grace: the Courtier chapter's opening vignette, the woman crossing the parterre at Versailles (The Classes, Courtier).
  • Noble for four quarterings and no money; Alert because she is always first to a fainting duchess. Four arts at 5th: a Secret, for the Mysteries, would make her Regard 5.
