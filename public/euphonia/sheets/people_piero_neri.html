Piero Neri

Human · Artisan · Cleric 5 (Life) · level 5
PeopleEuropeFlorenceLife DomainLevel 5

The Misericordia of Florence, founded in the 1240s, is a lay brotherhood that carries the sick to hospital and buries the dead, the poor and strangers first. Its brothers wear black robes and hoods so that no one knows who helped them, and they answer a bell at the brotherhood's house on the cathedral square at any hour.

The bell rings on the cathedral square at two in the morning. Six men in black robes and hoods come out of the brotherhood's house with a stretcher and set off at a trot. You can't see their faces. One of them, broad-shouldered, smelling faintly of raw wool, stops long enough to kneel by you in the street, put his hand on the cut on your arm and close it, and say, "Thanks be to God. Go home." Then he is gone after the others.

Background

A wool-carder of Santo Spirito, across the river, and a captain of the Misericordia, who has carried Florence's sick and dead under a black hood for nineteen years. He works his carding bench by day with the hood folded on the stool beside him, so he can be at the brotherhood's house before the bell finishes. His prayers close wounds, and the brothers say the bell rings louder on the nights he's on watch. Last winter he buried a foreign student with no name and a lute, and he wants the lute taken home to whoever is waiting for it.

How to play

  • He is an ally, and the man to ask who in Florence is sick, poor, dying or newly arrived. The brotherhood sees everything and says nothing, except to him.
  • Under the hood he is anyone. A party in trouble at night may be helped by him and never know it.
  • He is no fighter, but six hooded men with a stretcher at a trot clear a street faster than a patrol.
  • The hook is the lute. Find out whose it was, and take it home.

Why this build

  • Why 5th: nineteen years of the bell, and prayers that close wounds: a working healer, not a prelate.
  • Life Domain: the Misericordia's whole work is the sick and the dead.
  • Artisan and Crafter for a wool-carder who makes the brotherhood's stretchers too; Healer for nineteen years of the bell.
