The 1600s are the century of the long winters, and frost has killed the Franconian vines more than once (The Classes, Sorcerer Origins). The prince-bishops of Würzburg burned hundreds as witches in the late 1620s. Johann Philipp von Schönborn, prince-bishop of Würzburg since 1642 and Elector of Mainz, put an end to the witch trials in his lands. A town clerk who writes to him about a burning stall now gets a commissioner, not a commission.

Gear

A basket of eggs (some of them warm), a wool kirtle and a scorched apron, a hazel switch for the hens that she now uses as her Arcane Focus because it was the only thing that didn't catch, a small knife (as a dagger), her mother's rosary, a purse with eleven kreuzer in it. Languages: German (Franconian), and enough church Latin to follow a Mass. Tools: Brewer's Supplies and Cook's Utensils (Skilled); Navigator's Tools (Merchant), which she has never used and was given by the clerk at the guild office because that is what the form said.

The last egg. From the stall that burned: cooked hard in its shell, the shell blackened. She keeps it in her apron pocket and turns it over when someone is being rude. It has never cracked.

Dr. Sartorius's questions. A copy, in his hand, of every question he has asked her and every answer she gave. He gave it to her so she would know what he was sending to Würzburg. Nobody has ever done that before.

Background

A vintner's daughter of Kitzingen whose family has kept hens since the frost took the vines two summers ago. On a market day this spring a burgher's wife jostled her once too often and the whole stall went up: the straw, the awning, the baskets and the eggs in their shells. Nobody was hurt, and the eggs were cooked through. By noon three people said they had always known, and by evening the town clerk had written to the prince-bishop. Forty years ago that letter would have burned her. What came back down the river was Dr. Kilian Sartorius, a canon lawyer with a bucket, who asks her careful questions about what she was feeling at the time. She wants to sell eggs on a Saturday without anyone crossing the street, and she has learned to count to ten.

How to play

  • Your Release doesn't hit, it catches: Kindle burns down over several turns unless someone spends an action to put it out. A Kindled enemy has a choice between fighting and not burning.
  • Wreathed first. It lights the room, stings whoever closes on her and keeps an Overflow off her friends.
  • Careful Spell is the most important thing she knows. She has never hurt anyone, and she means to keep it that way.
  • The Mark: she can light a hearth with a touch, or snuff every candle in a church from the door. The second one has caused talk.
  • The hook is the prince-bishop's answer. Dr. Sartorius will write it, and he has started asking the party what they think.

Why this build

  • Why 3rd: it came through her this spring. Her origin's whole kit (Kindle, Wreathed, the Mark) and two Metamagic options, and a long way to go.
  • Fire Origin: the Sorcerer chapter's opening vignette, the egg-seller on the Main whose stall goes up (The Classes, Sorcerer Origins).
  • Merchant for a market stall; Lucky because nobody was hurt. Careful and Subtle, because what she wants most is for nobody to notice.
  • Johann Philipp von Schönborn is real, and acts here only as history records: he ended the witch trials in his lands. Dorothea and Dr. Sartorius's commission are this game's.
