Chuqui Ocllo

Human · Artisan · Counsellor 6 (Factor) · level 6
PeopleThe AmericasCuscoFactorLevel 6

Aqha, called chicha in Spanish, is the Andean maize beer brewed by women and poured at every festival and work party. A minka is communal work owed and repaid between families, and ayni is the reciprocity behind it; Situa is the September festival that drives sickness out of Cusco. In Euphonia Tawantinsuyu still stands, at war with Spain on the Apurímac.

A courtyard in Cusco in the afternoon, a great jar of aqha working in the shade. A broad woman with her sleeves tied back stirs it with a paddle and keeps track, out loud, of who owes whom a day's work, back three harvests, while the runners' widows on the bench correct her and are wrong. A Spanish muleteer, a prisoner, sits among them with a cup. Nobody is angry. Nobody is hungry. She hands you a cup and asks what your family can do.

Background

A brewer of Cusco whose house's aqha is poured at Situa and at half the minkas in the valley, and whose courtyard is where the runners' widows sit in the afternoons. She keeps track of who owes whom a day's work, back three harvests, and is never wrong. She has fed the Sapa Inca's officers, a captured Spanish muleteer and a huaca's keeper at the same bench without anyone leaving hungry or angry. She wants her son home from the Apurímac front in time to dig his own field.

How to play

  • She is a host and a contact who knows everyone in Cusco, and what they owe.
  • Her courtyard is neutral ground. Officers, prisoners and priests drink there together.
  • Ask her for help, and you will owe a day's work. She will remember.
  • The hook is her son on the Apurímac front. Bring him home for the planting.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: the valley's ledger of days in one woman's fingers.
  • Factor: ayni is credit, and her courtyard is a bank of work.
  • Artisan and Crafter for the brewing house; Tough for the jars.
