Background

A scholar of the science of letters at the court of Aceh's queen, Sultanah Safiatuddin. The queen has kept Aceh free and kept scholars around her, and Fatimah is one reason the Dutch knock before entering. She writes the talismans on the port's walls. Her science of letters shares an ancestor with the Children of the Emerald Tablet, and she would like them to admit theirs is the younger. She pays for books in pepper, generously. She wants a copy of a book of letters she has only seen quoted, said to be in a Bohra library in Surat.

How to play

  • She is a Monsoon Road patron who pays in pepper, generously, for books.
  • In a fight she is the reason the Dutch knock: Sculpt Spells means she can drop a Fireball on a boarding party with the defenders standing in it.
  • She would like the Children of the Emerald Tablet to admit theirs is the younger science. A party with a European wizard in it will hear about this.
  • The hook is the book in Surat. Whoever brings it gets the pepper; whoever reads it first gets her attention.

Why this build

  • Why 10th: a scholar at the queen's side, with Empowered Evocation: the letters on the wall that burn.
  • Evoker, as the roster has her: letters that smoke as they dry.
  • Sage and Skilled for a scholar of the queen's hall.
