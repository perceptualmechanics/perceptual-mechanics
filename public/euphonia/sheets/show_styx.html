Background

Alvise Trevisan, called Bisato, has rowed smugglers, lovers, and the Council of Ten's own men across the lagoon at night, and knows where the acqua alta will be before the moon does. He walked the Wheel in a fever as a boy and came back knowing he would carry anyone who paid the fare, and nothing about the fare itself. He will get a crew from the ghetto to a ship off the Lido in a fog, in the dark, at the tide's worst, and charge double for the fog. He has never once asked a passenger what was in the bag. What he wants is to be paid in advance, once, by someone who meant it. He works for himself and the boatmen's confraternity, and he knows Zanetta Bon, Vimbai Mapfumo and Domenego Cargnelutti.

How to play

  • Your action is a Planar Strike (or a spell); it deals your open vista's plane's damage. Your Bonus Action opens a vista (a Favor from your pool; two for an Influence that heals; never the same plane two turns running) beside a creature within 60 ft, or commands your spirit. The spirit doesn't fight: it acts on your turn, moving and Helping, and commanded it also uses its plane's Aspect.
  • You are the crew's way across the water. Two spirits at once: one scouts ahead (the Passenger remembers every road it has traveled), the other ferries the slowest passenger out of reach.
  • In the fog, Fog Cloud and Pass without Trace hide the boat and Silence hides the oar. Ethereal Stride takes four passengers through the Border Ethereal with you (Many Travelers).
  • In a fight, Forgetting breaks the Concentration of whoever is holding the crew, and Blood in the Pit turns your Hit Point Dice into damage: spend them when the boat has to get away, not before.
  • Alert and Tavern Brawler are a boatman's feats: first to move, and a shove that puts a man in the canal.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: the roster's level: two spirits (The Fare), the Astral, and Guide's Hand to take a crew through the Veil. Asphodel and the Unpaid, the shades who couldn't pay the fare, are 7th, a level ahead.
  • Sailor for Wisdom and Dexterity and a boatman's skills; Tavern Brawler makes the oar a weapon. Alert, because he sees the Ten's boat before it sees him.
  • The Deep Ethereal at 5th, for the Mist-Walker's Ferry and Through the Curtain. The obol is the River of Death's keepsake, always cold and never the fare: one more Favor.
