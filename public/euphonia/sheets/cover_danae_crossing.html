Background

Zanetta Bon is a Venetian halfling who went away and came home able to warp spacetime, with a piece of the Hidden in her: there is always another way in, and she can feel where it is. She made her living as a carnival masker and a forger of passes before the Hidden found her, and now she is the crew's way in on the Venice Job, and its way out. Ser Marin Zorzi, one of the Ten's State Inquisitors, has a file on her and no face to put on it.

How to play

  • Out of a fight you are the way in: Back Ways knows every route a day in the city can teach, and every door, drain, and servants' stair after an hour in the palazzo, and where its walls are thin enough for a Door. Keyhole opens a Door through 5 ft of wall you can't see past. Iron and salt at a house's doors and windows don't stop a Door you open in its wall (A Door of Your Own).
  • On the roofs, Back Ways makes every wall a stair: you climb at your full Speed, and your long jumps carry 10 ft farther (a running long jump: 18 ft, her Strength 8 plus 10). Doors and falling are your fastest road: step off the edge into a Door, out of its twin across the canal still moving, and latch it shut behind you. Bring the crew: up to three hold on and go through with you. The Ten's men can follow only by jumping in after you: Latch shuts the pair behind you, and if you leave it open for the crew, Where No One Follows keeps your side shut to everyone but the ones who came with you.
  • In a fight you are the crew's position: the bravo behind the archer, the wounded out the back wall, everyone off the bridge. Put a Spring warp where the enemy has to stand and cast with your action. Six warps a Short Rest; any warp still in place can be unraveled for Denial.
  • Seams on a casting turn: Hide in your own warp, then leave by another and stay hidden.
  • Feather Fall is always prepared, for a reason.
  • Small and nimble: Halfling Nimbleness takes you through a pursuer's space on a narrow ridge, and Naturally Stealthy lets you Hide behind one of the Ten's men. Brave means their shouting doesn't stop you.

Why this build

  • Why 7th: a rising figure, the crew's burglar on the Venice Job, the setting's intro heist. Seams and Where No One Follows (6th) make her the rooftop runner on the cover: through the wall, the crew behind her, and no one following; Misplace and Liminality (10th) are a career ahead.
  • The light half: Spring's push and teleport keeps her moving and her pursuers off the roof. Charlatan for Charisma and the forged passes, and Skilled for the acrobat's skills.
  • Halfling, after the book's own example of a Danae: a halfling who comes home able to warp spacetime. Halfling Nimbleness and Naturally Stealthy are a rooftop runner's traits.

Features

Danae (Crossing)
1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't enter a place warded with them by any means, teleportation included, unless a creature inside that isn't a Hidden creature asks you in; a companion who is already inside can (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. Within 30 ft: see thresholds between worlds, and know when a creature or object you can see is under an illusion or a shape-changing effect, is Fey, or is a Hidden creature. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 16). 6 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save. Success: it moves to the nearest unoccupied space outside, unaffected. Failure: it stays, and the warp (and its season) affect it at the start of its next turn if it is still inside. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +3 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the light half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): spring (Str save or moved 15 ft horizontally; a willing creature can teleport 15 ft instead) and summer (2d6 Fire, no save; you choose who is spared). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Long Rest: unravel one or more of your warps, in place or unused (a subclass's warps count) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 37 with every warp, 12 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Doors · A Door of Your Own · Latch · Fellow Travellers. Warps can be Doors. Bonus Action: open a linked pair on solid surfaces you can see within 60 ft (a wall, a floor, a ceiling, the side of a cart; 5-ft squares, 10-ft from 5th, and only a creature no wider fits); the pair is one warp. A creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the other, facing away from its surface; a Door sends on no one put into it any other way. A Door's space is the space in front of it. A Door can open in the floor under a willing creature, which drops through. A Door of Your Own: iron and salt on a house's doors, windows, and chimney don't stop you, or anyone who comes through with you, passing through a Door you open in its wall, floor, or roof: a Door is a new opening that nobody has warded. Your Gates and other teleportation get no such exception until Liminality (level 10). Inertia: a creature that falls into a Door comes out of the other still moving, up to the distance it fell, then takes one fall's damage for the whole distance (Prone if it takes any). Keyhole: one Door can open on an unseen surface within 30 ft, beyond up to 5 ft of wall, door, floor, or ceiling; you know whether the space beyond is open. Latch: once on each of your turns, passing through a pair of your Doors no more than 30 ft apart, close them behind you and regain the warp (Doors left open for friends still cost it). Fellow Travellers: when you go through one of your Doors, up to 3 willing creatures within 5 ft of you go through with you, arriving within 5 ft of the far Door (each its one trip through your warps that turn).
3Back Ways. Stealth proficiency (or another skill, if you have Stealth). A Climb Speed equal to your Speed (30 ft), and your long jumps carry you 10 ft farther. A day in a settlement: a route between any two places in it that avoids its main streets, watch posts, and toll gates, if one exists. An hour in a building: every way in or out a person could use (doors, windows, chimneys, drains, the servants' stair), even the ones its owner has forgotten, and where its walls are thin enough for a Door.
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
6Seams. Every warp that fills a space is a Gate for you (once per turn, as for all travel through your warps); in your own warps you can Hide as a Bonus Action on a turn you haven't made an attack roll, without cover and even while an enemy sees you; it ends when you leave the space of your warps, unless by traveling through one.
6Where No One Follows. When you travel through one of your warps, you don't provoke Opportunity Attacks for the rest of that turn, and you can close the way behind you: until the start of your next turn, no one but you and those who came through with you can enter the warp you came out of.
7Reading the Season. Bonus Action: up to 4 creatures you can see within 30 ft are read for 1 hour; the DM tells you each one's temper (one for each group of like creatures) and what lies under it. Advantage on Insight against them, and on Cha checks that play to their temper.
7Open Book. Detect Thoughts PB times per Long Rest without a slot; a creature you've read has Disadvantage on the save.
