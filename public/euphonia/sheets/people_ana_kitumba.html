Palmares is the federation of free settlements in the hills behind Pernambuco, built by people who escaped slavery, and in 1668 it has stood for decades against Portuguese and Dutch expeditions. In Palmares, the paladin's oath is to the people who got free.

The palisade at Macaco at dusk. A captain of thirty-three walks the wall with a spear and a Portuguese musket, greeting every sentry by name and asking after their families. A boy on the wall asks whether the expedition will come this year. She tells him the truth, and then she tells him what she will do about it, and he believes her, because she has never once failed.

Background

A captain of Palmares. Her mother came up from a sugar mill in Pernambuco with a baby and nothing else, and Ana was born free behind the palisade. She has fought off four expeditions and led the people who escaped the fifth's burning of a mocambo back to safety. Her oath is to the people who got free and to the ones still coming; it is the plainest in the world, and it has never once failed her. She wants to see a child of hers grow old in Palmares without ever having to fight for it.

How to play

  • She is the steadiest person in Palmares. Her auras make the wall hold.
  • As an ally she fights beside the party and asks only that they keep faith with the people behind the palisade.
  • Revivify and Lay on Hands: she brings people back from the wall.
  • Brás Furtado knows her name. When he comes, she will be waiting.

Why this build

  • Why 9th: four expeditions fought off, and Abjure Foes: the soldiers who see her on the wall lose their nerve.
  • Oath of Devotion, as the roster has her: the plainest oath, to the people who got free.
  • Soldier and Savage Attacker for a captain who has been fighting since she could hold a spear.
