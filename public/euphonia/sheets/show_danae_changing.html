Background

Hu Lanxin is a fox spirit in a woman's shape who remembers what she is. She has been seducer, wife, trickster and model of virtue, sometimes in one week. She is honest about everything except her age, and fate bends a little around her when she wants it to. A Ming prince who once loved her was buried with her fox-jade, and she wants it back, because it's hers, and she wants to know whether he was buried holding it. Fifty years ago she was in a Suzhou troupe with Su Wanniang, who still knows her; Fan Sanniang and Ok Rye-hwa know her too. On the Tomb Road she is always inside before the party is.

How to play

  • One Reaction, two uses: a lot or Flow of Fate. A readied lot lasts a minute, so palm one on the first turn of a fight, not the night before. A failure is a chance to call fair; a success, to call foul.
  • Call foul on the save that matters most: a failed save against your Hypnotic Pattern pays you back a warp.
  • Spring warps are a trickster's shove, and a free teleport for a friend who steps in.
  • Out of a fight, Small Mercies finds the carter going your way or the clerk who knows the pass, once a Long Rest, and tells you when the house is cheating; Road Luck keeps the company's axles whole.
  • You carry a piece of the Hidden: you are a Hidden creature. A room or building warded with iron and salt keeps you out unless someone inside who isn't a Hidden creature invites you, or you make a DC 13 Charisma save (at Disadvantage while the Hidden are at the ebb; fail, and no warded opening lets you in that night). An invitation can hold as long as you lodge there. A Danae's Sight or a Hidden Killer's craft knows you for what you are.
  • Fey Ancestry: she has Advantage against being Charmed. She has been the charmer for longer than the prince's dynasty lasted.

Why this build

  • Why 9th: the roster's level: Losing Streak and Road Luck (6th), Thresholds and Loop (9th). Double or Nothing and Lucky Charm are 10th.
  • Elf (wood elf) for a fox: Speed 35, Pass without Trace, Druidcraft. Entertainer and Musician for the Suzhou troupe she was in fifty years ago with Su Wanniang. The light half, for spring's shove and summer's fox-fire.
  • Her lot is the madiao pack; her Token of Method is Changing's die with a seventh face. Polymorph is how she wears her own shape when she wants to.

1Hidden Creature. You carry a piece of the Hidden. Iron and salt bind you: you can't pass inward into a room or building warded with them unless someone inside who isn't a Hidden creature invites you, or you succeed on a Charisma save (DC 13, at Disadvantage while the Hidden are at the ebb, but not on an open night); an invitation can hold for as long as you lodge there (The Setting, Iron and Salt).
1The Sight. See thresholds within 30 ft; know illusions, shape-changers, Fey creatures, and Hidden creatures. Insight and Perception; Sylvan.
1Warps. Bonus Action: warp a 5-ft Cube you can see within 60 ft for 1 minute (no Concentration; warps don't choose sides and can't overlap; save DC 17). 6 warps per Short Rest; one kept through a rest stays spent until it ends. Stretch: each foot inside costs 3. Gate: Gates link in a chain, last back to first; a creature that moves into one of its own will steps out of the next (a Gate sends on no one pushed in or caught in a Gate made on it); your first Gate comes as a linked pair for one warp. On a creature: once on each of your turns, make a warp where creatures stand; each makes a Dex save or enters the warp (and its season) at the start of its next turn. A creature can travel through your Gates (and Doors and Seams) only once per turn. Warps are magical (Dispel Magic ends one as a level 1 spell).
1Denial. Reaction when a creature you can see hits you (no Medium or Heavy armor, no Shield): unravel a warp for +4 AC against that attack, possibly turning it into a miss (+3, +4 from 9th, +5 from 17th).
2The Half. You chose the light half of the year (yours, not the calendar's): spring (Str save or moved 15 ft horizontally; a willing creature can teleport 15 ft instead) and summer (2d6 Fire, no save; you choose who is spared). A warp can carry one season you know; a creature that first enters it on a turn, or starts its turn there, is subject to it (one warp's season per creature per turn).
2Flee. Reaction when damage leaves you Bloodied (not at 0 HP), once per Short or Long Rest: unravel any number of your warps (lots and knots included) and go Away for 1 minute, with Temporary HP that replace any you have (your Danae level + 5 per warp: 39 with every warp, 14 with one). Away ends when they're gone, when you're Incapacitated, or when you end it. While Away: attacks against you have Disadvantage; once on each of your turns, teleport 15 ft (Flicker); you keep casting and Concentrating. Readjust: when it ends, at the end of that turn you can teleport 15 ft.
3Lots. Warps can be lots. Bonus Action: ready one (it lasts 1 minute, or until cast). Reaction, when a creature you can see within 60 ft fails or succeeds on a D20 Test: cast it and call fair on a failure (roll again, take the higher) or foul on a success (take the lower).
3Flow of Fate. When a creature you can see within 30 ft is hit by an attack roll, you can use Denial for it instead of for yourself, unraveling one of your readied lots; it gains the bonus to AC against that attack. The armor restriction is yours, not theirs.
3Small Mercies. Outside combat, when you or a companion goes looking for something ordinary (a dropped key, a cheap item a market might stock, a carter going your way, someone who knows the pass), the DM tells you where it turns up, if it can be found nearby; once per Long Rest. Watching one round of a game, lottery, draw, or wager, you know whether it's rigged (not how).
5Fae Weaving. On a Long Rest, weave up to PB nonmagical objects your tools could make (Medium or smaller, not consumables or ammunition, up to 10 GP × Danae level). Fairy gold; they come undone at your next Long Rest.
5The First Cycles. Warps can be 10-ft Cubes. Tilt: you choose which way is down; a creature that first enters on a turn or starts there makes a Str save or is thrown 10 ft that way and Prone; a willing creature can walk it, up a wall or across a ceiling. Pocket: bigger inside; up to eight Medium creatures (a Large one counts as four; no Huge); Total Cover both ways; 5 ft of movement to step out. The Way Back: Flee comes back on a Short or Long Rest.
6Losing Streak. Calling foul on a creature that then fails or misses regains one warp, once per Short or Long Rest.
6Road Luck. Traveling overland or by water with companions, the road's small mishaps (a thrown shoe, a cracked axle, spoiled flour, a missed tide) don't befall your company unless the DM wants one for the story. Once per Long Rest, at an uncertain way, ask which choice the year favors: "this one," "that one," or "neither."
7Reading the Season. Bonus Action: up to 5 creatures you can see within 30 ft are read for 1 hour; the DM tells you each one's temper (one for each group of like creatures) and what lies under it. Advantage on Insight against them, and on Cha checks that play to their temper.
7Open Book. Detect Thoughts PB times per Long Rest without a slot; a creature you've read has Disadvantage on the save.
9The Door Ajar. While Away, each Flicker can leave a free warp (any shape, a season you know) in the space you left.
9Thresholds. Spend 1 minute at a threshold you can see with The Sight to open it: Plane Shift without a slot or Material component, for you and up to eight willing creatures, only to or from the Hidden, arriving at the threshold's other side. Once per Long Rest.
9Loop. New shape: a creature trying to leave by any means makes a Wis save or steps back in from the opposite side, losing the movement (or the slot) it spent, and can't try that Loop again until its next turn; one that succeeds isn't affected by that Loop again.
