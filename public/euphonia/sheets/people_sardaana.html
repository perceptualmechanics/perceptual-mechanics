Ysyakh is the Sakha summer festival, held at midsummer on the meadows: kumis poured to the sky and the spirits, horse races, wrestling, and the osuokhai, the great circle dance led by a singer who improvises verse while the circle answers. The Russian fort at Yakutsk, founded in 1632, collects the fur tribute, the yasak.

Midsummer on the meadow above the Lena, and the sun won't set. A young woman in a beaded headdress with a horsehair whisk in one hand has pulled a Cossack's Sakha wife, a Russian clerk and you into the circle by the elbow, and is singing a verse about each of you. The circle answers. She makes a verse about the clerk's boots. The whole meadow laughs, and the clerk, to his own surprise, keeps dancing.

Her grandmother's cup. A carved wooden choron, the three-legged cup for kumis at Ysyakh. The first pour goes to the sky. Whatever the sky does with it, the meadow greens.

The tally stick. Her family's tribute, notched in pelts. The clerk's count and hers don't match. She has kept hers.

Background

A singer of the Lena meadows who leads the osuokhai at Ysyakh, and once kept a circle dancing from dusk to the next noon. She makes every verse new and never sings the same one twice; old men say her verses please the spirits of the meadow, and the meadow seems to agree. She pays her family's fur tribute at the fort and resents every pelt. She wants this year's circle to be the largest anyone remembers, so large the fort can hear it, and she means the fort to hear it.

How to play

  • She is a host and a contact on the Lena: everyone comes to Ysyakh, and she has made a verse about all of them.
  • Pulling strangers into the circle is how she finds out who they are. Dance, and she will tell you what the meadow knows.
  • She is no fighter, but nobody on the meadow lets anyone hurt her. A singer who pleases the spirits is everybody's.
  • The hook is the fort and the tribute. She wants it to hear the circle; the party can help it listen.

Why this build

  • Why 6th: a singer whose verses please the meadow's spirits, with a druid's reach and a festival's worth of friends.
  • Circle of the Land with the Polar land: the Lena's long winter and its one bright summer.
  • Entertainer and Musician for the osuokhai; Healer for the wrestlers.
