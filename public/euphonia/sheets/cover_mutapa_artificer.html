Simbarashe Moyo

Human · Artisan · Mhizha 20 (Constructor) · level 20
On the coverMutapa, 1668The great artificer

The grandfather of Mutapan artifice, alive in 1668: tired, wary of Europe, and unhappy with how the court stewards his work. He first showed that lightning is electricity, that electricity is its own force, and that lightning is magic's purest force; then he began putting electricity into things that aren't magic. He fought selling devices abroad and training foreign artificers, and lost. His test is coming: loyalty to the nation he helped found, or to the science he helped discover?

The merchant house's men come to the workshop at night to take back what the court says is the court's. The old man doesn't get up. He lifts the old arc gun off the bench, the one that started as a lightning rod, and the construct in the doorway stops being furniture. The first man through gets the strike, and the second gets what it jumps to.

Background

Simbarashe Moyo was a young man on the plateau when the king kept the mines, and an old one by the time anyone abroad noticed what came of it. The druid read the plants; he worked out what the stone could carry, then what the sky was carrying, and called the strike down a rod to see. The workshops, the calibration, the guild that makes a Mhizha out of anyone with the hands for it: that was one of his first apprentices, and he has never entirely forgiven the guild for it. He speaks every language the coast trades in and trusts none of the people who speak them to him. He told the king the shewstones were the most dangerous thing Mutapa had ever sold. The king sold them anyway. Now there is a Mhizha in London with an English apprentice, the Spanish are fishing platina out of their rivers, and every court in Europe wants to know how many more of him there are. There aren't any more of him. That's the problem.

How to play

  • You don't move much. The construct moves for you: it Strikes every turn you fire the gun, which leaves your Bonus Action for a Preparation, and Haste makes it two of you.
  • Every turn you fire the gun. At 20th its lightning ignores Resistance and chains; a slot on a hit is the Overcharge.
  • How It Works and Boon of Skill: there is nothing in the room you can't name, open, or fix. Out of a fight you are the oldest expert alive on almost anything electrical, and everyone knows it.
  • Speaking Wires are your answer to the shewstones: a channel that reaches a mile and no further, and that no foreign court can buy.
  • Your test is a story, not a feature: nation or science. Whatever you decide, someone will try to stop you.

Why this build

  • Level 20: the grandfather of the whole art; Lightning Itself (his own discovery as a class feature) and the Workshop Coat the book gives him.
  • Constructor: his discovery was putting current into things that aren't magic, and the construct, woken by current, is where it has gone furthest; it's also the one thing in the workshop that isn't the guild's. A weary old man with an iron companion that waits at the door. The Chemist (Mutapa eats because of its chemists) and the Gunner (the big work he wouldn't sell) are the other fits.
  • Artisan (Int +2, Dex +1; Crafter): he came out of the forge, not the study. Skilled, Observant and Boon of Skill make the polymath; War Caster keeps his Concentration.
  • Intelligence 20 by 8th; the Tuned Arc Gun +3 puts his gun and DC at +14 and 22.
