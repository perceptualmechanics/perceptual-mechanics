Agata Mangano

Human · Charlatan · Sorcerer 7 (Earth) · level 7
ShowcaseLevel 7Earth OriginSicily, 1668Catania under Etna

Sicily is a Spanish viceroyalty, and Catania is a town of black lava stone under Etna, which every Catanese calls simply "the mountain." Etna will open its flank next March, bury the villages on its southern slope, and send a river of fire to Catania's walls; the Earth sorcerers of the island already feel it coming. The city's defense against the mountain is Sant'Agata's veil, carried in procession toward the lava. This one has been selling the mountain's moods at fairs for years, and nobody believes a word she says.

At the fair under the Benedictines' wall she is reading a farmer's palm for two grani when the cup of wine on her table shivers. Nobody else notices. She stops talking in the middle of his future, puts both hands flat on the lava flagstones, and says, quite quietly, "Not this year. Next spring. North of Nicolosi." Then she remembers who she is supposed to be, and charges him double.

Gear

A carved walking stick of Etna chestnut as her Arcane Focus, and a stiletto (as a dagger) in her stocking; a painted cart with a fortune-teller's awning; a costume of a Moorish sibyl, turban and all, for the fairs; a Forgery Kit and a stack of printed saints' cards, some of them real; a bag of lava pebbles she sells as charms; a ledger of every tremor for twenty years, in her own shorthand, which is the most accurate record of the mountain in Sicily; a scrap of red cloth she swears was touched to Sant'Agata's veil. She sells copies by the dozen. This one she has never sold. Languages: Sicilian (in place of Common), Spanish, and enough church Latin to sound like a priest. Tools: Forgery Kit (Charlatan).

Seam Stone (Origin Stone, Earth; Wondrous Item, Common). Heavy for its size, black, warm on the side toward the mountain. She always knows which way is north and which way is down, and never loses her footing on loose stone or a ship's deck. Her father found it in the block that didn't fall.

Background

Born in the lanes below the Benedictines of San Nicolò, to a mother who told fortunes and a father who cut lava stone until a block stopped an inch above his head and everyone looked at the little girl standing beside it. Her mother saw a living in it at once. For twenty years she has sold earthquakes: the next tremor, the safe day to start a house, which saint to pray to when the cups rattle. Most of it was patter. Some of it wasn't, and the mountain has lately stopped letting her tell the difference. Since the summer it has been talking all the time, and what it says is March, and the southern flank, and the walls. She has told the Senate, the bishop and the Benedictines, and each of them looked at her painted cart and laughed. She is going to have to prove it before it proves itself.

How to play

  • Your Pressure is a clock. Hold it to 3, then Release on the creature that matters: three dice, and Bury takes 30 ft of its Speed and puts it on the ground.
  • Choose molten for anything stone shrugs off, and stone for anything fire doesn't bother; Bury holds either way. (From 14th, a molten Upheaval sets the heaved ground burning.)
  • Bedrock before anything else in a fight. While you're hardened your Overflow can't hurt your friends, so a missed Release at 3 is no disaster.
  • Tremorsense 30 ft means nobody sneaks up on you on the ground, and Ore-Sense is worth more to a mining company than any spell. Keep that quiet.
  • You are a charlatan who happens to be right. Your patter, your saints' cards and your painted cart get you into every square in Sicily; the trick is getting anyone to believe the true part.

Why this build

  • Earth Origin: the text puts Etna's eruption of next March in the origin's own introduction, and Sicily's Earth sorcerers feeling it come. She is the one who does.
  • Level 7: Tremorsense and Ore-Sense from 6th, and Stone Shape and Stoneskin at 7th for the walls she will be asked to shore up in March.
  • Charlatan (Cha +2, Con +1; Skilled): a fortune-teller who sells earthquakes, so that the one true prophecy in Catania comes from the least believable mouth. Tough (human Versatile) for a sorcerer who means to stand on the lava.
  • New character: no roster row has an Earth sorcerer, so she is invented: Agata Mangano, after the saint whose veil Catania carries toward the lava.
