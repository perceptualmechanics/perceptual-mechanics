Background

The family's majordomo: the Tom Hagen of a century early. Licensed magic made the biggest black market in the world, and magic can draw anything out of anyone who knows it, so the families invented a position: one person who runs the family's affairs and decides who knows what. He keeps the books, knows the law, hires the crews, talks to the other families, and says the thing the boss doesn't want to hear. Gennaro Esposito is majordomo to a Neapolitan family. Esposito is the surname Naples gives its foundlings: he wasn't born into connections, so he made himself the one the family can't do without. The chancery wanted a family name; the underworld only wanted someone who could count.

How to play

  • Don't plan out loud: get into trouble, then reveal you'd already greased the guard (About That, 5 a day). The DM judges whether you could have.
  • Before the job, Need to Know: brief each crew member on exactly their part, so anything magic pulls out of a captured runner is only what you told him, and you know the moment it happens.
  • The Hum tells you who did it, where it went, and who's paying. Crew (4 hands, one check for the whole job at d20 + 6) watches doors and lies for you; once a tenday you can set two gangs on each other.

Why this build

  • Why 10th: the family's established right hand. The Hum and Need to Know (10th) are the job: the family's secrets kept from magic. The Sit-Down (14th), where every underworld receives him as an equal, is ahead.
  • Scribe for a clerk's start (Intelligence and Wisdom, Skilled); Alert because a majordomo who loses initiative has already lost the argument.
