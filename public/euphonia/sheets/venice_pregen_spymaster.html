Bettina Scarpa

Counsellor 3 (Spymaster): features through level 3
AC 15 · HP 17 · Speed 30 ft

Who You Are

Bettina listens like a ledger: everything goes in, and nothing comes out unaccounted for. She is warm the way a good hostess is warm, which is to say she has already decided where you'll sit.

In Venice. Born into the chancery families who staff the Republic's offices, she copied the Ten's ciphers until she could tell a lie by its spacing, then spent ten years as a factor for the Dutch company at Surat. She is home, she says, on leave. She knows the Palace's servants' stair, the corridor behind the public halls, the cipher clerks' room and the Room of the Three Heads as far as its doorway. She was never admitted to the Secret Chancery, and she has never seen the press. Marco Padavin was a junior secretary in her day, and he would know her face without a mask.

Your way in. She put this crew together. When Don Íñigo's people asked around for talent, the name they kept hearing was hers, and she gave them yours.

Secret. The House of the Bird (Mutapa's bank, which pays Mutapa's spymaster) keeps a quiet factor in Venice, and through him Mutapa's spymaster, Shiri, pays Bettina too. Shiri would pay double to see the report burned rather than handed to Spain. Bettina hasn't decided yet, and nobody in the crew knows.

The crew. You recruited the others. You know Tilde ran messages for the Ten, and you suspect Kudzai answers to Mutapa as well. You knew his family's house at Surat by sight, from your years with the Dutch company there; he was a boy then.

How to play me. You see the fight before anyone else does. Observe the one that matters and say out loud what you learned, so the crew hears it; when a friend lands a blow on it, Exploit drives it home. You fight close, because you learn more within 10 feet, and when a blade finds you, Sidestep makes it half a cut and a step out of reach.

Features

Calculation (Counsellor 1). Defense: with no armor and no Shield, your AC is 10 + DEX + INT. Strike: with a Simple weapon, or a Martial weapon that has Finesse or Light, you can use INT for attack and damage.

Encyclopedic (Counsellor 1). Proficiency in two lore skills: History and Nature.

Observe (Counsellor 1). Bonus Action: a creature you can see within 60 ft becomes Observed for 1 minute, and you learn one fact: its highest ability, its weakest save, a Resistance/Immunity/Vulnerability, whether it's Bloodied, whether it's hurt, sick, exhausted, drunk or poisoned, its trade or station, or whether it's armed or carrying something concealed. Within 10 ft, you learn one more. Observing it again gives another fact and restarts the minute. Never what it thinks, feels or intends.

Exploit (Counsellor 1). When a creature you can see hits an Observed creature with an attack roll, you can add 2d4 damage of the attack's type (no action; decide after the hit, before damage; not doubled on a Critical Hit). Not before your first turn in a combat; after that, once until the start of your next turn.

Sidestep (Counsellor 1). Reaction when a creature you can see hits you: halve the damage, then move up to 10 ft without provoking Opportunity Attacks. Sidestep and Anticipate share 3 uses per Short Rest.

Direct (Counsellor 2). Once per turn when you Observe a creature on your turn, choose an ally within 60 ft who can see or hear you. On its next attack roll or ability check against an Observed creature before the start of your next turn (to hit it, talk it round, read it or fool it), it can add your d4 computation die after seeing the d20.

Anticipate (Counsellor 2). Reaction when an Observed creature you can see hits you or a creature within 30 ft of you: roll your d4 and subtract it from the attack roll, possibly turning the hit into a miss. Shares Sidestep's uses. When the hit is on you, choose: Sidestep for the sure half and a step out of reach, or Anticipate to try to erase it.

Deduction (Counsellor 2). Spend 10 minutes thinking, then ask the DM one question about something you've Observed, seen or examined; you get the most likely truth in a sentence or two: it moves the case forward without closing it. Feed it a lie and you'll be confidently wrong. If you haven't seen enough to answer, the use isn't spent. 3 per Long Rest.

Mental Index (Counsellor 2). Expertise in Investigation and Persuasion.

Counsellor Discipline: Spymaster (Counsellor 3). Your subclass.

Network (Spymaster 3). In any settlement, declare that you already have an asset inside one place (in Venice: a laundress in the Doge's Palace). Spend a favor (3 per Long Rest) to ask the asset one question about where it works; the DM answers as the asset would, which is usually, but not always, true. It won't fight or risk its life for you. By default it stays yours until you use it in a way that would expose it (a question only it could answer, acted on in front of the people it works among); the DM tells you when an asset is burned, and after a Long Rest you can declare a new one.

Human Behavior (Spymaster 3). When you Observe a creature that can speak, you can learn instead: whom it answers to, what it values most, whether it's hiding something right now, what it fears losing, or its price for a day's cooperation. You read all of it from how it lives; its thoughts stay its own.

Forewarned (Spymaster 3). Reaction when an Observed creature you can see forces a saving throw: shout the warning, and allies within 30 ft who hear you have Advantage on that save. 2 uses per Long Rest.

Alert (Feat (Human Versatile)). Add your Proficiency Bonus to Initiative; after rolling, you may swap Initiative with a willing ally.

Skilled (Feat (Company Factor)). Proficiency in Sleight of Hand, Stealth and the Disguise Kit.

Resourceful (Human). You gain Heroic Inspiration whenever you finish a Long Rest.

Equipment

Sword cane (as Rapier; concealable); Stonebow (as Hand Crossbow) and 20 sling bullets; Forgery Kit; Fine Clothes (with a black bauta mask for the Sensa); Lead-Lined Writing Box (Common magic item: papers inside can't be seen, read or detected by magic; lock DC 18; false bottom DC 20 Investigation); 4 GP, 9 SP, 6 CP.
